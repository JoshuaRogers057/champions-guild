# The Forgebound Legion

**Leader:** Forge-Marshal Korrum Vane — a heavy-framed Steelborn with soot-black armor, brass-reinforced joints, and old chain links welded across his chest like medals. Patient, severe, and almost impossible to provoke.

**Specialties:** Siege labor, line warfare, fortress assault, defensive holds, and engineering support.

---

The Forgebound Legion is industrial warfare given a contract seal. Their soldiers advance like moving foundries, protected by heavy armor, chained hammers, siege tools, and relentless discipline.

They are hired for the kind of work that breaks ordinary companies: long sieges, fortified roads, ruined battlefields, collapsing mines, and brutal reclamation campaigns. Their chain-wrapped hammer is more than a symbol. It is a warning that once the Legion binds itself to a task, it will keep striking until the obstacle gives way.
