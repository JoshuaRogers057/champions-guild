# The Frostfang Freehost

**Leader:** Jorik Icevein — a massive goliath veteran draped in wolf pelts with a shattered tusk necklace and a reputation for collecting unpaid debts personally.

**Specialties:** Shock raids, caravan interception, mountain warfare, bodyguard contracts, intimidation, and brutal winter campaigns.

---

The Frostfang Freehost is Thundrakar's most feared martial guild, formed from clanless raiders, exiles, oathbreakers, and warriors who chose coin over tribe. Cast out or self-made, they survive by selling strength wherever war, fear, or hardship creates demand.

Their ranks include axe berserkers, javelin hunters, frost scouts, sled cavalry, and hardened veterans who believe payment is sacred and mercy optional. They are known across Eryndor for one rule above all others: they always get paid.
