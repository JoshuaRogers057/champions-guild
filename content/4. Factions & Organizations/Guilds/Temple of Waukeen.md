# Temple of Waukeen

Wealthy nobles who worship at the Temple of Waukeen hold many of the loans being enforced against Ashbarrows' small businesses. Factor Elress Wain of the temple returned to a feast with armed guards and threats, and Father Alric, a priest of Waukeen, was escorted to a conference in [[Contramara]]. The temple paid for the return of the sewer creature's corpse and later ordered it removed.

A temple of Waukeen in Gilder's Rise was devastated by a bomb, with the Guild member Warjo presumed lost.
