# Temple of Yondalla

Leaders of the Temple of Yondalla were invited to a public feast, where a cleric of Yondalla assaulted Elress Wain and was taken into custody. A radical follower of Yondalla sabotaged an escort to stop Father Alric, and radical followers burned noble caravans in protest of Brunna Mossvale's execution.
