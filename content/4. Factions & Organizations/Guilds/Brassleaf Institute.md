# Brassleaf Institute

The Brassleaf Institute unveiled the Rootlet and called the Guild when she escaped. It called the Guild again when the Rootlet's tree vanished, and celebrated the Guild as heroes when the stolen tree was returned. The Institute held the replanting ceremony for the Rootlet, whose tree had been taken by [[Cogveil College]].
