# The Horned Cohort

**Leader:** Warmaster [[Velkara Drath]] — a scarred tiefling commander with obsidian horns capped in gold and a voice said to calm riots or start wars.

**Specialties:** Magical assaults, mid-ranged warfare, ambushes, insurgencies, and harsh terrain operations.

---

The Horned Cohort is [[Ash-Korrath]]'s most aggressive martial guild, formed from tieflings no longer content to survive in sanctuary beneath the volcano. They want coin, reputation, status, and a place in the wider world that cannot be denied. Many among them are disgraced officers, ambitious duelists, debt-bound veterans, or exiles eager to turn battlefield success into renewed standing.

Their ranks include flamecasters, javelin companies, hex archers, saboteurs, and veteran infantry trained to strike fast, break formations, and vanish before a counterattack forms. They travel eagerly, fight fiercely, and carry the fire of [[Ash-Korrath]] into every contract they accept.
