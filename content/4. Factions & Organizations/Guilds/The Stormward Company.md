# The Stormward Company

**Leader:** Admiral Seressa Vaelor — a sharp-eyed half-elf commander with silver braided hair, sea-blue tattoos, and a reputation for winning battles before the first blade is drawn.

**Specialties:** Naval warfare, coastal defense, escort contracts, boarding actions, weather magic, and rapid strike deployments from sea to shore.

---

The Stormward Company is Korivar's premier naval contract force, known for hunting pirates, guarding harbors, and escorting merchant vessels through dangerous sea lanes. Their sailors, marines, tidecallers, and battle-mages are trained to fight on shifting decks and storm-dark coasts.

They are often hired by ports, trade councils, and merchant houses that cannot afford to lose a fleet. When Stormward banners rise over a harbor, smugglers get cautious and pirates begin counting exits.
