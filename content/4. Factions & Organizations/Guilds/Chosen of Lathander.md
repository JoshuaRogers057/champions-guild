# Chosen of Lathander

Five Chosen of Lathander exist across Eryndor. Mazgar, Shedinn and Ki Ka'Rah are named Herald, Shield and Wand, and Everrett and Eldarin were later named Wrath and Prayer. Eldarin was named Lightwarden of Sol'Aenor, and the Chosen were honoured across the [[Redmarch Coalition]] after [[Skraggapost]].
