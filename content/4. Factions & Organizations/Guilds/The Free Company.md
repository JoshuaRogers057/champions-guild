# The Free Company

**Leader:** Marshal Garron Vale — a battle-scarred half-elf veteran whose mixed blood and mixed ranks made him the perfect commander for soldiers with nowhere else to belong.

**Specialties:** Adaptable warfare including sieges, open field battles, storm assaults, field fortifications, caravan escort, urban fighting, and frontier defense.

---

The Free Company is Spens given armor and a banner. It is made of people who had nowhere else to go, then became useful enough to matter. Its strength is not tradition or bloodline, but adaptability.

In a world obsessed with ancestry, honor, law, and old grudges, the Free Company offers a blunt alternative: stand in the line, keep the contract, and earn your place. Their ranks include engineers, cavalry, shield lines, scouts, mages, artillery crews, and hardened professionals able to reshape tactics to fit the contract.
