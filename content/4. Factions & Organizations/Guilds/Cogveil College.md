# Cogveil College

The Guild found Cogveil College deeply involved in the theft of the Rootlet's tree, and tied to powerful academic and noble circles. It hosted the summer symposium where the Guild investigated it, and ran a hidden operation of stolen research, prototype technology, illusion manipulation and duplicate constructs. Lingering Cogveil systems controlled the tree's memories, life force and bond until the Guild destroyed them. See also the [[Brassleaf Institute]].
