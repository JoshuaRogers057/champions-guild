# Steel Sails

**Leader:** Unit-Captain Veyr — a lean Steelborn commander with salt-darkened plating, narrow blue eye-lights, and a hullmaker's mark stamped into his shoulder. Calm, exact, and speaks only when an order or answer is needed.

**Specialties:** Naval escort, piracy suppression, amphibious assault, transport security, and coastal bombardment.

---

Steel Sails is a fleet that does not sleep. Once they sign a contract, the work gets done. Their crews move with Steelborn precision, adjusting to weather, distance, damage, and enemy action without panic or delay.

Organic sailors find them unnerving because they do not tire, complain, or lose focus when storms rise. A Steel Sails vessel on the horizon carries a simple promise: the route will be crossed, the cargo will arrive, and resistance will be calculated into the plan.
