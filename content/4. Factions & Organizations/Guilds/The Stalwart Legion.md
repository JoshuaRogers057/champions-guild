# The Stalwart Legion

**Leader:** Tharvik "Ironhead" Gravestone.

**Specialties:** Shieldwall warfare, tunnel fighting, siege defense, fortress retaking, convoy protection, and endurance campaigns.

---

The Stalwart Legion is Kar Dromm's most respected martial guild, famed for honoring contracts above all else. Their word is considered as binding as iron, and breaking a Stalwart contract is remembered for generations.

The Stalwart Legion is a contract carved into stone. When the Legion finally arrives, the line will hold. They are not fast, flashy, or easily moved, but nearly impossible to break once committed. Their word carries the weight of old dwarven oaths, and their grudges are remembered with the same care as their victories. Hiring them means buying certainty. Betraying them means creating a problem that may outlive the betrayer.
