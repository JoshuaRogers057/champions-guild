# The Brass Wardens

**Leader:** Marshal [[Tivik Brassmere]] — an aging gnome veteran with a steel left arm, monocle targeting lens, and a reputation for absolute punctuality.

**Specialties:** Rail security, vault protection, cargo escort, urban defense, siege support, and anti-bandit operations.

---

The Brass Wardens are Lexovar's premier contract force, built primarily from remodeled, repaired, and outdated constructs no longer used in frontline national service. Rather than destroy valuable machines, Lexovar refitted them into a profitable contract force commanded by living officers, artificers, and engineers.

Their ranks are filled with refitted sentinels, restored warframes, field-tested automata, and the gnome artificers who keep them running under pressure. To the Wardens, every contract is part patrol, part engineering challenge, and part restoration project. They are most at home guarding rail lines, securing vaults, escorting cargo, or testing an old construct design in real field conditions.
