# The Dreaming Dark

The Dreaming Dark plotted to assault the dreams of Eryndor and draw the Quari out of Dal Quor. Veyren Solace, a Lucent-Bound servant, was building a Dream Anchor in the Woad, and [[Aethrael]] worked to open a path for it into Eryndor. Its bridge into Eryndor was collapsed at [[Skraggapost]].
