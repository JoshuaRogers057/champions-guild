# Veilkeepers of Oathend

The Veilkeepers of Oathend are an order that keeps necromantic knowledge and relics from evil hands, and they hired the Guild to investigate an estate. They took delivery of the Ossuary Mandate at [[Oathend]]. Their manor fell to undead released by Narthex, and they were recorded as fallen after the vault was lost.
