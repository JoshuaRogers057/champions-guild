# The Vanguard

**Leader:** [[Sir Aelric Dawnshield]] — a golden-armored paladin with a glowing sword. Aelric left a corrupt holy order to found this righteous organization.

**Specialties:** Coastal defense, piracy suppression, trade lane protection, river traffic, and merchant road escort.

---

The Vanguard is a lawfully chartered martial guild that operates in Varondale, known as Champions of Justice and Protectors of the Innocent. Functionally they guard the trade hub of [[Hearthpoint]], serving as an independent defense against piracy and raiders outside of crown authority.

The Vanguard protects the human trade lanes, guarding coastal routes, river traffic, merchant roads, and the cargo fleets that keep [[Kastalshire]] moving. Their shield-over-sail symbol has become a welcome sight to merchants who can afford them. A Vanguard contract is expensive, but so is losing a ship full of grain, coin, or noble passengers.
