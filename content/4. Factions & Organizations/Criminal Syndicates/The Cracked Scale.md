# The Cracked Scale

**Leader:** [[Shaka the Cracked]] — a golden-scaled dragonborn with facial scarring and a reputation for ruthless precision. Wields a magical rapier called Shadowfang.

---

The Cracked Scale is a powerful criminal syndicate based in [[Rakkath]], handling smuggling, laundering, illegal trade, stolen fragments, and restricted goods moving through Dominion systems. It thrives because it understands where the Drakmir Dominion is strongest and where that strength can be quietly exploited.

Its structure relies on smugglers, corrupt quartermasters, spies, assassins, merchants, and military contacts who know how to profit without openly challenging the state. In [[Rakkath]], the Cracked Scale may look like a black-market supply network. In Redmarch, [[Sylvandar]], [[Thundrakar]], or Kar Dromm, it operates through foreign brokers and deniable trade routes.
