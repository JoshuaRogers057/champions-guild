# Smiles Gang

The Smiles Gang is a clown-dressed gang serving a servant of Norebo. It attacked a company searching for a missing husband, then set a trap of travelers in the mountains for ransom, and one member was captured. Its trail later led to a cave outside [[Ciliren]], where vanishing homeless people were lured, and to an underground bunker where it fences stolen goods for sea elves and bribes guards.

The gang was found in floating isles over the [[Lexovar Woods]], where its leader escaped through a portal. Some of its members were corrupted into bestial forms or lost to the tests of a temple trial of rebirth.
