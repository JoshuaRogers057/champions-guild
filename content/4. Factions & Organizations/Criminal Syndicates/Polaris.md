# Polaris

**Leader:** Unknown. No confirmed leader has been identified.

---

Polaris is a mysterious [[Thundrakar]] criminal faction whose motto is *"Bring order to a chaotic world."* Very little is known about its true structure. The organization appears to operate through isolated agents, hidden cells, and coldly disciplined missions that suggest a larger doctrine beneath the surface.

Its goal seems ideological rather than merely criminal: control, order, and the removal of forces it deems destabilizing. In [[Thundrakar]], it may look like a secret society moving through exile camps and winter roads. In other nations, it may appear as quiet fixers, informants, or masked operatives who leave only a white star on black behind them.
