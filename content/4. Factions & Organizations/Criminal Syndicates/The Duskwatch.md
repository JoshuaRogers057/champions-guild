# The Duskwatch

**Leader:** The Veiled Whisper — a mythical goblin believed by some to be multiple individuals throughout time. Identity has never been confirmed.

---

The Duskwatch is Eryndor's thieves' network, built around fences, handlers, stolen goods, and quiet supply lines. The organization survives by knowing who steals, who buys, who needs something hidden, and who can move goods without asking questions.

Other criminal organizations often need the Duskwatch because it can supply tools, sell stolen goods, move contraband, and connect thieves to buyers. Its structure is cell-based, with fences acting as local anchors and handlers controlling what lower members know. In Borgnar, the Duskwatch may look like a coded marketplace of thieves and brokers. In harsher Redmarch territory, it may appear as smugglers, informants, hidden storehouses, or quiet thieves working beneath warband notice.
