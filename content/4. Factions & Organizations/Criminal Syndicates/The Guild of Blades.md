# The Guild of Blades

**Leader:** Known only as The Regent. Identity unconfirmed.

---

The Guild of Blades is one of the most secretive and dangerous organizations in Eryndor. The Guild does not steal, smuggle, or run rackets. It kills. Its work is rare, precise, expensive, and usually political. The Guild is hired when someone wants a single person removed without a war, scandal, or obvious trail.

Its structure is hidden behind handlers, watchers, safe houses, and assassins who may never know the client or one another. In Korivar, it may exist as a whispered tool of last resort. In other regions, it may appear as a lone duelist, a quiet bodyguard, a court servant, or nothing at all until someone important dies.
