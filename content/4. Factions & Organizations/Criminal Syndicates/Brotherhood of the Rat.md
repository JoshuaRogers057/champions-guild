# Brotherhood of the Rat

**Leader:** [[Creeve Barrow]].

---

The Brotherhood of the Rat is blatant, dangerous, and hated across [[Kastalshire]]. Led by [[Creeve Barrow]], the Brotherhood survives through extortion, mugging, gambling dens, dockside rackets, intimidation, and open cruelty against people who cannot easily fight back. They are not elegant criminals and do not pretend to be.

Their structure is built from street crews, enforcers, arsonists, knife-men, informants, and local bosses who rule by fear. Their goal is coin and control, taken as directly as possible. In rural [[Kastalshire]], they may look like road thugs and protection racketeers. In dock cities, they become smugglers, gambling bosses, and violent crews who make sure everyone knows the Rat is watching.
