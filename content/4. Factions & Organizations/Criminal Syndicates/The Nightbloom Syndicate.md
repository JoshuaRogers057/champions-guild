# The Nightbloom Syndicate

**Leader:** Lady [[Vaerisse Thornshade]] — an elegant elven noblewoman publicly known as a patron of gardens and charity, privately feared as the hidden hand behind Nightbloom.

---

The Nightbloom Syndicate is the most prominent criminal network of the Branch Sphere, with its strongest presence in [[Rael]]. Nightbloom operates through merchant circles, harbor channels, private salons, and back-alley vice. It is not merely a hidden organization — its runners, hired blades, and collectors are known on the streets, especially where trade and desperation meet.

The Syndicate deals in illicit goods, blackmail, forgery, protection rackets, and quiet violence when needed. Its upper ranks favor discretion and elegance, while its lower ranks are visible enough to control territory and intimidate rivals. A noble may know Nightbloom as a source of secrets. A dockworker may know them as the people who come knocking when coin is owed.
