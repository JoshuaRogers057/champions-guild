# The Velvet Ledger

**Leader:** [[Selrik]] — a masked figure who always appears in gold.

---

The Velvet Ledger is one of the most subtle and powerful criminal organizations in Eryndor. Led by [[Selrik]], a masked figure always seen in gold, the Ledger hides behind trade houses, banks, shipping records, investment circles, and respectable financial offices. Its members rarely threaten anyone directly. They ruin families through debt, buy officials through favors, bury rivals through false records, and move markets with a few quiet signatures.

Their structure is built around accountants, brokers, blackmailers, assassins, and silent partners who often appear completely legitimate. In Thalenmark, they may look like refined financiers. In other regions, they may appear as moneylenders, trade consultants, shipping clerks, or harmless record keepers who know exactly which number can destroy a life.
