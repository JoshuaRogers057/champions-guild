# The Ashwings

**Leader:** [[Kaelis Emberveil]] — a masked firebrand agitator known for speeches, sabotage, and vanishing before dawn.

---

The Ashwings are a resistance-criminal undercurrent in the [[Bastions of Solara]]. They define themselves as enemies of Solara's hypocrisy, striking at symbols of false holiness, corrupt officials, abusive officers, and systems that hide cruelty beneath radiant language.

Their structure is cell-based and emotional, built from agitators, saboteurs, dissidents, informants, and desperate people from forgotten lower districts. Their goal is exposure, disruption, and revenge against a nation they believe has mistaken image for justice. In [[Auris Prime]], they may look like underground rebels. In border regions, they may become smugglers, pamphleteers, arsonists, or fugitives sheltered by the disillusioned.
