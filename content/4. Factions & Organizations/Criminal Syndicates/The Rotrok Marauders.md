# The Rotrok Marauders

**Leader:** Gorath "The Butcher" Krenz — a ruthless artificer with a passion for chaos.

---

The Rotrok Marauders are a rogue coalition of bandits, mercenaries, saboteurs, and violent opportunists. Their structure is rough but dangerous, with raiding crews, corrupt engineers, tunnel scouts, hired blades, and artificer-backed destroyers who know how to make stone fail.

Their goal is plunder, disruption, and the pleasure of turning secure places into traps. In Kar Dromm, they may look like mine saboteurs and tunnel raiders. Outside dwarven lands, they may become hired wreckers, caravan attackers, or mercenary criminals specializing in destruction.
