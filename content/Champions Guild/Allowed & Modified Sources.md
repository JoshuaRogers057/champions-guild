# Allowed & Modified Sources

The following is the definitive list of sourcebooks and content allowed on the server. If a source is not listed here, please assume it is **not** allowed without prior admin approval.

---

## ✅ Core Rules (2024)

The following books are allowed in their entirety using the 2024 ruleset updates.

- **Player's Handbook** (2024 Edition)
- **Dungeon Master's Guide** (2024 Edition)
- **Monster Manual** (2024 Edition)

---

## ⚠️ Allowed with Modifications

Content from these sources is allowed, but specific mechanical changes have been applied to fit the server's balance.

### Eberron: Forge of the Artificer

- No Feats
- No House Backgrounds

### Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn

- **Purple Dragon Commandant (Feat)**
- **Modified Prerequisite:** You must have the **Purple Dragon Rook** feat AND **Martial Weapon Proficiency**.

---

## 🛡️ Restricted (Requires Approval)

The following content is available for use, but you must open a ticket for approval before adding it to your character sheet.

### Astarion's Book of Hungers

- **Vampire Devotee** (Background)
- **Dhampir** (Species)

---

## ⚠️ Custom Feats

The following feats have been adapted from old source books.

### Gunner

*General Feat (Prerequisite: Level 4+, Dexterity 13+, Proficiency with Firearms)*

You gain the following benefits.

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Ignore Loading.** You ignore the Loading property of the Pistol and Musket. If you're holding one of them, you can load a piece of ammunition into it even if you lack a free hand.

**Firing in Melee.** Being within 5 feet of an enemy doesn't impose Disadvantage on your attack rolls with firearms.
