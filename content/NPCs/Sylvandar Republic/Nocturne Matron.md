# Nocturne Matron

## Overview
- **Location / Context:** [[Night Bloom Syndicate]]
- **Affiliation:** [[Sylvandar Republic]]
- **Occurrences in source material:** 2

## Role & Notes
- The Syndicate is believed to have cells in Sylvandar, Rael, and Thenal, with quiet influence in Val'Quith through military supply contracts and noble patrons. Their symbol is a pale flower opening beneath a black moon. Their rumored leader is called the Nocturne Matron, though no one agrees whether this figure is a single elf, a rotating title, or a fiction used to protect the true leadership.
