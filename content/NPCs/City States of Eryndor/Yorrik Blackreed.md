# Yorrik Blackreed

## Overview
- **Location / Context:** [[The dual City of Lizardfolk and bullywags]]
- **Affiliation:** [[City States of Eryndor]]
- **Occurrences in source material:** 2

## Role & Notes
- Yorrik Blackreed – He was born a lizardfolk, and after a yuan-ti raid was discovered by a kind bullywug family and raised as one of them. He serves an ideal function as a bridge between the two people. Yorrik is fully loyal to the market district. He runs the Trade Pool with quiet authority and a memory for every deal ever made.
