# Cristpas Hutin

## Overview
- **Location / Context:** [[The Roost]]

## Role & Notes
- Cristpas Hutin – The aged leader of The Roost. An owlin of great renown, he is slow to speak and slower still to judge. His memory stretches back more than a century, and his counsel carries the weight of legend.
- Cristpas Hutin – Ancient owlin leader of the Council of the Sky-Wise. Frail in body, formidable in judgment. Said to remember weather patterns from a century ago more clearly than yesterday’s meal.
