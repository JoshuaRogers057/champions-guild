# Thalkur Brazevein

Warden Thalkur Brazevein – Brass-jawed commander of the Molten Guard.

Crucible Row – Forge and Flame Here, metal is baptized in soulfire at the Soulforge Crucible. Warden Thalkur Brazevein, a brass-jawed war veteran, oversees the Molten Guard. He is feared for executing deserters by fire-duel.
