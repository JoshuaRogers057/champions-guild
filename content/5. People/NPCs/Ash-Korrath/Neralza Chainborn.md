# Neralza Chainborn

Archpactress Neralza Chainborn – Blindfolded contract master of the Cinder Court.

Beside the throne, the Cinder Court debates the fate of the city. Chief among its voices is Archpactress Neralza Chainborn, a blindfolded tiefling whose mastery of soul-thread contracts is said to bind devils to silence and nobles to obedience.
