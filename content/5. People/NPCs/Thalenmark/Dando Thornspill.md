# Dando Thornspill

Theatrical and cunning, Dando Thornspill controls plays, newspapers, courier networks, and blackmail pipelines throughout the [[Thalenmark Empire]]. He shapes public opinion ahead of key votes, says little directly — but nothing escapes his gaze.

During [[The Talvereth Crisis]] he was rescued from a fortified bank by a Champions Guild Apprentice team, making him one of the eight surviving Gilded Council members.
