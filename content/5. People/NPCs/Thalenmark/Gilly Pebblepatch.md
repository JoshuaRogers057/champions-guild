# Gilly Pebblepatch

Matron Gilly Pebblepatch leads the village of [[Snootburrow]], tending its underground mushroom and truffle farms. A skilled herbalist, she quietly supports [[Yondalla]] over [[Waukeen]] in the empire's growing religious divide.
