# Brand Oakmantle

Brand Oakmantle runs a network of worker-owned mills and looms across the [[Thalenmark Empire]]. A Free Trade Faction stalwart, he is one of the few Council members whose wealth is built on cooperatives rather than monopoly.

He is one of the eight Gilded Council members to survive [[The Talvereth Crisis]].
