# Rillan Dewdrop

Rillan Dewdrop manages the staples exchange, maintaining fair-price bands for grain and fish from the harvest fleets — keeping food affordable for common folk across the [[Thalenmark Empire]].

He is one of the eight Gilded Council members to survive [[The Talvereth Crisis]].
