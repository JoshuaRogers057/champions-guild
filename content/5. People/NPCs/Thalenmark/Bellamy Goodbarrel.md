# Bellamy Goodbarrel

Matron Bellamy Goodbarrel leads the village of [[Sweetroot]]. Elderly and kind, with a steel-trap mind for preserving techniques and negotiation, she works to protect village autonomy from [[Talvereth]]'s reach.

She was a distant cousin of the late [[Marlowe Grint]] and despised what he had become.
