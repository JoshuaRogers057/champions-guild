# Selrik

Selrik is the masked leader of [[The Velvet Ledger]], the farthest-reaching criminal organization in all of Eryndor. Always appearing in gold, their true identity — even their species — is unknown. Through blackmail, insider trading, and market manipulation, Selrik is believed to influence members of the Gilded Council itself.

[[The Velvet Ledger]] was a driving force behind [[The Talvereth Crisis]] and remains active in [[Talvereth]].
