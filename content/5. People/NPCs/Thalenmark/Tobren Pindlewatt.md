# Tobren Pindlewatt

Tobren Pindlewatt is the quiet master of banking and finance in the [[Thalenmark Empire]]. As Chief of Coinring Investments he directs capital allocation, factoring, and venture pools across the empire. Rarely seen in public, Tobren is believed to be either a reclusive genius or a magically projected illusion.

He is one of the eight Gilded Council members to survive [[The Talvereth Crisis]].
