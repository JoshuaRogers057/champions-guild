# Marlowe Grint

Once a street hustler, Marlowe "Silvertongue" Grint rose to become a real estate and logistics titan and Master of Merchant Arbitration. Charming and subtle, he ran so-called "clean-room" oaths and was rumored to operate an invisible network of spies and blackmailers.

His distant cousin, Matron [[Bellamy Goodbarrel]] of [[Sweetroot]], despised what he had become.

---

## Death

Killed during [[The Talvereth Crisis]]. The exact circumstances of their death are not publicly known.
