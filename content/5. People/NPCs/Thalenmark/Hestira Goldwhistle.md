# Hestira Goldwhistle

Tough, practical, and commanding, Hestira Goldwhistle owned nearly half the Saltgate Docks and controlled hull capacity and yard timelines across the [[Thalenmark Empire]]. She lost her arm to a sea monster and replaced it with a magical prosthetic.

---

## Death

Killed during [[The Talvereth Crisis]]. The exact circumstances of their death are not publicly known.
