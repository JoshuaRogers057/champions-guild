# Corin Hearthwhistle

Corin Hearthwhistle oversaw security contracting and procurement for the [[Thalenmark Empire]], including licensing of [[The Bronze Talons]] and private enforcement vendors.

---

## Death

Killed during [[The Talvereth Crisis]]. The exact circumstances of their death are not publicly known. With his death, no one currently oversees Bronze Talons licensing.
