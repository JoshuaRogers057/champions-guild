# Jeffro Goldbrook

Jeffro Goldbrook built Goldbrook & Co. into one of the empire's great lending houses, promising "access to capital" while expanding into haulage.

---

## Death

Jeffro Goldbrook was assassinated in his own home roughly three months before [[The Talvereth Crisis]]. His killer was never identified. The campaign to fill his vacant Council seat — widely seen as bought and rigged — convinced many that the whole system was corrupt, and became one of the sparks that ignited the riots. Goldbrook & Co. remains leaderless, and credit markets are destabilized.
