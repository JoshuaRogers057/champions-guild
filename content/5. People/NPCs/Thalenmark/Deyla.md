# Deyla

Deyla is the red-haired halfling leader of [[The Bronze Talons]], the elite mercenary company of [[Talvereth]] that handles security, debt collection, and contract enforcement. Pragmatic in her leadership, she rides a Pteranodon named **Swiftwing**.

Sheriff [[Mo Mumblefoot]] of [[Dapplebank]] once served as a route scout under her command.
