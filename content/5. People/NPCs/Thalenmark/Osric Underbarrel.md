# Osric Underbarrel

Osric Underbarrel controls the auction houses of the Grand Exchange in [[Talvereth]], wielding listing power over what is bought and sold on the continent's largest marketplace.

He is one of the eight Gilded Council members to survive [[The Talvereth Crisis]].
