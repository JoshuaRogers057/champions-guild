# Bramble Muffletop

Chef Bramble Muffletop leads [[Nibblebrook]], the comfort food capital of the empire and home of its culinary academy. He maintains a friendly rivalry with Vintner [[Hobbin Grapecheek]] of [[Glimtwine]], competing annually at the Harvest Jubilee.
