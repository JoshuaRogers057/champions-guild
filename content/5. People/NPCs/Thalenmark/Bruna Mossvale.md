# Bruna Mossvale

Bruna Mossvale was a fiery-eyed Yondallan prophetess who preached on the outskirts of [[Talvereth]], proclaiming that the "Age of Greed" would bring ruin if not mended by repentance and a return to halfling humility. She rapidly gained rural followers and predicted a "reckoning of coin," attracting both fanatical supporters and wealthy enemies. She was disliked by nearly every member of the Gilded Council.

Her movement became one of the key sparks of [[The Talvereth Crisis]]. Some towns call her a saint and martyr; others, a traitor.

---

## Death

Bruna Mossvale died during [[The Talvereth Crisis]]. Her followers remain active, and her death has only deepened her legend among the rural faithful.
