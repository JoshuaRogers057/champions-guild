# Ginder Tosslepot

Clocksmith Ginder Tosslepot leads the village of [[Brasshill]]. An eccentric inventor of halfling watches and minor automatons, he dreams of an academic post at Hearthlight University.

He corresponded with the late [[Velza Merriweather-Bramblehook]] on arcane gearwork theories.
