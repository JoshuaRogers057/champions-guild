# Velza Merriweather-Bramblehook

Velza Merriweather-Bramblehook headed the Enchanters' Consortium, controlling the licensing and supply of enchanted goods. She lived a life of glamor and spectacle, with floating projections of her profit margins trailing her like a cape. She corresponded with Clocksmith [[Ginder Tosslepot]] of [[Brasshill]] on arcane gearwork theories.

---

## Death

Killed during [[The Talvereth Crisis]]. The exact circumstances of their death are not publicly known.
