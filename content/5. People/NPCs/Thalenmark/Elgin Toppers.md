# Elgin Toppers

Hearthfather Elgin Toppers leads [[Cloverfold]], the tea-farming village at the heart of the empire's religious tensions after a Waukeenite-funded bank attempted to seize ancestral farmland — a conflict that left three dead and a temple burned.
