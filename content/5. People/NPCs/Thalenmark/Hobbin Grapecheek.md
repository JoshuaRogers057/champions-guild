# Hobbin Grapecheek

Vintner Hobbin Grapecheek leads the village of [[Glimtwine]] and its vineyards. A master winemaker, charismatic and well-traveled, he maintains a friendly rivalry with Chef [[Bramble Muffletop]] of [[Nibblebrook]], competing annually at the Harvest Jubilee.
