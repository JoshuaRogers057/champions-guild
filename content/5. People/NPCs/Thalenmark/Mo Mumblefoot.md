# Mo Mumblefoot

Sheriff Mo Mumblefoot leads the village of [[Dapplebank]] and its halfling constabulary school. Honest and gruff, he enforces rural law with wisdom and wit, and feels alienated from [[Talvereth]] bureaucracy.

He is a former route scout who served under [[Deyla]] of [[The Bronze Talons]].
