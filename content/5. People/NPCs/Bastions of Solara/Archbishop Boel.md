# Archbishop Boel

Archbishop Boel was called before the Grand Jury of Solara over the theft of Starforged materials. He fled the [[Bastions of Solara]] and was wanted by the state.

He was later tried before the Tribunal of the White Sword, found guilty by the Triumvirate of Radiance and sentenced to death by the Procession of Lights. Rescuers tried to free him.
