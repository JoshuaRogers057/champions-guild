# Lieutenant Rahmiel

Lieutenant Rahmiel is an officer of the Bastions of Solara who asked the Guild to find who stole materials for Starforged arms, suspecting officials of high rank. He later summoned the Guild to guard the execution of Archbishop Boel, and has sworn that Lord Varos Neightreign will answer for his treachery.

He sent the Guild escort wagon and priests to the Starforges and afterwards began writing further contracts, then asked the Guild to look into missing children and to be discreet. He believed Ashwing watchers followed his movements, and he is the childhood friend and oathbrother of Sariel Veyne.
