# Helvar Rimebraze

## Overview
- **Affiliation:** [[Bastions of Solara]]
- **Occurrences in source material:** 2

## Role & Notes
- The forge-town is overseen by Forge-Canon Helvar Rimebraze, a battle-scarred veteran turned master artificer-priest. Once a frontline commander for the [[Dawnward]] Legions, Helvar lost his left arm in a border war and had it replaced with a radiant-forged limb...
- his forge rituals mix military cadence with scripture. Helvar answers only to the Highlord of Valor and serves as the final approver of all relics bound for the Legions, Skyborne, or elite orders. His temper is legendary. So is his loyalty.
