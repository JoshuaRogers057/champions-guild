# Lorelia Solmere

This mist-veiled island town serves as a quiet retreat for wounded veterans and philosophers. Marble statues of angels line its shores. Notable Feature: The Bell of Reprieve, an enormous floating chime that only sounds during moments of cosmic alignment.
