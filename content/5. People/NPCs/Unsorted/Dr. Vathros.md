# Dr. Vathros

Dr. Vathros, called the Vivisectionist, sent the vampire [[Bictra]] to Narthex with a means of teleporting to him directly. His lieutenants operated the machinery that expanded the [[Oathend]] dome. At his castle he offered the Guild a bargain: he would dismantle the domes and disappear for a hundred years if they let him live, and the Guild refused twice.

His wife, once lost to illness, was rebuilt as his greatest creation and fought beside him, while his apprentice Narthex abandoned him mid-battle. The Guild believes he was destroyed in the Empire of Bones assault, after a fight of nearly two hours.
