# Dr. Vathros

Dr. Vathros, called the Vivisectionist, was a vampire and a necromancer. He was one of the vampires held in thrall by the vampire lord Tavard, and went free when members of the Guild killed Tavard at a masquerade in August 2025 (Wispers and Fog). Unlike most of the freed vampires he kept to himself and went his own way. He did not make the Dome over Zindariel, which stood long before him and whose making nobody understands, but he made it his headquarters, since necromancy is stronger there than anywhere else in Eryndor. He studied it until he learned how to make more, and used that knowledge to raise the new domes of 2026. Many of the monsters that later spread across the continent came out of his laboratories there.

He sent the vampire [[Bictra]] to Narthex with a means of teleporting to him directly. His lieutenants operated the machinery that expanded the [[Oathend]] dome. At his castle he offered the Guild a bargain: he would dismantle the domes and disappear for a hundred years if they let him live, and the Guild refused twice.

By the time the Guild reached his castle he had become a lich as well as a vampire, and the final battle was the first time anyone saw him in that form. His wife, once lost to illness, was rebuilt as his greatest creation and fought beside him, while his apprentice Narthex abandoned him mid-battle. The Guild believes he was destroyed in the Empire of Bones assault, after a fight of nearly two hours.
