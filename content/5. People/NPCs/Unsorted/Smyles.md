# Smyles

Smyles is Digdug's wife, who survived the farm attack but was held under cursed Ledger chains, then moved to an unknown place. She was recovered as a doll after the Velvet Ledger discarded her, and was later restored to her own body and reunited with Digdug and [[Firebug]].
