# Dura

Dura is a teenage orc warrior who guided the Guild into [[Borgnar]] and showed contempt for outsiders. Dura smiled faintly at the Guild's success at the Opening Games. Later, Dura prepared the Guild's private barracks and asked for payment in training with Valdrin.
