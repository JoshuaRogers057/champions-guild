# Firebug

Firebug is Digdug's son, taken alive years ago and recorded by the Velvet Ledger as Subject F-13 and renamed Kindle. Digdug recovered him at Old Marnie's cottage, where he had accidentally turned his mother into a doll. His magic manifested unstably during the ritual that restored his mother, [[Smyles]].
