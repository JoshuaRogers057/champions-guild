# The Rootlet

The Rootlet is a botanical construct of the Brassleaf Institute. She was newly unveiled when she escaped during a public demonstration, and was recovered. Her bonded tree was later taken, and she crumbled, leaving only her heart-seed. She chose to return to her tree on her own terms and is growing again.
