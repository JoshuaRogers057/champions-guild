# Bictra

Bictra is a vampire in the service of the vivisectionist [[Dr. Vathros]], first met in [[Elkhara]]. She was met again in the vault beneath the Veilkeepers' manor, where she was disfigured by Aubrey and spirited away. She was killed at the Last Tower, where she was experimenting on her victims, and Aubrey's badge was recovered from her.
