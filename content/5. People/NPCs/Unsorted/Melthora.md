# Melthora

Melthora is a hag whose jewel-encrusted music box was taken by Captain Cavanth; his daughter Saxson was transformed by her curse. The Guild hoped to pay her price to heal Tallo.

She struck bargains with Guild members, trading favours owed, a Hag's Eye and a ship, and became Wizbit's patron. Bogrum learned upsetting things about his pact with her.
