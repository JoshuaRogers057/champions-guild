# Grashna

Grashna is a Blood Priest who runs the Bloodpits arena with Kragga Ironskull, and he agreed to let Ren Stray fight beside Mira. After Mira was freed he declared a blood feud on the Guild.

He seemed displeased at the Guild's success in the Opening Games. He later put out his own left eye beside Torgar in honour of Gruumsh and Krok, and his hostility to the Guild ended.
