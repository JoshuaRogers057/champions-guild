# Marshal Drazan Volsk

Marshal Drazan Volsk was a servant of the Vivisectionist, Dr. Vathros. Volsk operated the dome machinery beneath Oathend and escaped, and was later killed at Greyspire Fortress.

The Guild believes Dr. Vathros was destroyed in the Empire of Bones assault.
