# Gregor the Fourth

This continued until the year 752, when the human king Gregor the Fourth, known for his wisdom and justice, wary of the elves and their influence over the nation, challenged their authority and their vaulted wisdom. He spent his reign refusing warfare and instead beginning institutes and monasteries.
