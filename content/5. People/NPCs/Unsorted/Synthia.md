# Synthia

Synthia is a girl turned half spider by [[The Dusk Queen]]'s curse and a priestess in training of Mielikki, who came to the Guild with the wood elf Noir. She asked the Guild to return a baby owlbear to its mother, and enclave members called her Synthesia while the Guild kept her hidden.

She was the target of a kidnapping attempt at a fair in the Slums District, and was rescued. She was revealed as a member of the Oakspire Enclave, which demanded her return.
