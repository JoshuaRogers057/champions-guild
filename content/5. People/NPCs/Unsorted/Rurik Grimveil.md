# Rurik Grimveil

Rurik Grimveil is leader of the small goliath Clan Glimveil, and hired the Guild to raid pirates and invited it to help take a castle. He agreed to guard Barrowbank and drive off its besiegers in exchange for information and payment. He received the plans for Castle Kelgor, which is now led by him.
