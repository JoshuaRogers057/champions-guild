# Elowen

Elowen was sought in [[Contramara]], where a party of mages was already pursuing her. She is Vaelynn's niece, and Vaelynn learned her location from a sea hag in return for a bargain. She was rescued from a Guild of Blades ship at Korivar, then teleported to the hag Seana when Vaelynn claimed her.
