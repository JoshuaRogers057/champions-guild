# Sabina Grint

Sabina Grint hired the Guild to investigate Ashbarrows' failing small businesses, and later hired it to find the clerk Edrin Halver and received its report. She held a public feast for nobles and commonfolk to ease tensions. She also sent the Guild to look into rising prices, and may use the evidence in the courts.
