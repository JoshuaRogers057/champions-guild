# Aethrael

Aethrael is a Planetar who disappeared, and was found Inspired at the Veiled Chorus preaching enforced harmony before escaping. Aethrael intended to open a major rift at [[Skraggapost]] to admit the Dreaming Dark, and was captured alive after the rifts were closed.
