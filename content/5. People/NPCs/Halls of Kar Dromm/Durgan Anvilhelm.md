# Durgan Anvilhelm

Situated directly beside a tributary shaft of the Stone Road, [[Stonebarrow]] is a humming industrial tunnel complex where ore is hauled night and day. Durgan's gruff leadership has made the settlement one of the realm’s most efficient raw metal suppliers.
