# Skolda Bearbane

Nestled in a high-altitude bowl surrounded by jagged cliffs, Volgrimm’s Roost trains war goats, cave bears, and mountain drakes. Skolda’s command of creatures is near-legendary, and her riders are vital to dwarven border patrols.
