# Morra Steelvein

A mountain fortress embedded in a sheer cliff face, [[Khazgrim Hold]] guards the northern mountain pass. Its walls are thick, its ballistas enchanted, and its troops hardened. Jarl Morra is known for her stone-cold efficiency and unshakable nerve.
