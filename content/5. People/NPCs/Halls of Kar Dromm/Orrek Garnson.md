# Orrek Garnson

Built into the exposed spine of a granite ridge, [[Garnhold]] is known for crafting unbreakable tools, siege parts, and mining implements. Orrek’s iron-forged pragmatism and invention have made [[Garnhold]] indispensable to the kingdom’s logistics.
