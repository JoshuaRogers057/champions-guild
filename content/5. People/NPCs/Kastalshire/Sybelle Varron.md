# Sybelle Varron

Duchess Sybelle Varron rules from here with grace. She is known for her devotion to the Kingdom but her true passion lies in her parties. She knows everyone and is a relentless gossip. The duchy is managed by her advisors while she plans her parties. She is not corrupt, just vapid.

Duchess Sybelle Varron (Lyricast): A socialite and hostess, politically passive but well-connected. Delegates rule while maintaining high social standing.
