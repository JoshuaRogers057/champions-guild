# Arven Dallinar

Count Arven Dallinar is a polished nobleman raised in [[Contramara]]. Though not native to Aremir, he’s proven adept at balancing profits with local loyalty. He’s expanded [[Falbrin]]’s mining guilds and brought in dwarven architects to improve structural stability. He’s also known for his public festivals, which are rare but extravagant. He is not favored by Duke [[Garran Velmouth|Velmouth]].
