# Belric Morrin

Count Belric Morrin is a refined, soft-spoken aristocrat known for his refined manners and forward-thinking policies. A lover of art and architecture, he has funded the expansion of [[Frostford]]’s public gardens and sponsored several bardic competitions. Though his city is far from danger, he maintains strong ties to Duke Renward despite the duke's open hostility to the [[Contramara]] government.
