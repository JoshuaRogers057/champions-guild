# Mira Veynar

A city guard in Raethalor and a childhood friend of Ren Stray. She helped the Guild look into the missing illegitimate nobles of Kastalshire, and was later kidnapped and forced to fight in the Bloodpits of Borgnar for nearly two months before the Guild freed her.
