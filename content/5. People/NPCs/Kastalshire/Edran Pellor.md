# Edran Pellor

Count Edran Pellor is an adventurous former sea captain whose noble family traded in foodstuffs. Edran Pellor made his reputation sailing shipments into hard-to-access places. He retired inland to oversee [[Caer Merel]] after the death of his father in the year 1018. With a booming voice and a hundred stories, he’s beloved by the farmers who feed the kingdom. Rumors are he is growing bored and that his attention to duty is slipping.
