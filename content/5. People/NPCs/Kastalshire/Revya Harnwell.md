# Revya Harnwell

Countess Revya Harnwell is a former military commander who governs with disciplined efficiency. She personally oversees tax collection, trade route security, and mining rights. While she lacks Duchess Sybelle’s grace, her respect is unquestioned—especially among the merchants and miners. She is a frequent guest to Duchess Varron.

Countess Revya Harnwell ([[Dawnmarch]]): Stern, competent, and militaristic. Trusted by merchants and strong in tax collection. A key political ally of Duchess Varron.
