# Elric Hawnspear

Duke Elric Hawnspear is a grizzled, broad-shouldered noble with calloused hands and a quiet mind. Once a forester to a minor noble house, he rose through the ranks not by politics, but by loyalty, battle merit, and wisdom. Known for working alongside druids and agricultural mages, he knew that a hungry people know no loyalty and made full bellies a staple of his rule. He’s a popular ruler who favors simple truths and strong walls.

Duke Elric Hawnspear (Caer Thian): Former forester turned popular leader. Pragmatic and loyal, known for his agricultural reforms and alliance with druids.
