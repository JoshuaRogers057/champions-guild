# Liraeth Dorn

## Overview
- **Location / Context:** [[Kastalshire nobility]]
- **Affiliation:** [[Kastalshire]]

## Role & Notes
- Countess Liraeth Dorn is a firm but admired leader, a battle-scarred woman known for quelling a miner's revolt with negotiation instead of force. When the next group of miners thought her weak she sealed the mines and let them negotiate their release through stone. She holds the respect of the frontier garrisons and oversees vast operations extracting iron and tin from the northern hills. She is quiet, severe, and efficient. It has long been rumored that she is sleeping with Duke [[Velmouth]].
- Duke Garran [[Velmouth]] (Aremir): A survivalist former battalion commander, skeptical of centralized rule. Respected for his military past but at odds with crown loyalty. Rumored relationship with Countess Liraeth Dorn.
- Countess Liraeth Dorn ([[Arestead]]): Resolute and intelligent, known for negotiation under pressure. Admired for defusing a miners' revolt and for managing dangerous frontier operations.
