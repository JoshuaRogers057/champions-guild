# Cipher-Saint Echobronze

Cipher-Saint Echobronze – Archivist of the Cogspire Vaults. A slender Steelborn with filigree-like etching along his limbs and glowing ocular lenses. Echobronze composes thought-poems out of combat data and weaves fragments of memory into logic-music. He teaches through paradox.
