# Been Laddel

Clockspire is led by a tall Gnome with a beard that stretches just past his knees, Been Laddel.

Notable NPC: Headmaster Been Laddel is a tall, long-bearded gnome who leads the Institute with strange patience. He often pauses mid-sentence, as if waiting for time to catch up with him.

Been Laddel of Clockspire publicly insisted his city stood above petty competition. This claim was often delivered while unveiling a superior version of someone else’s work. Many inventions emerged from this era. So did many fires.
