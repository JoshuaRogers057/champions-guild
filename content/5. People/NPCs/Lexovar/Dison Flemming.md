# Dison Flemming

[[Nimblethorp]] is the industrial and civil engineering powerhouse of the gnome nation. It is home to Gearshield Academy, which specializes in engineering-based combat suits, bridgework, and civic innovation. The academy is overseen by Headmaster Dison Flemming, a mathematical prodigy and the youngest ever to sit on the Arcane Council.

Notable NPC: Headmaster Dison Flemming is a mathematical prodigy and the youngest member of the Arcane Council. He treats engineering like moral philosophy and considers inefficient design a personal insult.
