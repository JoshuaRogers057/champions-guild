# Thresk Taloncrest

General Thresk Taloncrest – The city’s master tactician and former war hero. Scorched by dragonfire long ago, he now teaches younger warriors how to strike from the clouds and disappear like thunder.

The Roost’s military is commanded by General Thresk Taloncrest, an aging aarakocra known for his war record against draconic raiders in his youth. His feathers are scorched in places, and his left wing no longer supports long flight, but his mind is a storm...
