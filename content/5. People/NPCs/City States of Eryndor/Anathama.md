# Anathama

Vash’Ka is ruled by strength, ambition, and coils of subtle manipulation. The absolute authority lies with Anathama, a powerful female yuan-ti abomination who has ruled longer than any rival dares to whisper. Her word is law, but her grip is always tested. The yuan-ti test strength and authority it is their nature.

Vash’Ka, ruled by Anathama, remains hierarchical, secretive, and dangerous. Noble houses scheme constantly. Hydra cults persist. Their artisans are masters of boundwood craft, venom lore, and patient cruelty.
