Sirikra Vellune is a member of the Cracked Scale hired to run the outpost in [[Kryzath]] day to day. [[Anathema]] struck Sirikra in the face with a shovel. Sirikra sold Anathema to a Dominion officer and was dismissed for the betrayal.
