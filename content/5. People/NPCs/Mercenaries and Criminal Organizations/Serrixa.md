# Serrixa

Serrixa kept the Bolt Hole tavern in [[Rakkath]], was killed and revived, and escaped. Serrixa later led Cracked Scale forces at an outpost and escaped again after being wounded. A Cracked Scale storm mage was held captive on the Rat's Nest and freed by the Guild.
