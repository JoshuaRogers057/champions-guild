## Death

The Drakmir Dominion remains [[Sylvandar]]'s most emotionally charged rival. The Long War, the burning of Emberwood, the death of Prince-General Caelthir Vaelith, and the destruction of sacred Woaden Trees remain open wounds. Trade may exist in limited form where necessity demands it, but genuine trust is absent.

First was the death of Prince-General Caelthir Vaelith, whose fall remained an open wound within the royal house and among those who still believed Elven noble blood ought to command destiny.
