# Ayren Thornleaf

Captain Ayren Thornleaf – Leader of the Woaden Rangers, a stoic and near-legendary figure who enforces the laws protecting the sacred trees. Said to have once slain a corrupted treant with a single arrow.

Built vertically through the forest canopy, the city operates in tiers of elevated platforms, woven bridges, and cascading light spells. [[Sylvandar]] is a harmony of nature, arcana, and artistry. The entire city is a living tribute to the elven ethos of beauty...
