# Myrrik Velthorn

Scale Bearer Myrrik Velthorn – A gold-scaled dragonborn economist and master of trade law. Velthorn wears robes of scale-etched contracts and speaks in layers of price and value. Considered incorruptible in principle, but unshakably ruthless in policy.
