# Vyrrak Stormcleave

Scale Bearer Vyrrak Stormcleave – Bronze-scaled and storm-scarred, Vyrrak is a former reaver admiral turned statesman. He is famous for slaying a sea serpent atop a flaming mast during the Leviathan Ambush. Keeps a harpoon by his side at all times.
