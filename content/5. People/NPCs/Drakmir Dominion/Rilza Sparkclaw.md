# Rilza Sparkclaw

Guildmistress Rilza Sparkclaw: The kobold chief of engineering. Brilliant, condescending, and indispensable. She keeps the Scaled Warrens and most of the city’s infrastructure functioning with ruthless efficiency and a quick word.
