# General Dracnar Kiz

General Dracnar Kiz is a general of the [[Drakmir Dominion Info|Drakmir Dominion]] at [[Kryzath]]. He enlisted the Guild against the flying fortress and offered an outpost in return for one more mission.

He refused a duel of honor under mysterious circumstances. The challenger, a young cadet named Drakath, proved to be his illegitimate son, and the two were reconciled.
