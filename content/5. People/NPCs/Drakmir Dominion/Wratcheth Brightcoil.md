# Wratcheth Brightcoil

Scale Bearer Wratcheth Brightcoil – A white-scaled kobold genius and [[Dalath]]’s representative to the Dominion Conclave. Known for his manic focus, constant muttering, and disdain for “creative deviation.” Kavrix wears a mechanical monocle linked to a head-mounted lens array and is never without his automaton assistant “Clank.”
