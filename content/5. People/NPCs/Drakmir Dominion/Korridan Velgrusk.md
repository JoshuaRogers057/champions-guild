# Korridan Velgrusk

Wavemaster Korridan Velgrusk – Storm-priest and captain of the *Tempest’s Howl*. Leads sea patrols and weather rituals. His staff doubles as a lightning rod, and his crew swears by his gift to call wind and veil ships in fog.
