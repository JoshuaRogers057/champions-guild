## 📚 Books

### Core Rulebooks (2024)

These books can be used in their entirety with the standard 2024 ruleset — no extra steps needed.

- **Player's Handbook** (2024 Edition)
- **Dungeon Master's Guide** (2024 Edition)
- **Monster Manual** (2024 Edition)

### Arcana Unleashed

You may use content from this book, except:

**Banned**
- **Otherworldly Familiar** (Feat)
- **Epic Boon** feats

### Eberron — Forge of the Artificer

You may use content from this book, except:

**Banned**
- Feats
- House Backgrounds

### Forgotten Realms — Heroes of Faerûn

You may use content from this book, with one change:

**Modified**
- **Purple Dragon Commandant** (Feat): you must have the **Purple Dragon Rook** feat **and** Martial Weapon Proficiency.

> [!note] Eryndor names
> Some backgrounds and feats from this book have been renamed slightly to fit the lore of Eryndor, so they may appear under a different name on the website and in the Lore Master's Adaptation Guide (for example, Purple Dragon Rook is Knightly Order Rook). They work the same way. This page uses the official book names.

### Lorwyn — First Light

*Feywild-touched content.* Only the following pieces from this book are allowed.

**Allowed Species**
- **Rimekin**
- **Lorwyn Changeling**

**Allowed Feats**
- **Shadowmoor Hexer**
- **Child of the Sun**

**Allowed Backgrounds**
- **Lorwyn Expert** (comes with Child of the Sun)
- **Shadowmoor Expert** (comes with Shadowmoor Hexer)

### Ravenloft — The Horrors Within

Only the following pieces from this book are allowed.

**Allowed Subclasses**
- **College of Spirits** (Bard)
- **Grave Domain** (Cleric)
- **Hollow Warden** (Ranger)
- **Phantom** (Rogue)
- **Shadow Sorcery** (Sorcerer)

**Allowed Origin Feats**
- **Sharp Eye**
- **Survivor**

**Allowed Backgrounds**
- **Haunted One** (comes with Survivor)
- **Investigator** (comes with Sharp Eye)

**Requires Approval**
- **Hexblood** (Species)
- **Lupin** (Species)
- **Reborn** (Species)
- **Reanimator** (Artificer Subclass)
- **Undead Patron** (Warlock Subclass)

**Banned**
- **Dark Gift** feats, in any form
- **Mist Wanderer** (Background)
- **Spirit Medium** (Background)

### Astarion's Book of Hungers

You may use content from this book. Two options need approval before they go on your sheet:

**Requires Approval**
- **Vampire Devotee** (Background)
- **Dhampir** (Species) — *identical to the Dhampir species in Ravenloft: The Horrors Within; only one version exists on this server*

## Custom Feats

These are feats adapted from older source material for use on this server.

### Gunner

*General Feat — Prerequisites: Level 4+, Dexterity 13+, Proficiency with Firearms*

You gain the following benefits:

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Ignore Loading.** You ignore the Loading property of the Pistol and Musket. If you're holding one of them, you can load a piece of ammunition into it even if you lack a free hand.

**Firing in Melee.** Being within 5 feet of an enemy doesn't impose Disadvantage on your attack rolls with firearms.
