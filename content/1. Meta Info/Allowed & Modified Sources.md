## ✅ Fully Allowed

These books can be used in their entirety with the standard 2024 ruleset — no extra steps needed.

- **Player's Handbook** (2024 Edition)
- **Dungeon Master's Guide** (2024 Edition)
- **Monster Manual** (2024 Edition)

---

## ⚠️ Allowed with Modifications

These sources can be used, but specific parts have been changed or removed to keep things balanced.

### Eberron: Forge of the Artificer

You may use content from this book **except**:
- Feats
- House Backgrounds

### Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn

This source is allowed, with one change:

**Purple Dragon Commandant (Feat)**
- **Modified Prerequisite:** You must have the **Purple Dragon Rook** feat **and** Martial Weapon Proficiency.

## 🔧 Custom Feats

These are feats adapted from older source material for use on this server.

### Gunner

*General Feat — Prerequisites: Level 4+, Dexterity 13+, Proficiency with Firearms*

You gain the following benefits:

**Ability Score Increase.** Increase your Dexterity score by 1, to a maximum of 20.

**Ignore Loading.** You ignore the Loading property of the Pistol and Musket. If you're holding one of them, you can load a piece of ammunition into it even if you lack a free hand.

**Firing in Melee.** Being within 5 feet of an enemy doesn't impose Disadvantage on your attack rolls with firearms.
