# Peoples of Eryndor by Homeland

---

## Flavor

All approved playable species are marked with **(Playable)**. Any species not marked as such are **not playable** for the Champions Guild.

Flavor is free. Players may cosmetically describe their character as another species, lineage, or cultural variant so long as the approved mechanics remain unchanged.

> **Example:** A player may choose **Dragonborn (Playable)** mechanics and flavor the character as a kobold. Mechanically, the character is still a Dragonborn and uses all Dragonborn rules.

This allows creative character concepts while maintaining clear mechanical standards, balance, and consistency across the Guild.

---

## Location

Any species may originate from any nation, kingdom, or city-state in Eryndor.

Homeland listings represent the **most culturally associated or majority population origin**, not a restriction on character creation.

> **Example:** Contramara, the capital of Kastalshire, is only about **70% human**, with the remaining population made up of many other peoples from across the world.

Eryndor is a connected world of trade, war, migration, education, mercenary service, and opportunity. Species heritage and place of origin do not have to match.
