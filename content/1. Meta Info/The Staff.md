## Josh
**Founder & Guild Master**
*Founded the guild and oversees the whole community — setting direction, keeping the world moving, and making sure games keep happening.*

As Guild Master, Josh oversees the community and keeps the adventures rolling. He draws inspiration from The Witcher and The Elder Scrolls, but The Lord of the Rings is his absolute favorite — that series, along with Harry Potter, is what first ignited his love for fantasy and still shapes the stories he tells today.

---

## Ryan
**Lore Master**
*Maintains and develops the lore of Eryndor, keeping the world consistent across DMs and the source of truth for how things fit together.*

Ryan has been playing since 2020 and DMing for two years, and serves as the guild's Lore Master — a role he genuinely enjoys, so don't hesitate to message him on Discord with questions. He pulls inspiration from just about everywhere: books, TV, movies, comics, anime, and the people around him. Really, anything with a good story.

---

## Eda
**Community Keeper**
*Looks after the members and the day-to-day feel of the community*

Eda has been part of the guild for a couple of years, having gotten her start with D&D right here on the server. Off-screen she's a pediatric nurse — well-versed in handling crazy kids — and she swears you can't out-weird her. She tends to play clerics and bards, loving the work of making everyone else look awesome and building those great party bonds; she's deep into role-play and community storytelling but never says no to a solid dungeon crawl. Laid-back, chatty, and always up for some unpredictability, she's usually around the chats or helping new players, so reach out anytime. Her inspirations run from classic literature like Frankenstein and Jane Austen to The Lord of the Rings, plus modern media and the rich folklore of Scotland, Wales, and England.
