# Thalenmark Empire — Government, Military, Economy & Culture
*Halflings of Eryndor*

---

## Government

The Halfling Mercantile Empire is governed by the **Gilded Council**, a body of the **twenty wealthiest Halflings in the world**. In this plutocracy, power is entirely economically based. Entry requires immense wealth, control of major trade arteries or institutions, and the support of at least half the existing members. There are no term limits — Councilors fall only through bankruptcy, assassination-by-proxy, or financial ruin.

A ceremonial **Empress and Royal Family** exist purely for tradition, pageantry, and diplomacy. They hold no true authority.

**Political reality:** Laws are written as contracts. Votes are negotiated, bought, or deferred. Enforcement is outsourced to mercenaries, banks, and private security. The Council almost never acts unanimously — power flows through shifting coalitions.

**Factions within the Council:**
- **Lords of Profit** — Cutthroat monopolists and economic aggressors. Favor market domination, hostile takeovers, and debt leverage.
- **Steady Ledger** — Long-term planners focused on infrastructure and national stability. Often act as mediators — but never altruistically.
- **Free Trade Faction** — Advocates for open markets and smaller traders, though their supposed altruism is frequently questioned.

## Military

The Halfling Empire has a small standing army but massive potential through reserves of caravan guards, mercenaries, and sailors. The empire prefers **economic strangulation** over open warfare.

- **Navy** — Largest fleet in Eryndor, focused on merchant escort and anti-piracy efforts — which at times results in exponentially more piracy.
- **Sky Riders** — Aerial cavalry on griffons, giant hawks, and pteranodons, deployed with rapid precision.
- **Route Runners** — Elite halfling cavalry mounted on giant flightless birds. Exceptional scouts and sharpshooters with hand crossbows and swords.

---

## Economy

The Halflings control the majority of Eryndor's trade. They serve as brokers, transporters, and financiers. All major trade routes pass through [[Talvereth]] sooner or later. The empire boasts the largest lending institution in the world — funding everything from kingdoms to commoners — plus insurance, escrow, and debt enforcement networks.

Debts are sacred. Debtors are found. Defaults are punished. **Debts *will* be paid.**

---

## Fragment Access

The Thalenmark Empire owns **two shard mines**, operated by halfling overseers and accountants — but much of the manual labor is done by displaced human and dwarf miners. Production is above average and may increase further with magitech advancements, if they can be secured from the gnomes.

## Culture

Halfling culture revolves around **commerce, contracts, and coin**.

- **City Halflings** — Wealth-focused, brightly dressed, obsessed with honor-bound contracts.
- **Rural Halflings** — Reputation-focused, production-driven, family-oriented.
- **Caravan Halflings** — Product-obsessed and fiercely loyal to their wares — many would die before losing their goods.

**Major Institutions:**
- **The Aviary (Sky Riders Academy)** — Trains halfling aerial cavalry and their mounts; teaches mount care and air superiority tactics.
- **Hearthlight University** — Specializes in Divination and Abjuration magic; also teaches engineering, logistics, and mathematics. Funded by the Gilded Council; graduates owe 10 years of public service.

Beyond Talvereth, the empire is composed of dozens of small towns and Sky Rider barracks. These towns pride themselves on their inns and warehouses, frequently serving as rest stops for caravans.

## Religion

Halfling religion is divided between two powerful faiths:

- **Yondalla** — Goddess of community, fertility, and home. Dominates rural areas where halflings value kinship, productivity, and tradition.
- **Waukeen** — Goddess of wealth and trade. Reigns in urban centers like [[Talvereth]], where success is measured in ledgers, not legacy.

This spiritual divide has caused rising tension. The rural faithful claim Waukeen's worship has corrupted the halfling soul; Waukeenite priests accuse the Yondallan sects of resisting progress. A conflict in [[Cloverfold]] — where a Waukeenite-funded bank attempted to seize ancestral tea-farming land — turned violent, resulting in three deaths and a burned Waukeenite temple. The Gilded Council refused to take sides.

The divide is no longer subtle. It was a major spark of [[The Talvereth Crisis]], and religious tensions remain unresolved. Clerics traveling outside their home region are often shadowed or harassed.

## Mercenaries & Criminal Organizations

### The Bronze Talons
*Base: Talvereth*

Elite mercenaries handling security, debt collection, and contract enforcement. Founded by injured or rogue Sky Riders and Route Runners.

- **Leader:** [[Deyla]], a red-haired halfling with a pragmatic leadership style and a Pteranodon named Swiftwing.

### The Velvet Ledger

A secretive financial syndicate of brokers, saboteurs, and assassins. Believed to control several Gilded Council members. Rarely acts directly. The farthest-reaching criminal organization in all of Eryndor — its outposts can be found in every city. If they are crossed, they can find their targets anywhere.

- **Leader:** [[Selrik]], a masked figure who always appears in gold. Employs blackmail, insider trading, and market manipulation.

> *"If you need force, hire the Bronze Talons.*
> *If you want results no one can trace, you balance the Velvet Ledger."*

The Velvet Ledger was a driving force behind [[The Talvereth Crisis]] and **remains active within the city**.

## Old Blood

**Trait: Luck of the Old Blood**

Halflings marked by the Old Blood bear coin-like circles around their eyes and temples. Anyone with a marked ancestor within three generations may manifest it.

> **Level 7 Power: Boundless Fortune** — Once per long rest, impose disadvantage on an attack roll made against you or an ally within 30 feet before the roll is made.
> **Level 7 Drawback: Fraying Luck** — Your next attack roll, saving throw, or ability check is made with disadvantage.

Some halfling families consider these markings sacred; others see them as dangerous temptations. The Gilded Council neither endorses nor opposes them — though rumors persist that several Council members are Old Blood descendants.

## Grudges & Political Strain

- **Bronze Talons / Velvet Ledger:** The Talons have occasionally worked for the Ledger. Only their strict contracts prevent open hostility. Rumors persist of one high-ranking Talon captain accepting an undisclosed favor from Selrik.
- **Urban vs. Rural:** A growing cultural rift divides urban halflings (coin-driven, Waukeen-worshipping, progressive) and rural halflings (community-centered, Yondalla-devout, tradition-bound). It affects legislation, Council voting blocks, and trade tariffs on goods from villages like [[Cloverfold]].
- **Displaced Peoples:** Resentment simmers among human and dwarven populations displaced by halfling trade dominance. Underground groups circulate leaflets calling for boycotts of halfling goods.
- **Gnomish Rails:** The gnomish rail systems pose a rising economic threat. Some Council factions want partnership; others see the gnomes as existential competitors. Rumors blame recent gnomish "industrial accidents" on halfling-funded sabotage.
- **Goblinoid Raids:** Goblinoid factions regularly raid outlying trade routes. Sky Rider retaliation has burned several goblin settlements, escalating tensions.
- **Internal Council Friction:** A proposed tax exemption for small traders sparked walkouts, bribes, and a duel by proxy on the Grand Exchange floor between the Free Trade Faction and Lords of Profit.
- **Tax Resistance:** Several towns hoard wealth locally, resisting collection demands from Talvereth tax agents. Mercenaries have been dispatched, fueling further rural dissent.
- **The Prophetess's Legacy:** The Yondallan prophetess [[Bruna Mossvale]] preached on the outskirts of Talvereth that the "Age of Greed" would bring ruin without repentance. Her movement was a key spark of the Crisis; she died during it, and her followers remain — some calling her a martyr.
