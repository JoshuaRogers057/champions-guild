# The Gilded Council

The ruling body of the [[Thalenmark Empire]] — the twenty wealthiest halflings in the world. Following [[The Talvereth Crisis]], only **eight members survive**. Twelve seats sit vacant, and the maneuvering to fill them has already begun.

*Velvet Ledger ties and corruption status are insider knowledge (DM-facing) — not public record.*

## Power Vacuum — Hooks

- **Faction balance shattered:** Lords of Profit lost 4 of 6 seats; Free Trade lost 4 of 7; Steady Ledger lost 4 of 7. Every faction is scrambling to fill vacancies with loyalists.
- **Bronze Talons oversight is gone:** Corin Hearthwhistle handled Bronze Talons licensing and procurement. No one currently oversees the empire's chief enforcers.
- **Credit markets destabilized:** Goldbrook & Co. is leaderless; claimants compete openly for the lending empire.
- **[[The Velvet Ledger]]'s grip:** Three of eight survivors have confirmed Ledger ties. The syndicate's relative influence over the Council has *grown*.
