# Verdant Bay

Verdant Bay is a hidden mountainous inlet near **Drakhal'Rath**, tucked between steep blackstone cliffs and thick coastal growth. Smugglers slip in under fog, unload beneath overhanging stone, and disappear through narrow goat paths and old volcanic tunnels before patrols ever see a sail.

---

## Head of Port: Salla

A green-scaled kobold broker with sea-wet boots, bright eyes, and a habit of knowing which patrols are late before the patrols do.
