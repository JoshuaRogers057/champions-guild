# Frostbreath Point

Frostbreath Point is a secret dock hidden in the frozen forests north of **[[Azurath]]**, where the trees lean heavy with ice and the shoreline disappears under fog and snow.

---

## Head of Port: Nixra Coldhook

A white-scaled kobold smuggler wrapped in seal fur and old military canvas. She is small, cheerful, and completely willing to leave enemies tied to trees until the cold finishes the argument.
