# Viridius Grove

Viridius Grove is named for an ancient green dragon said to have once claimed the surrounding forest as its domain. The druids here protect those old woods with quiet cruelty, specializing in poisons, venomous plants, concealed paths, and natural warding. They are less concerned with beauty than control.

---

## Thornspeaker: Thornspeaker Velratha Virn

A green-scaled dragonborn druid with dark robes, vine-wrapped horns, and a voice that never rises above a whisper. She believes the forest should warn intruders once, then punish them forever.
