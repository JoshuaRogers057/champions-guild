# Sequestered Solitude

Sequestered Solitude is a remote bastion founded by an old war party that had seen too much death. It became a retreat for soldiers whose wars followed them home — a quiet place where veterans can live, work, sleep, train, or simply sit in silence without being asked to explain themselves.

Those who arrive respectfully are treated with surprising hospitality. Those who arrive loudly are reminded that peace is easier to keep when everyone inside was once very good at war.

---

## Stillwarden: Stillwarden Othrak Veyl

A gray-scaled dragonborn veteran with one missing horn and a voice that rarely rises above a murmur. He does not hate people. He just believes some wounds grow worse when the world keeps shouting.
