# Brotherhood of the Skies

The Brotherhood of the Skies is a hidden kobold monastery carved into a desert mesa, home to elite wyrmling riders who patrol the roads, trade paths, and borderlands of the Dominion from above. Each rider bonds with a young dragon, training for aerial scouting, messenger work, rescue flights, and sudden strikes.

---

## Talonwarden: Talonwarden Ikkit Vaash

A lean kobold rider with wind-worn scales, bright yellow eyes, and a bronze wyrmling named Kreeva. He trusts dragons faster than people and considers that common sense.
