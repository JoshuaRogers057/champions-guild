# Elkhara

Elkhara is a quarantined region overrun by mutated creatures, from which artificers were rescued. A Drakmir garrison held a barricade here, and Shavar was killed at the artificers' laboratory. The Nest was destroyed and Fenalith killed, ending the mutation crisis.
