# Icewind Grove

Icewind Grove lies along the Dominion's northern coastline beside the **Ice Sea**, where towering pines bend under frozen wind and salt frost clings to the bark. These druids tend the cold forests, guide winter travelers, and keep ice wolves as companions, scouts, and guardians.

---

## Frostroot: Frostroot Kavax Thenn

A white-scaled kobold druid wrapped in pine-fur and bone charms. He is small, sharp-eyed, and usually surrounded by wolves that listen better than most soldiers.
