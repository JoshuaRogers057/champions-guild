# Skraggapost

Skraggapost is a goblinoid village on the edge of the nation, and a task concerning it was agreed. It was named as the site of [[Aethrael]]'s planned rift, and its whole population was teleported to the Guild hall in [[Nation of Lexovar|Lexovar]].
