# Tarrin's Tankard

Tarrin's Tankard was a derelict tavern on Silvershade Lane, haunted by a ghost named Tarrin, and its deed was signed to Zed, Kairis and Thad. It was repaired after the lumber job and prepared for opening with a manager hired, but its grand opening was interrupted by an explosion in the street outside. Zafan and Thad were arrested at the Tankard. The tavern was later destroyed in the blast during the Talvereth crisis.
