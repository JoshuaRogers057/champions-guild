# Stonegate Hold

Stonegate Hold is a dwarven fort that the caravan reached after the ambush on the road through Lexaror. Its patrol was missing below, its trade ledger and pass-seal were stolen, and someone within was helping the Rok Rot smugglers use the old lower tunnels.
