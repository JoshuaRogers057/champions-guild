# The Floating Isles

Ruins on the Floating Isles carry the mark of Cyric. Researchers of the Arcane Ward were studying them, and the Smiles Gang followed. A temple there is a trial for those seeking rebirth, guarded by a fallen angel set to watch as punishment.
