# Oathend

Oathend was the seat of the [[Veilkeepers of Oathend]], and the Ossuary Mandate was delivered here. It fell to undead and a green mist and was declared lost, though a dome expanding from the vault beneath its manor was halted. [[Dr. Vathros]] later offered to dismantle the Oathend dome as part of his bargain.
