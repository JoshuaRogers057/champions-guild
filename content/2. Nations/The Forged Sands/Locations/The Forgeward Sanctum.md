# The Forgeward Sanctum

The Forgeward Sanctum is built in the shadow of a great **Creation Forge**, where the Steelborn treat the Forge not merely as a machine, but as the sacred source of life, memory, and purpose. Its monks worship the Forge through maintenance, silence, vigilance, and ritual service.

The **Forgewardens** serve alongside the official Forge Guard, but their role is more spiritual and obsessive. The Forge Guard defends by command; the Forgewardens defend by devotion.

---

## Forgeward Elder: Threxus

An ancient Steelborn with a bronze-black frame, heat-scarred plating, and a chest core that glows like banked coals. Their voice carries the low hum of a working furnace. Threxus believes the Forge is not owned by the Steelborn. The Steelborn are owned by the Forge.
