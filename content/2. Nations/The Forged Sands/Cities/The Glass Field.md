# The Glass Field

The Glass Field serves as both gate and gauntlet to the Steelborn homeland — a vast, gleaming expanse of superheated sand transformed into razor-sharp glass. At its heart stands the **Apex Ember**, a towering 2,500-foot obsidian-and-silver monolith whose core births new Steelborn into the world.

**Defenses:** Heat Field (radiated heat melts gear in under a day), Glass Blade Terrain (razor-sharp glass dangerous for invading armies).

---

## Leadership

**Prime Bastion Axion-7** — Supreme commander. Encased in scorched blacksteel armor. Has not left the city in two centuries. Revered as the "Living Directive."

---

## Districts

**The Prism Ward** — Crystalline administrative hub at the base of the Apex Ember. Logic tribunals and memory-route debates.
**The Ember Array** — Defense perimeter of modular steel towers, firelance turrets, and arcane cannons.
**The Crucible Steps** — Trial Grounds for all newly forged Steelborn carved into the cliffside.
**Echo Market** — Suspended platforms over glass. Tinkerers, salvagers, scrap traders, and foreign merchants. Overseen by **Tradecore Glintcipher**.
**The Melted Fields** — Jagged ridges, mirrored plains, and semi-molten fissures. No siege has ever lasted here.
