# Red Harbor

Red Harbor is the maritime lifeline of the Steelborn nation — the only city where non-Steelborn can dwell safely for extended periods. Built into rust-colored cliffs and cooled lava shelves. At the center lies the fractured **Embermoor Core** — a broken relic now powering long-range communication arrays and arcane signal towers.

**Defenses:** Great Chain (blocks harbor access), Great Cannon (largest cannon in the world, situated in the crumbling remains of the Creation Forge — no non-Steelborn has ever seen the true extent of its range).

---

## Leadership

**Ironwake** — Slender, long-limbed, built for stealth and adaptability. Rumored to have survived over fifty trips to the ocean floor, walking home each time.

---

## Districts

**The Embermoor Vault** — Once a full Creation Forge, now shattered. Engineers harness its leaking resonance. Patrolled by Codebound sentinels.
**Ironwake Docks** — Reinforced docking platforms supporting warships to experimental submersibles. Overseen by **Helmsage [[Quenforge]]**.
**The Alloy Expanse** — Residential and diplomatic sector for non-Steelborn. Houses embassies and neutral negotiation halls.
**The Signal Crucible** — Communication relays and echo towers. Overseen by **Signal Warden Hexbyte**.
**Shattering Canal** — Jagged trench turned molten waste sluice and emergency defense.
