# The Forged Sands — Government, Military, Economy & Culture
*Steelborn (Warforged) of Eryndor*

---

## Government

**The Council of Echoes** — A gathering of the oldest Steelborn still functioning since the dark times. Each city appoints two representatives based on longevity, experience, and harmony with the Group Mind of the Council. Decisions are made through near-silent mental debate, with public sessions held only for ceremonial pronouncements. Questioning the will of the Council of Echoes is simply not done. They maintain constant mental communication even over vast distances.

---

## Military

All Steelborn operate in **Nodes** — squads of 6 to 10 members linked by a Group Mind sharing limited telepathic communication and heightened coordination.

**Tactics:** Swift precision strikes, minimal vocalization, overwhelming focus on single targets before redirection. Use of terrain, ambushes, and mechanical siege engines.

**The Forge Guard** — Elite unit tasked with defending the Creation Forges. They do not ask questions. They do not take captives.

---

## Economy

Nearly self-contained, centered on mechanical goods, magitech, and repairs. Trade limited to acquiring Fragments, spell components, and arcane reagents. Resource allocation is need-based internally; strict value-based trade with outsiders. **Red Harbor** is the primary trade gateway, exporting arcane hardware and importing rare reagents.

---

## Fragment Access

The heart of each Steelborn is a specialized fragment — for them it is life. The forging of new Steelborn is a sacred process done at the **Creation Forges**. Fallen Steelborn are revered and their bodies recycled for new Steelborn. The theft or destruction of fragments from the fallen is a capital crime that has always resulted in brutal retaliation.

Red Harbor's **Embermoor Core** is a fractured non-functional Creation Forge, repurposed to fuel long-range arcane communication and surveillance.

---

## Culture

**Group Mind:** A psychic link enabling coordination and emotional stability within approx. 100 ft. Any Steelborn may drop or be added to any given Group Mind, though some have greater synergy. Not all Steelborn enjoy or tolerate the Group Mind — those often seek a living in the wider world.

**Isolationism:** Outsiders are tolerated but monitored. Visitors must follow local laws and withstand the harsh climate.

**Emotional Pragmatism:** Emotions are acknowledged but secondary to logic. Emotional health is maintained for functional stability.

**Creation and Renewal:** The forging of new Steelborn is sacred.

---

## Religion

Most Steelborn revere **The First Spark** — a philosophy about the origin of consciousness. Some worship traditional gods of craft, order, or knowledge in private.

---

## Mercenaries & Criminal Organizations

### The Steel Sails
*Headquarters: Red Harbor | Leader: Admiral Steelwake*

A fleet of steel-hulled ships crewed by a variety of sailors. Designed for unrelenting presence, brute resilience, and overwhelming boarding tactics. Contracts are engraved in plates on the mast for all to see. They never go back on a contract. Refused smuggling or slaving contracts — considered "civic dishonor."

**Admiral Steelwake** — Became legendary after surviving the Breathless Tide incident, walking along the ocean floor after being thrown from his vessel during a kraken attack. He rebuilt himself and founded the Steel Sails.

### The Forgebound Legion
*Headquarters: The Glass Field | Leader: Prime Marshal*

The Steelborn mercenary hammer. They excel at siege warfare, battlefield fortification, and contract-bound enforcement. Once deployed, they don't retreat. If betrayed, **Clause Zero Protocols** activate: immediate retaliation with overwhelming force. No mercy. No delay.

**Prime Marshal** — One of the oldest operational Steelborn in Eryndor. Originally a siege-engine commander, lost during a forgotten contract, rediscovered and awakened in 980. Has led the Forgebound Legion ever since.

---

## Grudges & Political Strain

**Isolationists:** Strong borders, limited embassies, no immigration.
**Neutrality:** No formal alliances or treaties.
**Major Political Rift:** An incident with the Gnomes nearly resulted in war roughly forty years ago. Since then, the Steelborn and the Gnomes have no diplomatic relationship in any form.
