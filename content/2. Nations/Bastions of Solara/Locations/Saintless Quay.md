# Saintless Quay

Saintless Quay sits below the cliffs near **[[Empyrean Hold]]**, where supply lifts, military docks, and sea caves create blind spots even the Skyborne cannot fully watch.

Everyone who works the Quay understands the risk: if Solara catches them, there will be no trial worth remembering.

---

## Head of Port: Jaska Brightscar

A hard-eyed human smuggler with radiant burn marks across her face and a habit of smiling only when things get worse. She knows patrol schedules, cliff currents, and which soldiers can be bribed before they remember their virtue. She is charming when paid and merciless when crossed.
