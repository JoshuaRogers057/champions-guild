# The Order of the First Dawn

The Order of the First Dawn arrives after the others have acted. They heal survivors, rebuild towns, cleanse battlefields, bury the dead, feed orphans, restore temples, and teach the people that Solara's judgment was necessary. They are genuinely compassionate, but they also serve a political purpose: they make the wound look holy after the knife has done its work.

---

## Dawnmaster: Dawnmaster Halvek Sorn

A scarred aasimar veteran with cropped white hair, gold eyes, and a calm voice that rarely rises. He is gentle with victims and merciless toward anything that would make new ones.
