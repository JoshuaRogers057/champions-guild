# The Golden Furrow

The Golden Furrow protects Solara's farms, orchards, grain roads, and harvest villages. Its druids bless seed, guard against famine, punish crop-burning, and oversee sacred fields that feed temples, soldiers, and the poor. They are beloved by common folk because their miracles usually look like full bellies.

---

## Harvest-Mother: Harvest-Mother Calithra Wain

An older aasimar woman with strong hands, copper-gold eyes, and robes embroidered with wheat and sunflowers. She is practical, stern, and deeply maternal. She does not care how noble someone is if they waste food while children go hungry.
