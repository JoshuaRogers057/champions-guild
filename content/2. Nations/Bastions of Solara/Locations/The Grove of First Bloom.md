# The Grove of First Bloom

The Grove of First Bloom is devoted to renewal, healing, and restored life. Its druids are called after fires, plagues, battles, undead attacks, and cursed blights to bring ruined land back from death. They grow dawnblossoms, goldenroot, and radiant fruit used in healing rites and cleansing ceremonies.

---

## Dawnroot Keeper: Dawnroot Keeper Vossal (Aurelian Voss)

A warm but weary aasimar druid with gold-flecked skin, sun-pale hair, and hands permanently stained with soil. He believes no victory is complete until something living grows where blood was spilled.
