# The Hollow Halo

The Hollow Halo is hidden beneath the lower terraces of **[[Auris Prime]]**, buried among forgotten service tunnels, old pilgrimage cisterns, and abandoned shrine foundations.

The smugglers here survive by dressing crime in holiness: prayer carts, funeral processions, charity wagons, and temple supply crates all hide cargo.

---

## Head of Port: Mercy-of-Ash

A masked aasimar woman with burned wings, gray robes, and a voice trained to sound like a grieving priestess. She is calm, careful, and terrifyingly patient. She does not think of herself as a criminal. She thinks of herself as the person people find when Solara's mercy runs out.
