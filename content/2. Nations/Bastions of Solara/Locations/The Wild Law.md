# The Wild Law

The Wild Law is Solara's harshest grove, devoted to the old balance of beasts, forests, storms, hunger, and survival. Its druids believe nature is not kind, but it is just in ways civilization forgets. They judge poachers, blighters, careless miners, and nobles who think land exists only to be owned.

---

## Thornfather: Thornfather Odrien Vaulk

A severe aasimar druid with bark-dark armor, antler scars across his shoulders, and gold eyes that rarely blink. He does not speak of nature as peaceful. He speaks of it as older than law, and far less patient.
