# The Chain of the Red Vow

The Chain of the Red Vow is Solara's closed monastery of obedience, sacrifice, and righteous force. Once the Tribunal passes sentence, the Chain acts. Its members arrest nobles, break rebellions, seize estates, purge cults, execute traitors, and enforce divine law when ordinary soldiers are too weak, compromised, or afraid.

---

## Martyr-Captain: Martyr-Captain Ordan Thricebound

A broad-shouldered aasimar with burn scars across his chest and three broken oath-chains wrapped around one arm. He believes the highest form of faith is obedience when obedience costs everything.
