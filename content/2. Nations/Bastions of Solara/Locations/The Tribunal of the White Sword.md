# The Tribunal of the White Sword

The Tribunal of the White Sword is Solara's closed monastery of divine judgment, sacred law, and formal condemnation. Its members do not investigate. They receive the evidence, weigh the soul, name the crime, and declare the sentence. Their rulings are treated as holy law.

---

## High Justicar: High Justicar Vaelren Oathmark

A stern aasimar with gold-white eyes, a perfectly trimmed beard, and a voice that makes excuses sound embarrassing. He believes mercy without judgment is cowardice dressed as virtue.
