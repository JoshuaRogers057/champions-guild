# The Stillwater Mercy

The Stillwater Mercy is a sanctuary grove built around quiet springs, pale pools, and willow-shaded paths where violence is forbidden. Its druids shelter refugees, cleanse trauma, heal the wounded, and offer peace to those broken by war or judgment. Even Solaran soldiers lower their weapons before entering.

---

## Keeper: Keeper Naevra Softfall

A soft-spoken aasimar druid with silver-blue eyes, bare feet, and a voice that calms rooms before people notice. She is gentle, but not weak. Anyone who brings violence into her sanctuary is removed without anger and without mercy.
