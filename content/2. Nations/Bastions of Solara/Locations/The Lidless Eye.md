# The Lidless Eye

The Lidless Eye is Solara's closed inquisitorial monastery, feared for uncovering corruption, heresy, treason, fiendish influence, and hidden disloyalty. Its members are confessors, investigators, spy-priests, and holy interrogators who believe vigilance means never looking away. Admission is by invitation only.

---

## Inquisitor-Mother: Inquisitor-Mother Savaeryn Thrice-Veiled

A pale aasimar woman with silver eyes, white gloves, and a voice soft enough to make silence feel dangerous. She believes truth is not discovered. It is extracted.
