# The Starforges

The Starforges are the sacred anvil of Solara. A mountain settlement blessed and cursed with constant heat, where the most potent weapons of the Bastions are born. Nestled in the elemental rift valley known as the Godscar Hollow, the settlement pulses with radiant energy powered by molten veins and consecrated flame.

This is no mere smithing town. It is a temple to divine warfare. Every item is more than a tool — it is an extension of the Solar Creed. Blades are etched with vows, shields with prayers, and armor with radiant oaths. The craftsfolk here are known as **Sunbinders** — a caste of sacred artisans who blend magic, metallurgy, and religious duty into each masterpiece. The town is fiercely guarded. The secrets of star-metal alloying, sunfire tempering, and soul-binding enchantments are treated as state-level mysteries.

*"We do not craft weapons. We awaken them."*

---

## Leadership

**Forge-Canon Helvar Rimebraze** — A battle-scarred veteran turned master artificer-priest. Once a frontline commander for the Dawnward Legions, he lost his left arm in a border war and had it replaced with a radiant-forged limb powered by divine flame. His leadership is absolute; his forge rituals mix military cadence with scripture. Answers only to the Highlord of Valor. His temper is legendary. So is his loyalty.

## Districts

### The Crucible Chain
A ring of forge halls, each focused on a different material or divine source. Includes the **Forge of the Ninth Ember** (star-metal), the **Solvault Crucible** (radiant armor), and the **Cinder Choir Furnace** (chant-based weapon shaping). Smith-priests perform their work to hymns that both harden steel and burn heresy.

### Emberhold
The main residential quarter for Sunbinders and their kin. Blackstone homes sealed against the heat. Cooling fountains enchanted to shed divine light like mist.

### The Anointing Vaults
Underground sanctuaries where newly forged relics are consecrated. Not open to the public — those who enter without permission are branded or disappear.

### Solmarch Landing
A small, fortified docking plateau for bringing in refined ore and carrying out finished arms. Managed by the **Flamegild Guard**. Also serves as the only entrance for outsiders — and a checkpoint thick with divine wards.
