# Solara Bastion

Solara Bastion is the gleaming spear-tip of the [[Bastions of Solara]] — the first line of defense at the edge of the sacred corridor to the west. Carved into the cliffs like a god's throne, the fortress looms with the weight of holy war, casting long shadows over those who would challenge Solara's might.

From the outside, it is blindingly beautiful: gold-veined stonework, radiant banners that shimmer with heatless flame, and statues of winged warriors with blades drawn skyward. But within those polished walls lies a brutal truth — the city is one enormous training ground, a crucible for zealotry and control. Soldiers are sculpted here, not just trained. Righteousness is forged like iron, bent until it obeys.

Obedience is praised. Dissent is cleansed. And glory, as always in Solara, is best earned when someone is watching.

---

## Leadership

**Commander-Militant Vaeron Solmarch** — Clad in radiant steel and crowned with a halo of blessed fire. A battlefield legend and political beast — capable of rallying troops with scripture and executing deserters with the same hand. Behind his eyes flickers a long history of "necessary violence." Reports to High Marshal Thessira.

**High Marshal Thessira Dawnrend ("The Dawnstorm")** — Supreme General of the Sunblade Legions. A brilliant strategist and merciless reformer who purged half the officer corps during the last "Solar Reassessment." Rumored to be next in line for Highlord. Her loyalty is not to any single Highlord — but to the Legions themselves.

## Districts

### The Emberwatch Walls
The outer defense ring — multi-layered walls lined with archways, burning sigils, and sunsteel ballistae. Torchlight never dims. Patrols rotate in unbroken cycles. Prisoners held in the **Vault of Reclamation**, where they are "redeemed" through servitude.

### Dawnblood Ward
Central plaza and command district. Contains the **Hall of Radiant Oaths** (military command center). A public execution square called the **Sunmirror** reflects midday light into the eyes of those condemned until the heat and radiant light extinguishes them.

### The Crucible Path
The military academy and training district. Home to the **Sanctum of Flamebound Discipline**. Cadets train in radiant arms, tactics, mounted warfare, and obedience doctrine. Graduates take the **Oath of Resolve**, binding their service to light… and law.

### Hearth of Brass
Industrial district of divine forges and armorworks. Crafts magical armor, sunblades, war standards, and radiant siege engines.

### The Shadeground *(Unofficial)*
Beneath the city's lowest level — a tangle of tunnels and hollowed barracks once used in the early wars. Now half-forgotten, housing drifters, failed recruits, and soldiers with injuries too political to treat. Rumors whisper of the **Sunbroken** — disillusioned legionnaires wielding dark corruptions of sunblades known as Nightfangs.
