# Bastions of Solara — Government, Military, Economy & Culture
*Aasimar of Eryndor*

---

## Government

**Ruling Body:** The **Triumvirate of Radiance** — three Aasimar Highlords representing the celestial virtues:

- **Highlord of Valor** (Commander of the Legions) — Oversees military defense and foreign affairs.
- **Highlord of Wisdom** (Keeper of Sacred Laws) — Heads the judicial system, ensures balance among cities and Highlords, and maintains equality of virtue.
- **Highlord of Mercy** (Arbiter of Peace) — Oversees civil service and ensures citizens' well-being through acts of charity, justice, and virtue.

**The Divine Judges:** Guided by the Highlord of Wisdom, these scholars draft laws, treaties, and pacts. Often accompanied by Sunblade Legionnaires to prevent or end conflicts that go against the interests of the nation.

**The Luminous Council:** A group of clerics, sages, and paladins who advise the Triumvirate. Elders in an advisory position only — they have no official governing power.

**The Everbright Accords:** A sacred oath binding all Aasimar to service — military, judicial, or civil. Most willingly uphold this sacred duty. When an individual is problematic to the state, the Accords are most often the legal precedent under which they are tried.

---

## Military

Bastions of Solara maintain a large standing army and constantly repel raiders from the Redmarch Coalition. They secure the only land bridge connecting east and west.

- **Sunblade Legions** — Elite warriors clad in golden armor, wielding radiant-infused weapons.
- **Skyborne Cavalry** — Pegasus-mounted Valkyrie Aasimar serving as aerial strike forces and scouts.
- **Heavenly Guardians** — Celestial constructs summoned in times of dire need — manifestations of divine power.
- **Wardens of Light** — Inquisitors who hunt fiends, heretics, and corruption with unwavering discipline.
- **Dawnstrikers** — A teleporting rapid-response unit, capable of appearing anywhere in the Bastions to neutralize threats before they spread.

## Economy

The economy of the Bastions is not built upon gold-hungry merchant lords or agricultural expansion, but upon divine service, spiritual authority, and absolute control over the continent's most vital trade corridor. The Aasimar do not seek profit in the traditional sense — they demand tribute.

**Divine Arbitration:** As neutral enforcers of divine law, the Aasimar regularly host and resolve disputes between kingdoms, guilds, and noble houses. All who petition the Triumvirate must pay — whether in coin, relics, oaths of fealty, or future obligations. Tribunals at the Hall of Celestials often result in both economic judgments and spiritual penance.

**Radiant Weapon Exports:** The Starforges produce radiant-forged weapons unlike any crafted elsewhere in Eryndor. Surplus weapons, armor, and warding artifacts are sold to foreign religious orders or high-paying paladins of lawful alignment. The export is tightly regulated and prices are extreme.

**Trade Tariffs and Control of the Sacred Pass:** The Bastions sit directly atop the Sacred Pass — the only stable overland route connecting eastern and western Eryndor. All goods are taxed at 5–15%. Fragments are taxed up to 40% due to their magical volatility. Smuggling or evading tariffs is considered a divine crime. This control effectively makes the Aasimar the tollkeepers of civilization, earning both reverence and resentment.

---

## Fragment Access

The fragment mines controlled by the Bastions are sacred, heavily fortified, and fiercely regulated — treated as hallowed vaults guarded by paladins and celestial wards. All mining operations are overseen by the **Order of the Dawn Tithe**, a guild of clerics, paladins, and divine architects. Miners are more akin to acolytes than laborers, offering daily prayers before digging and ritually cleansing each fragment removed.

## Culture

**Guardians of Peace:** Aasimar of Auris act as divine arbitrators, intervening to prevent war between mortal nations. The nation projects itself as divine peacekeepers. This carefully cultivated image is cherished and protected.

**Oathbound Honor:** Every citizen is raised to know that their people alone value justice, mercy, and righteousness above all. The reality is that like every nation, good and evil intermix in abundance.

**Sacred Laws:** Law is divine and absolute. Publicly there is no tolerance for corruption, dishonesty, or betrayal. Realistically bribes are a problem anywhere money is involved.

**The Luminous Trials:** Spiritual and physical tests of virtue, required for knighthood or leadership. Success grants the right to serve in military or judicial office.

**Cultural Tone:**
- *Public Behavior:* Controlled, ceremonial, reverent. Every act is a performance of grace and loyalty.
- *Private Truths:* Corruption is hidden behind veils of holiness. Power pays well.
- *Religion:* The Solar Creed is not just a faith — it is law. Officially benevolent. Quietly brutal.

Auris Prime is light with little warmth, law with sparse justice, and order with conditional mercy. Yet from its mountaintop throne, it shines on — untouched, unchallenged, and very carefully admired.

---

## Religion

Most Aasimar worship **Tyr** (God of Justice). Domain: Order, War. Tyr's unflinching commitment to justice, law, and righteousness reflects the Aasimar drive for divine order.

All Aasimar are nominally held to at least the notion of the **Seven Virtues:**

- **Valor** — Courage in defense of the innocent and righteous cause
- **Wisdom** — The ability to discern truth and uphold balance
- **Mercy** — Compassion shown to those who suffer, tempered with justice
- **Justice** — The divine enforcement of order and moral law
- **Faith** — Loyalty to the divine path, even in darkness
- **Charity** — The giving of self and wealth for the benefit of others
- **Fortitude** — Endurance through pain, fear, and adversity

Much of their adherence is left up to individual perspective.

## Mercenaries & Criminal Organizations

### The Ashwings

An unofficial resistance group operating beneath Auris Prime in the tunnels of the Hollow Steps. They resist Solara's hypocrisy with ink, blade, and stolen fire. Their membership is drawn from the poor, the heretical, and the disillusioned. Their exact numbers and leadership are unknown.

## Old Blood

**Trait: Radiance of the Firstborn**

> **Effect (Level 7):** Once per long rest, when you or an ally within 30 feet takes damage from an enemy, you may use your reaction to emit a burst of radiant light. The attacker must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier) or take 2d10 radiant damage and be blinded until the start of their next turn.
> **Drawback:** After using this ability, you are blinded for the next minute.

**Physical Markings:** Glowing veins of silver or gold beneath the skin, faint motes of light trail behind them, and their voices momentarily echo with celestial resonance.

## Grudges & Political Strain

**Neutral Mediators:** The Bastions of Auris refuse to take sides in mortal disputes, serving only as divine peacekeepers — unless the Triumvirate unanimously agrees.

**Respected but Feared:** Their judgments are final, and their arrival may signal major political upheaval. Trade and troop movement restrictions from east-west travel may spell defeat for those they oppose.

**Enemies of Darkness:** Fiendish forces are the eternal enemy of the Bastions. All incursions are met with holy wrath. The Tieflings in particular have been "cleansed" from most of Eryndor by the Aasimar for little reason other than their species.

## Notable NPCs

### The Triumvirate of Radiance

**Highlord Kaelion Draeven — Lord of Valor**
Towering war hero and commander of the Dawnward Legions. A master strategist who carefully controls conflict optics. Golden plate armor; silver hair.

**Highlord Seris Valrien — Lord of Wisdom**
Keeper of sacred laws and master of bureaucratic nuance. His neutrality is a mask, and his legal system favors adaptability to power.

**Highlady Marcelline Aestra — Lady of Mercy**
Radiant public figure and chief diplomat. Her compassion is leveraged for political gain. Kings know her face.

### Solara Bastion

**Commander-Militant Vaeron Solmarch** — Warpriest and fortress-lord. Ruthless, lawful fury. Delivers both sermons and executions with the same hand.

**High Marshal Thessira Dawnrend ("The Dawnstorm")** — Supreme General of the Sunblade Legions. Rumored to be next in line to become a Highlord.

### The Starforges

**Forge-Canon Helvar Rimebraze** — Veteran artificer-priest and battle commander. Lost his arm in battle, replaced with a radiant-forged divine limb.

### The Hall of Celestials — The Trisphere

**Arbiter Delen Marr (Judgment)** — Cold, razor-eyed judge. Views compromise as weakness. Final rulings known as absolute.

**Arbiter Nyelle of the Folded Flame (Healing)** — Warm, motherly, and terrifying in political manipulation. Uses redemptive penance for political gain. An unmatched healer.

**Arbiter Callien Starhelm (Clarity)** — Scholar-priest resolving matters of faith and arcane crime. Loyal to the Highlords' image.

### The Golden Spire

**Archflame Hierarch Auren Tal'Vec** — Soft-spoken and possibly semi-divine figure. Controls which "divine visions" are accepted. A master manipulator of celestial ritual and spiritual policy.

### Empyrean Hold

**High Skywarden Seraphiel Thorne** — Commander of the Skyborne Cavalry. Obsessed with altitude, precision, and swift punishment. Her authority is near-total on the eastern front.
