Varondale is the easternmost and most perilous of the five duchies. Hemmed in by dark seas to the east and steep, frozen mountain ranges to the west, the region sees constant threats from sea raiders, giants, monstrous wildlife, and occasional cultic forces hiding in the cliffs. Its people do not expect rescue. They prepare, endure, and adapt.

Varondale is dangerous, but not helpless. Its position on the water and its control of deep harbors, shipbuilding, mineral exports, and coastal trade make it more independently wealthy than many outer regions. The duchy is less reliant on direct crown favor than its distance might suggest, which makes its relationship with Contramara especially complicated.

The land is treacherous, cold and craggy, with very little flat land suitable for farming. Communities survive by harvesting the sea and burrowing into the mountains for iron, coal, and more exotic minerals. Magic here tends to focus on preservation, warding, and weather resistance. Architecture in Varondale is fortress-first: thick stone walls, iron-braced shutters, sharply pitched slate roofs, and rooftops designed to anchor netting in case of high winds or flying threats.

**Primary Function:** Fishing, mining, shipbuilding, and coastal trade

**Region:** Northeastern Kastalshire — jagged coasts, frigid mountains, sparse arable land, and hostile wilderness
