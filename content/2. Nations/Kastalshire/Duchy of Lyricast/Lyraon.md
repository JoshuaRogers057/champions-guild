Lyraon is the spiritual and agricultural soul of the duchy. Surrounded on all sides by massive farmland estates, vineyards, and sacred groves, Lyraon is quiet, rural, and traditional. The city lacks the high walls of other cities and instead spreads wide like the lands it feeds. Farms extend right up to its cobbled roads, and even its town square features market gardens. It is protected by ring forts and castles to the north.

---

## Ruler

**Count Thomel Quayne** is a beloved innovator who developed new rotational crop models and soil enrichment rituals. He believes deeply in stewardship and can often be found working alongside farmers or sketching irrigation designs at his field desk. He hates Duchess Varron and would see her deposed if he could.
