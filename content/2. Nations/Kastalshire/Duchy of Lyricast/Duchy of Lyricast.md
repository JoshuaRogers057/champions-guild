Lyricast is the heartland of humanity's agricultural might. Its endless fields ripple with golden grain, and its orchards stretch beyond the horizon. Life here moves slowly but deeply. Time is measured by harvests, traditions, and the waxing of the moons. The people are family-focused and deeply rooted, favoring oral histories, rural festivals, and old farming magic passed down through generations. Though nestled between Caer Thian and Aremir, Lyricast remains somewhat aloof, richer in soil than in politics. This stands in contrast to the politics of Dawnmarch.

**Primary Function:** Farming, hawking, and hunting

**Region:** Central-west Kastalshire — vast plains, gentle hills, fertile fields, and lightly forested stretches
