Contramara is the capital of Kastalshire and the seat of House Thalros. It is home to the **Palace of Kastal** and the **Citadel**, a repurposed castle that now serves as the bureaucratic heart of the nation.

Contramara is the center of royal attention, prestige, wealth, ceremony, education, and corruption. It is the place where chivalry becomes most polished and most politically useful. The closer a noble house stands to Contramara, the more it is shaped by tournaments, court manners, patronage, academy access, legal maneuvering, and royal favor.

---

## Institutions

### The Praetorian

The Praetorian is a martial school that trains life-sworn soldiers in three divisions.

**Royal Guards** protect the royal family, palace, and capital.

**Town Guards** are dispatched nationwide to maintain peace and prevent rebellion.

**Shadow Guard** specialize in disinformation, espionage, and propaganda. They are not exclusively human.

Once a person graduates from the Praetorian, they may be recalled at any point to serve the nation. The Praetorian represents one of Kastalshire's clearest promises and warnings: service to the crown can elevate a person, but it also binds them for life.

### The Arcanum

The Arcanum is Kastalshire's battle-magic school. Admission requires either a noble endorsement or a graduate's recommendation and a successful entrance examination. The requirements speak clearly to Kastalshire's culture: talent matters, but access still passes through endorsement, recommendation, and social gates.
