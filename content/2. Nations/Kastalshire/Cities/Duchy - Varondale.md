# Duchy of Varondale

**Primary Function:** Fishing, mining, shipbuilding, and coastal trade
**Region:** Northeastern Kastalshire — jagged coasts, frigid mountains, sparse arable land, and hostile wilderness

Varondale is the easternmost and most perilous of the five duchies. Hemmed in by dark seas to the east and steep, frozen mountain ranges to the west, the region sees constant threats from sea raiders, giants, monstrous wildlife, and occasional cultic forces hiding in the cliffs. Its people do not expect rescue. They prepare, endure, and adapt.

Varondale is dangerous, but not helpless. Its position on the water and its control of deep harbors, shipbuilding, mineral exports, and coastal trade make it more independently wealthy than many outer regions. The duchy is less reliant on direct crown favor than its distance might suggest, which makes its relationship with Contramara especially complicated.

The land is treacherous, cold and craggy, with very little flat land suitable for farming. Communities survive by harvesting the sea and burrowing into the mountains for iron, coal, and more exotic minerals. Magic here tends to focus on preservation, warding, and weather resistance. Architecture in Varondale is fortress-first: thick stone walls, iron-braced shutters, sharply pitched slate roofs, and rooftops designed to anchor netting in case of high winds or flying threats.

---

## Duchy Capital: Varonhold

Built directly into the side of a mountain overlooking the sea, Varonhold is as much a fortress as it is a city. Its outer walls are carved from the mountain itself, reinforced with adamantine plating and dotted with ballista towers aimed at land and sea alike. Inside, tiered chambers descend deep into the rock, sheltering the city from storms and sieges. The sky is often grey, the air is cold, and torches line the streets at all hours.

**Duke Tharian Vael** is a grim figure rarely seen without his thick fur cloak and blackened steel breastplate. A former admiral who lost an arm during a giant raid, he now governs as a master strategist and defensive planner. He speaks little, acts decisively, and keeps a war council of trusted veterans, mages, and monster hunters. His word is law, and few challenge it because few survive the wilds without his protection.

---

## County City: Hearthpoint

Located on a sizable island more than 100 square miles in size, Hearthpoint is the duchy's trading gem. With a deep natural harbor, it is one of the safest anchoring points on the northeastern coast. Despite constant pirate harassment and harsh weather, the city thrives on trade, shipbuilding, and mineral exports. Its stone docks, ring walls, and war galleys make it a fortress-isle in its own right.

**Countess Sereda Flintmere** is a seasoned leader with salt in her blood and fire in her eyes. Once an heiress and socialite, she was constantly underestimated until her father died. She rose in prowess and reputation as a stateswoman, and she rules Hearthpoint with the precision of a captain running a ship. Her navy works to keep pirates in check, and her ports bustle with trade from across Eryndor and beyond. She has no patience for mainland politics, but she keeps a keen eye on rising threats and rising tides alike.
