# Duchy of Rath

**Primary Function:** Mixed modest farming, forestry, mining, and fishing
**Region:** Northern Kastalshire — a sprawling rugged land of hills, cold forests, isolated valleys, and unclaimed highlands

The Duchy of Rath is the largest of the five duchies by land, but much of that territory is wild and untamed. North of the county city of Raethalor, the terrain becomes harsh and unsettled, with cold hills, wind-lashed cliffs, and dense pine woods where few roads run true. Ancient ruins dot the landscape, and monstrous threats often go unanswered for weeks due to distance and sparse garrisons.

The people of Rath are hardened by necessity. They are fiercely independent and skeptical of outsiders, especially bureaucrats from Contramara. They prize self-sufficiency, community defense, and simple, effective craftsmanship. Their architecture reflects their environment: stone walls, thick beams, steep gabled roofs, and reinforced windows dominate every settlement. Many towns are half-fortified by default.

---

## Duchy Capital: Strongfork Hold

About 40 miles south of Raethalor, nestled precisely at the fork of the great Frostwater River, lies Strongfork Hold — the seat of the Dukes of Rath. The castle is both a military bastion and a hydro-engineered marvel. Waterwheels and arcane aqueducts provide power to workshops and heating for the interior halls. The hold has commanding views of both river branches and stands as a symbolic dividing line between the wilds and the rest of the kingdom.

**Duke Caldus Renward** is an aging noble whose stone-faced demeanor matches his fortress. A traditionalist to the bone, Caldus trusts local militias over the king's armies and has turned down all invitations to Contramara. He maintains an elite border ranger corps known as the **Coldwatch**, who patrol the duchy's outermost reaches with brutal efficiency. He is close friends with Duke Garran Velmouth of Aremir.

---

## County City: Raethalor

Built on both banks of the river near a broad glacial-fed lake, Raethalor is the beating heart of northern Rath. It is a key trade stop between the northern cities of Kastalshire. Raethalor boasts multiple bridges, water-powered mills, and river docks. The city is fortified but open-feeling, with timber-laced walkways and marketplaces floating just above the waterline. It serves as a supply depot and market for surrounding towns.

**Countess Vyra Nashen** is a visionary and skilled diplomat. She maintains trade relations with nearby duchies and counties and is known for resolving border disputes with negotiation backed by veiled threats. Her leadership has made Raethalor one of the most stable cities in the region, a bright spot in an otherwise turbulent duchy.

---

## County City: Frostford

To the far south lies Frostford, a jewel of serene beauty nestled along the shores of a crystal-clear lake fed by snowmelt and magic. Frostford is one of the most picturesque cities in all of Kastalshire. With gently rolling farmland, elegant stone bridges, and a growing merchant quarter, the city is wealthier than most would expect in the cold north. It is also a favored retreat for the nobility of Contramara seeking solitude and good fishing.

**Count Belric Morrin** is a refined, soft-spoken aristocrat known for polished manners and forward-thinking policies. A lover of art and architecture, he has funded the expansion of Frostford's public gardens and sponsored several bardic competitions. Though his city is far from danger, he maintains strong ties to Duke Renward despite the duke's open hostility toward the Contramara government.
