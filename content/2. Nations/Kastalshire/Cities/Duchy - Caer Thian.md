# Duchy of Caer Thian

**Primary Function:** Forestry and farming
**Region:** Southwestern Kastalshire — a coastal peninsula of dense forests and fertile farmland

Caer Thian is the breadbasket of the human realm, a sprawling peninsula carpeted in wheat fields, orchards, and dense forests. With long, rugged coastlines and a humid climate, the region boasts plentiful timber and agricultural exports. Its location also makes it vulnerable to raiders and pirates along the southern coast. Its people are hardy and practical, shaped by sea winds, forest danger, and the rhythm of the harvest.

---

## Duchy Capital: Caer Thian Castle

Caer Thian Castle is a sprawling complex with four large towers situated along its walls. One tower serves administration, one serves as the duke's palace, one serves as a storehouse, and the last as a barracks. The central keep serves as a common area for the entire palace. The castle stands 15 miles east of Caer Merel.

**Duke Elric Hawnspear** is a grizzled, broad-shouldered noble with calloused hands and a quiet mind. Once a forester to a minor noble house, he rose through the ranks by loyalty, battle merit, and wisdom rather than politics. Known for working alongside druids and agricultural mages, he understands that a hungry people know no loyalty. Full bellies have become a staple of his rule. He is a popular ruler who favors simple truths and strong walls.

---

## County City: Caer Valance

Caer Valance is situated between dense forests and sprawling farmland. The walled city is considered an ideal place to live — safe and with easy access to trade goods. The forest provides steady lumber, resulting in many wooden structures, and the greatest human carpenters are said to come from Caer Valance. The forest is dangerous, however. Foresters do not enter alone, but in guarded teams. Local forest rangers constantly deal with monsters that emerge from the trees.

**Countess Maera Bristal** is an insightful leader and an advocate for rotational farming and druidic conservation methods. She is known for her rivalry with pirate factions and commands a regional fleet of swift-response coastal rangers. She is a force of balance between need and greed, though some whisper she is too loyal to the forest and places too many restrictions on the harvesting of old wood deeper within.

---

## County City: Caer Merel

Situated in golden fields of wheat and corn, Caer Merel is a city built on the backs of farmers. It boasts large and highly secure grain houses and storage facilities. Caer Merel employs an army of mages to deal with vermin, mostly rats. This practice is highly respected, and the duty is not taken lightly. Caer Merel is not walled, but rings of watchtowers extend roughly 20 miles around the city to warn of invaders or monsters.

**Count Edran Pellor** is an adventurous former sea captain whose noble family traded in foodstuffs. He made his reputation sailing shipments into difficult and dangerous places. He retired inland to oversee Caer Merel after the death of his father in the year 1018. With a booming voice and a hundred stories, he is beloved by the farmers who feed the kingdom. Rumors suggest he is growing bored and that his attention to duty is slipping.
