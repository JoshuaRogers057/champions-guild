# Duchy of Aremir

**Primary Function:** Farming and mining — especially iron, gold, and common metals
**Region:** Central Kastalshire, with rocky foothills, highland forests, rolling valleys, and rugged northern borderlands

Aremir is a land of contrast and quiet resilience. Its northern reaches are scarred by monster incursions, bandit warbands, and haunted ruins, while its southern territories include some of the richest mining zones in the kingdom. Life here is hard and shaped by danger. Aremirians are pragmatic, stoic, and deeply self-reliant. They value grit over glory and respect those who contribute to survival, whether that means wielding a pickaxe, a ledger, or a sword.

---

## Duchy Capital: Velgard Hold

About 200 miles southeast of Arestead, nestled in the cradle of two low mountain ridges, sits the fortified palace known as Velgard Hold. Built partially into the mountainside and partially out on great terraces overlooking the valley, it is both a center of governance and a fortress of last resort. Beneath the palace lies a vast underground shelter designed to store grain, ore, weapons, and refugees if needed.

**Duke Garran Velmouth** is a lean, hard-eyed veteran with a permanent limp from an old frost giant encounter. Once commander of a mountain patrol battalion, he now rules Aremir with a survivalist's pragmatism. His court is filled with former adventurers, dwarven engineers, and grim-faced mining barons. He mistrusts ceremonial displays and despises useless nobles. His open criticism of the kingdom and his questions about why northern and southern Kastalshire should remain one nation have placed him in a precarious position.

---

## County City: Arestead

Arestead lies far to the north, close to the wildest parts of the kingdom. It is a walled town with watchtowers that never sleep and walls reinforced with iron sheeting. Its streets are wide enough for supply carts and monster patrols, and its buildings are compact, squat, and efficient. Arestead is more fortress-town than city. Its people measure time not in seasons, but in how long it has been since the last threat came down from the hills.

**Countess Liraeth Dorn** is a firm but admired leader — a battle-scarred woman known for quelling a miner's revolt with negotiation instead of force. When the next group of miners mistook restraint for weakness, she sealed the mines and let them negotiate their release through stone. She holds the respect of the frontier garrisons and oversees vast operations extracting iron and tin from the northern hills. She is quiet, severe, and efficient. It has long been rumored that she is sleeping with Duke Velmouth.

---

## County City: Falbrin

Falbrin sits to the south, nestled in the golden foothills near Contramara's trade routes. The city has a gentler tone than Arestead. Its mines produce gold, silver, and luxury metals, and its citizens enjoy greater wealth and relative peace. Its streets are clean, its stone buildings are often inlaid with copper or gilded trim, and its markets overflow with fine tools, cut gems, and metalwork.

**Count Arven Dallinar** is a polished nobleman raised in Contramara. Though not native to Aremir, he has proven adept at balancing profits with local loyalty. He expanded Falbrin's mining guilds and brought in dwarven architects to improve structural stability. He is also known for public festivals, rare but extravagant. He is not favored by Duke Velmouth.
