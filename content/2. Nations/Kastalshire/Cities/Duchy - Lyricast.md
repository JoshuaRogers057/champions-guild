# Duchy of Lyricast

**Primary Function:** Farming, hawking, and hunting
**Region:** Central-west Kastalshire — vast plains, gentle hills, fertile fields, and lightly forested stretches

Lyricast is the heartland of humanity's agricultural might. Its endless fields ripple with golden grain, and its orchards stretch beyond the horizon. Life here moves slowly but deeply. Time is measured by harvests, traditions, and the waxing of the moons. The people are family-focused and deeply rooted, favoring oral histories, rural festivals, and old farming magic passed down through generations. Though nestled between Caer Thian and Aremir, Lyricast remains somewhat aloof, richer in soil than in politics. This stands in contrast to the politics of Dawnmarch.

---

## Duchy Capital: Palace of Windsong Vale

Located about 20 miles outside Dawnmarch, the capital Palace of Lyricast is not a crowded metropolis but a vast, open-air palace complex surrounded by rolling farmlands and natural groves. The Palace of Windsong Vale is both castle and agricultural institute — a sprawling estate filled with flowering terraces, arcane irrigation channels, and chambers carved from pale limestone. It is rare for more than a week to pass without a feast, tournament, party, or festival hosted in Lyricast.

**Duchess Sybelle Varron** rules from here with grace. She is known for her devotion to the kingdom, but her true passion lies in her parties. She knows everyone and is a relentless gossip. The duchy is largely managed by her advisors while she plans her social calendar. She is not corrupt, merely vapid.

---

## County City: Lyraon

Lyraon is the spiritual and agricultural soul of the duchy. Surrounded on all sides by massive farmland estates, vineyards, and sacred groves, Lyraon is quiet, rural, and traditional. The city lacks the high walls of other cities and instead spreads wide like the lands it feeds. Farms extend right up to its cobbled roads, and even its town square features market gardens. It is protected by ring forts and castles to the north.

**Count Thomel Quayne** is a beloved innovator who developed new rotational crop models and soil enrichment rituals. He believes deeply in stewardship and can often be found working alongside farmers or sketching irrigation designs at his field desk. He hates Duchess Varron and would see her deposed if he could.

---

## County City: Dawnmarch

Dawnmarch is the duchy's beating economic heart and its closest connection to Contramara. Built with taller stone structures, its streets are busier and its guard more organized than in Lyraon. It rests near rich veins of ore and light forests, giving it access to lumber and trade goods that feed both its coffers and its reputation. The city often mocks the rural people of Lyraon while enjoying the fruits of their labor.

**Countess Revya Harnwell** is a former military commander who governs with disciplined efficiency. She personally oversees tax collection, trade route security, and mining rights. While she lacks Duchess Sybelle's grace, her respect is unquestioned, especially among merchants and miners. She is a frequent guest of Duchess Varron.
