Nestled precisely at the fork of the great Frostwater River lies Strongfork Hold — the seat of the Dukes of Rath. The castle is both a military bastion and a hydro-engineered marvel. Waterwheels and arcane aqueducts provide power to workshops and heating for the interior halls. The hold has commanding views of both river branches and stands as a symbolic dividing line between the wilds and the rest of the kingdom.

---

## Ruler

**Duke [[Caldus Renward]]** is an aging noble whose stone-faced demeanor matches his fortress. A traditionalist to the bone, Caldus trusts local militias over the king's armies and has turned down all invitations to [[Contramara]]. He maintains an elite border ranger corps known as the **Coldwatch**, who patrol the duchy's outermost reaches with brutal efficiency. He is close friends with Duke Garran [[Garran Velmouth|Velmouth]] of Aremir.
