The Duchy of Rath is the largest of the five duchies by land, but much of that territory is wild and untamed. North of the county city of Raethalor, the terrain becomes harsh and unsettled, with cold hills, wind-lashed cliffs, and dense pine woods where few roads run true. Ancient ruins dot the landscape, and monstrous threats often go unanswered for weeks due to distance and sparse garrisons.

The people of Rath are hardened by necessity. They are fiercely independent and skeptical of outsiders, especially bureaucrats from Contramara. They prize self-sufficiency, community defense, and simple, effective craftsmanship. Their architecture reflects their environment: stone walls, thick beams, steep gabled roofs, and reinforced windows dominate every settlement. Many towns are half-fortified by default.

**Primary Function:** Mixed modest farming, forestry, mining, and fishing

**Region:** Northern Kastalshire — a sprawling rugged land of hills, cold forests, isolated valleys, and unclaimed highlands
