Caer Thian is the breadbasket of the human realm, a sprawling peninsula carpeted in wheat fields, orchards, and dense forests. With long, rugged coastlines and a humid climate, the region boasts plentiful timber and agricultural exports. Its location also makes it vulnerable to raiders and pirates along the southern coast. Its people are hardy and practical, shaped by sea winds, forest danger, and the rhythm of the harvest.

**Primary Function:** Forestry and farming

**Region:** Southwestern Kastalshire — a coastal peninsula of dense forests and fertile farmland
