# Ash-Korrath

A city of secrets and sovereignty, veiled in stone and shadow beneath a living volcano. Tieflings have forged an empire beneath the earth, their streets lit by glowing magma veins and soulglass lanterns. Every citizen is bound by the Ashen Creed, and every secret is sealed in stone. Accessible only by a single fortified tunnel.

---

## Leadership

**High Sovereign Velcryn Dreadmark** — Clad in obsidian armor, Velcryn rarely speaks aloud, instead letting fire-glyphs bloom in the air to issue decrees.
**Archpactress Neralza Chainborn** — Blindfolded tiefling whose mastery of soul-thread contracts binds devils to silence and nobles to obedience.

---

## Districts

### The Throne Core
Located at the bottom of the city's spiraling descent. Houses the **Infernal Throne** and the **Cinder Court**.

### Emberreach Market
Primary hub of commerce. The **Firetongue Bazaar** offers everything from hell-etched daggers to bottled demon breath. Overseen by **[[Rinvali Emberlace]]**.

### The Ashen Mantle
Homes carved into glowing rock faces, linked by skywalks and hovering bridges. Houses the **Pacthall Academy**, ruled by **[[Vaelith]] Cindervow**.

### Crucible Row
Metal baptized in soulfire at the **Soulforge Crucible**. **Warden [[Thalkur Brazevein]]** oversees the Molten Guard.

### Hollowshade Warrens
Sprawling commoner quarters. The **Glowpools** and **Cinder Chapel** serve as heart of daily life. Overseen by **Foreman [[Grothar Leadskull]]**.

### Riftgate Vaults
Sealed behind runed gates. Houses the **Wyrmbind Crypts**. **Magistrate [[Olvik the Smoke-Scarred]]** oversees punishment and interrogation.

### The Emberloft
Vast aviary holding the city's hellclaws and giant bats. **Captain [[Sarra Flamecoil]]** commands the riders.
