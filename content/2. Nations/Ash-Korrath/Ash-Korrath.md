# Ash-Korrath — Government, Military, Economy & Culture
*Tieflings of Eryndor*

---

## Government

**Ruling Body:** The **Council of Infernal Lords** — powerful tieflings, each descended from a unique infernal bloodline. They prioritize survival, secrecy, and power.

**The Grand Infernal:** First among equals; assumes supreme authority during times of war or crisis.

**The Ashen Creed:** Enforced by **Ember Judges**, this legal code maintains ruthless discipline and secrecy within the city.

---

## Military

- **Hellfire Sorcerers** — Wield soul-scorching infernal magic.
- **Molten Armor Knights** — Brutal warriors clad in lava-infused armor.
- **Flaming War Beasts** — Infernal hounds, magma drakes, and fiendish constructs.
- **Ember Judges** — Inquisitors who eliminate traitors and enforce the Ashen Creed.
- **Pyre Guard** — Elite city defenders trained in both blade and spell.

---

## Economy

Underground economy of smuggling, devil pacts, and black-market exchanges. The **Emberreach Market** (overseen by Rinvali Emberlace) handles infernal relics and magical reagents. The **Soulforge Crucible** generates enchanted weapons and war materials.

---

## Fragment Access

Smuggled fragments and infernal artifacts circulated through the Firetongue Bazaar. The Riftgate Vaults are rumored to contain sealed fragment cores and soulbound relics. Access tightly restricted and enforced by Magistrate Olvik the Smoke-Scarred.

---

## Culture

Survival Above All. Strength, secrecy, and ruthless loyalty define life in Ash-Korrath.

**Sigilmarks:** Magical brands cast at birth, appearing only upon betrayal.
**Festival of the Hollow Ember:** Once a year, all flames extinguish in a ritual of silence.
**Pacts as Power:** A contract here is sacred. Breaking it is death.

### The Ashen Creed

The central binding principle of Ash-Korrath City. Even its wording is secret. Core tenets:

**Silence to Outsiders** — No citizen may reveal the location, structure, or existence of [[Ash-Korrath City]] to those not bound by the Creed.
*Punishment: Branding by Sigilmark and public burning of the tongue.*

**Loyalty to the Flame** — Loyalty to the city and the Council supersedes family, blood, and self.
*Punishment: Soulfracture — magical severance of infernal lineage.*

**Obedience to Pact-Law** — Contracts, both spoken and inscribed, are sacred and inviolable.
*Punishment: Pact Reversal — the condemned inherits their own broken pact's burden.*

**Respect the Veiled Flame** — The Grand Infernal and the Ember Judges are instruments of the city's will. Public dissent is considered sacrilege.
*Punishment: Mind-Sealing — magical muting and confinement in the Smoldering Depths.*

**Guard the Ember of Legacy** — Infernal bloodlines must be honored and cultivated.
*Punishment: Exile.*

---

## Religion

The city reveres **Asmodeus** as the origin of their strength, though no official temple exists. Pacts carry sacred weight. Breaking them is death.

**Festival of the Infernals** — Occurs during solstices; features duels, infernal offerings, and pacts, especially with Asmodeus.

---

## Mercenaries & Criminal Organizations

Ash-Korrath has extensive smuggling rings, soul traffickers, and infernal debt collectors operating in secret. The Firetongue Bazaar is notorious for shady deals and untraceable sales. Devil pacts serve as mercenary contracts; breaking them incurs infernal consequences. It is very likely that [[Ash-Korrath City]] is home to a powerful criminal or mercenary syndicate, though its identity or existence is unconfirmed.

---

## Old Blood

**Trait: Ashen Flame of the Old Blood**

> **Effect (Level 7):** Once per long rest, when you take damage you can use your reaction to force the attacker to make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + proficiency bonus + Charisma modifier) or take 2d10 fire damage and be blinded for a minute.
> **Drawback:** After using this feature, you are blinded for the next minute.

**Physical Markings:** Blackened veins glowing faintly like magma, eyes shimmer with burning embers, a persistent smell of ash clings to their presence.

---

## Grudges & Political Strain

- Celestial orders and holy nations see Ash-Korrath as a heretical blight.
- Ash-Korrath avoids entanglements and punishes interference harshly.
- The Cinder Court is rumored to be divided over how much power the High Sovereign should wield.
