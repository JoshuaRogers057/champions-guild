# Thundrakar — Government, Military, Economy & Culture
*Goliaths of Eryndor*

---

## Government

Thundrakar is governed by the **Council of Six Chieftains**, with each chieftain representing one of the six great Goliath clans: Stonefist, Firebrand, Frostbane, Cloudbreaker, Thundercall, and Hillroot. Leadership is earned through trial and combat, and each Chieftain is the strongest or most cunning of their lineage.

Nuelhiem serves as the nation's political center. The High Chieftain presides, selected not by bloodline but by feats, vision, and the acceptance of the clans. Currently, this role belongs to **Drogath Stormvein**.

The Council meets formally at Titan's Seat during the annual **Giants Moot**. Laws are oral, ancestral, and situational. Decisions are passed through debate, combat trials, or **stormbinding rites**.

- **High Chieftain Drogath Stormvein** (Nuelhiem) — Unifier of the clans, stormborn and wise.
- **Warchief Skarn Kar-Turog** (The Wall) — Militant, feared, controls Thundrakar's northern defense.
- **High Mootmistress Arva Stormvein** (Tundralheim) — Keeper of traditions, arbiter during the Giants Moot.

---

## Military

The Goliath military is forged in clan loyalty. Thundrakar's true power lies in the **Wartide Pact** — a sacred agreement that all six clans will unite in times of war.

- **Clan Stonefist:** Juggernauts wielding oversized weapons, defending mountain passes.
- **Clan Firebrand:** Shield-focused infantry, renowned for phalanx tactics and anti-fragment fervor.
- **Clan Frostbane:** Cavalry specialists, riding woolly rhinos and frost behemoths.
- **Clan Cloudbreaker:** Rune-mages, stormcallers, and siege-breakers.
- **Clan Thundercall:** Shamans and druids who weaponize weather, lightning, and beasts.
- **Clan Hillroot:** Berserkers, fearless in frontal assaults and tunneling warfare.

The Wall houses the **Frostjaws** raider fleet with 8–12 longships and the warship *Hunger of Kar*. Ritual Combat and Honor Duels play a central role in Goliath military life.

## Economy

Wealth flows from beast-husbandry and agriculture (large herds of mammoths, woolly sheep, dire wolves, frost rhinos), smithing (the Stormforge Hold in Nuelhiem produces stormsteel and thunderforged weapons), fragment trade and ruin salvage, seasonal raiding, and festivals like the Giants Moot and Bridgewake.

**Notable Resources:** Firebrand Forges (rune-enhanced armor), Cloudbreaker Silver & Gold Mines, Glowroot Wells (bioluminescent alchemical ingredients), Thunderpens (war-beast breeding).

---

## Fragment Access

Goliaths control two major fragment mines — one in the eastern highlands, one in the western tundra. Fragments are considered taboo by many, particularly **Clan Firebrand**, which believes fragments weaken the soul's connection to stone and sky and openly denounces fragment-powered weapons. Despite philosophical reservations, fragments are traded to other nations for metals, rare foods, medicines, and supplies. Magitech is almost entirely absent; rune-based spellcraft anchored in proto-giant tradition is preferred.

The Gnomes of Lexovar most predominantly benefit from trade with Thundrakar.

## Culture

Thundrakar spans the freezing tundra to the north of Eryndor, divided into east and west by the Ice Sea — a unity of Clans Kar (west) and Thundra (east). Goliaths believe they are descendants of the ancient Giants.

Strength is moral truth. Deeds are currency. Goliaths hold competitions of all kinds: wrestling, hammer throws, sled pulling, hunting races, cliff scaling.

**Goliath tattoos** mark achievements, oaths, and clan lineage — made with volcanic ink, glowroot resin, and animal blood. The sacred rite is performed only by elders, and only witnessed events can be marked.

The fallen are buried beneath stone cairns, sometimes mountain-sized. Cairn size reflects honor.

**Three major festivals** (all Eastern Thundrakar, the sacred ancestral homeland):
- **Bridgewake Festival** (Giants' Causeway) — Mock-reconstruction of the ancient bridge.
- **Flame Vigil** (Nuelhiem) — War stories recited until dawn.
- **The Naming Winds** (Tundralheim) — Youths climb storm cliffs to earn their adult names.

## Religion

Goliath religion is deeply animistic and ancestral. Most worship personified forces of nature and the echoes of the Giants.

- **The Skyfather** — The storm and judgment (Cloudbreaker).
- **Stonemother** — Endurance, memory, and hearth (Stonefist).
- **The Everhunt** — Predator and prey in eternal chase (Hillroot).
- **The Voice Below** — Earthquakes, secrets, ancestral dreams (Stonefist).

**Religious Figures:** Stormseers (diviners of weather and fate), Lorecallers (priests reciting ancestral deeds), Beastbinders (shamans treating war-beasts as spirit kin). Goliath faith is not coercive. It is lived, earned, and invoked.

## Mercenaries & Criminal Organizations

### The Thunderhide Legion
*Base: The Giants' Causeway | Motto: "We ride the storm into the bones of gods."*

A mobile warband of berserker-cavalry raised from Clan Frostbane and Hillroot (~120 active members). Mounted on mammoths and frost behemoths. Known for brutal charge tactics, environmental warfare, and cliff-mounted ambushes. Their frost-armored mounts are bred to shrug off magic and fear.

**Past Contracts:** Held a pass during a hobgoblin siege; slayed a fragment-mutated wyvern near Tundralheim; escorted a cursed relic from the Dome — refused entry, waited outside, fought off undead for 6 nights.

## Old Blood

**Trait: Echo of the Titanborn**

> **Effect (Level 7):** Once per long rest, as a bonus action, enter a Titan's Trance for 1 minute. Gain temporary HP equal to proficiency bonus at the start of each turn. Gain resistance to cold damage. Carrying capacity doubles and you are considered one size larger.
> **Drawback:** When the trance ends, gain one level of exhaustion.

**Physical Markings:** Skin hardens and becomes stone-like around the eyes. Glowing giant-runes etch across arms and chest.

## Grudges & Political Strain

- **Drakmir Dominion:** Ancient animosity. Many clans train specifically for anti-draconic tactics. Trade is virtually non-existent.
- **Fragment Philosophy Clashes:** Clan Firebrand's denouncements of fragment-based weaponry cause diplomatic strain with trade hubs.
- **Halfling Trade Tensions:** Warchief Skarn Kar-Turog's raids have led to refusal of trade access at The Wall.
- **Internal:** Hillroot vs. Firebrand (raiding ethics); Stonefist vs. Frostbane (blood-rights in the eastern peaks). Settled in ritual duels during the Giants Moot.
