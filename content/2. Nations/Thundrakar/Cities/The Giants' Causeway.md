# The Giants' Causeway

Twin towns built on either side of a massive mountain chasm — remnants of a shattered bridge of Giant origin. The clans of Cloudbreaker, Hillroot, and Frostbane share primary influence here.

**Unique Lore:** The Fractured Span (ruins of the original Giant bridge), Whispers of the Deep (rumors of a Titan or construct below), The Binding Pillars (proto-giant ritual monoliths, site of the Bridgewake Festival).

---

## Leadership

**Commander Vekk Stoneborn** — Former duel champion turned tactician. Oversees the Thane's Roost and Chasm Market trade security.

---

## Districts

**Stonecross Hold (West)** — Moaning Arch (singing stone support beam), The Thane's Roost (joint militia HQ).
**Chasm Market (Bridge Level)** — Rope Road skywalk trading platforms, seasonal vendor tribes.
**Echohearth (East)** — Hall of the Forgotten Spans (crystal bridge memorial), Duel Steps (warrior trial cliff tiers).
**Rootward Quarter (Below)** — Thunderpens (war beast breeding), Glowroot Wells (bioluminescent plant farming).
