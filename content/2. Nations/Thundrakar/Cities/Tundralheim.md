# Tundralheim

Tundralheim is the myth-shrouded summit of Goliath heritage. This city built among the clouds is a monument to Goliath endurance. It becomes a thunderous epicenter of activity during the sacred **Giants Moot** — a ceremonial gathering of clans, champions, beastmasters, and lore-keepers from across the icy realm. Songs, rites, and ancestral voices echo across the cliffs. Pilgrimage here is a rite of passage.

---

## Leadership

**High Mootmistress Arva Stormvein** — Stoic and regal cousin of Drogath Stormvein. Keeper of sacred lore and rituals.

---

## Notable Locations

**The Hall of Echoes** — Moot chamber and judgment dome.
**The Bone Stair** — Trial stairway carved of mammoth ivory.
**Skycaller's Perch** — Stormwatch and spiritual beacon. Site of meditation and vision rituals.
