# The Wall

Built into the side of an impossible black wall that stretches from sea to sea across the northern peninsula, this city is less a settlement and more a war-machine carved into myth. The structure it gets its name from rises 20 feet high, utterly smooth, made of unbreakable stone that predates any recorded history. No magic affects it. No tools scar it. No one knows who built it.

---

## Leadership

**Warchief Skarn Kar-Turog** — Blunt and calculating, clad in glacial rune-etched armor. His legendary axe, *Voidcleaver*, is said to have split a frost giant in half.

**The Frostjaws Fleet** — Raider longships and the warship *Hunger of Kar*, launched from Icespine Harbor.

---

## Key Locations

**The Cliffkeep** — Command fortress and warlord gathering site.
**Hammerline Arena** — Ritual combat and training.
**The Vowring** — Oath-circle plaza where divine wrath is feared.
**The Belowroads** — Secret tunnels and cisterns of unknown origin.
