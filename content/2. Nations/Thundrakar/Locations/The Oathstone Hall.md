# The Oathstone Hall

The Oathstone Hall is a **western** Thundrakar war-monastery built near the frozen raiding coasts, where initiates are remade into living siege weapons. They worship no god — they believe strength is the only honest language, blood the only permanent oath, and mercy something victors may discuss after the enemy is broken.

The Oathstone warriors fight like mythic raiders: massive bodies, ritual scars, heavy weapons, wolf-hide cloaks, bone charms, and terrifying discipline beneath the savagery.

---

## War-Master: Karn-Tuul

A massive goliath woman with a broken jaw, iron rings through both ears, and frostbite scars across her hands. She speaks in short commands and tests visitors by insulting them until they either stand firm or shrink. She believes civilization makes warriors soft, and that only pain, hunger, blood, and victory reveal what a person truly is.
