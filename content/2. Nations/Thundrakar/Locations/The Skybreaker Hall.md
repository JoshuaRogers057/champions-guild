# The Skybreaker Hall

The Skybreaker Hall is an **eastern** Thundrakar war-monastery built high among storm cliffs and giant ruins, where the air is thin and the wind can tear a man from the mountain. Initiates climb to the Hall without ropes; those who fall are left to the stones.

The Skybreakers are cliff-warriors, storm-raiders, and highland executioners. They fight from impossible terrain, leap from ledges, break shield walls with falling charges, and use drums, horns, and war chants to shake enemies before the first axe lands.

---

## Stormthane: Stormthane Korrak Vesh

A gaunt, towering goliath with one clouded eye, wind-burned skin, and old fall-scars across his ribs and shoulders. He rarely raises his voice because he expects obedience before volume is needed. Korrak believes death by falling is not tragedy. It is judgment.
