# The Broken Spear

The Broken Spear sits in old northern war ruins, where students study ambush, tactics, retreat, and defeat. They believe losing is only shameful if nothing is learned. Their warriors become scouts, trap-setters, battlefield readers, and commanders who know how to turn failure into the next kill.

---

## Spear-Master: Spear-Master Varkka Dreadwise

An old hobgoblin veteran with one eye and no patience for empty boasting.
