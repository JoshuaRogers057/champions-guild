# The Gloamtongue Cell

The Gloamtongue Cell operates beneath [[Borgnar]], training spies, negotiators, blackmailers, assassins, and oath-brokers. Its members are called **Gloamtongues**, and every survivor bears the brand of a forked tongue. They fight with secrets before blades, learning coded speech, false surrender, hostage leverage, and how to turn an enemy's pride against them.

---

## Whisper-Chief: Whisper-Chief Yegra Sootfang

A bugbear woman with ash-dyed fur, soft steps, and a smile that never reaches her eyes. She speaks gently, remembers everything, and has ruined warlords without drawing steel.
