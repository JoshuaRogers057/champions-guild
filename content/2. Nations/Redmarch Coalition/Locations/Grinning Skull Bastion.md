# Grinning Skull Bastion

Grinning Skull Bastion is a fortified manor ringed by hundreds of skull totems. Its warriors train in fear, death, pursuit, and brutal close combat. They are relentless hunters who break morale before they break bodies.

---

## Skull-Lord: Skull-Lord Brakkaum Grinmaw

A massive bugbear with bone piercings, filed teeth, and a laugh that never sounds friendly.
