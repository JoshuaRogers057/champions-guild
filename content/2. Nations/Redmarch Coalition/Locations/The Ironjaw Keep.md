# The Ironjaw Keep

The Ironjaw Keep is a conquered fortress carved into a mountainside, now training fighters, rangers, clerics, and hard pilgrims in survival, tracking, mountain warfare, and battlefield discipline.

---

## Keep-Master: Tharnak Vulg

A broad orc cleric-warrior with a steel plate bolted over his lower jaw and a voice like grinding stone.
