# Redmarch Coalition — Government, Military, Economy & Culture
*Orcs and Goblinoids of Eryndor*

---

## Government

The Goblinoid "government" is a loose authoritarian structure ruled by the Old Blood Orc Families. At its head is the Warchief, traditionally the leader of Grushnar, the largest city and almost always an Orc.

Tribes remain autonomous, and any Warchief who tries to govern the tribes directly doesn't last long. The Warchief functions as a military commander only during nation-wide wars. Leadership is not hereditary, but earned through combat or tribal merit.

### Council of Blood

A group of retired warriors from each tribe that advises the Warchief, based in Grushnar.

**Warchief Grisha the Ironhowl** leads with terrifying charisma and brute efficiency. Earned his title by breaking an ogre's spine in public combat and seizing control of three warbands through raw fury.

- **Karg Bonesplitter** — Hobgoblin strategist of the Shattered Pike tribe, responsible for military logistics and battle formations.
- **Yurra Skullmire** — Bugbear matron and master of shadow warfare, former leader of the feared Shadowmaw.
- **Thrikkle Safa** — Goblin beast rider commander, still rides into council meetings on a Giant Wolf Spider named Scream.
- **Vornok the Black-Fanged** — Orc drillmaster, retired berserker, and enforcer of discipline within Grushnar's barracks and streets.

## Military

Each city primarily functions autonomously though in times of war they unite into a terrifying military force. The goblinoid military when united relies on overwhelming force, ferocity, and intimidation.

**Large Goblinoids (Orcs, Hobgoblins, Bugbears):** Shock troops with massive weapons, often entering a blood frenzy that breaks enemy lines.

**Small Goblinoids (Goblins):** Light cavalry, beast riders mounted on war boars, dire wolves, or giant wolf spiders, armed with shortbows and curved swords.

**Tactics:** Ambush, brutal frontal assaults, relentless intimidation.

Each major city contributes: Grushnar trains elite melee warriors; Molgarak breeds and deploys the finest beast-riders and scouts; Urthak Var specializes in spirit-channeled warriors and fire mages; Borgnar provides mercenary contracts and logistical support.

---

## Economy

Goblinoids rely on smithing, hunting, raiding, and bartering. Trade is minimal and usually coerced. The greatest export is warriors — entire tribes hire out as mercenaries during campaign seasons and return with supplies for winter.

- **Borgnar:** Main economic hub, home to a thriving black market. Deals in weapons, poisons, slaves, relics, and mercenary services.
- **Grushnar:** Supplies weapons and armor via its massive forges.
- **Molgarak:** Trains raiders for hire.
- **Urthak Var:** Sells enchanted weapons and spiritual services.

---

## Fragment Access

The Goblinoid nation controls territories rich in fragments, especially in wild and mountainous regions. Borgnar hosts one of the most productive fragment mines in the Coalition but lacks the expertise to extract its full potential. Grushnar uses volcanic shards in weapon-forging rites at the Anvil of Gruumsh. Urthak Var uses fragment-imbued altars and rituals to commune with spirits.

Despite high natural access, fragment refinement remains a bottleneck. Most fragments are traded raw or used in religious and weapon-forging contexts rather than large-scale magitech development.

## Culture

Goblinoid society is a war-based tribal culture. For a member of the Redmarch Coalition to not have fought is to have not yet lived. Every adult must prove themselves in a duel, raid, or battle trial before they can be considered an adult. The more difficult the challenge, the more renown is won.

Mastering magical abilities is considered a trial in and of itself — mages are exempt from trials by combat. Arcane mastery immediately grants adulthood status.

Dishonor is worse than death, though handled in secret. Assassinations and tactical withdrawal, even theft, are frequently looked on favorably. Destruction of plunderable goods or the assault of the weak brings no renown.

Cultural values vary slightly by region:
- **Grushnar:** Discipline, hierarchy, physical dominance.
- **Molgarak:** Mobility, quickness, beast-bonding. Youths paired with animals before given weapons.
- **Urthak Var:** Ancestral reverence and spiritual fire. Combat is ceremony. Elders treated as oracles.
- **Borgnar:** Coded etiquette, contract honor, non-lethal competition. Intelligence and leverage carry power.

Each city hosts at least one martial or magical academy. Strength in Redmarch is not just expected — it is required.

## Religion

**Gruumsh, the One-Eye** is the dominant and unifying deity. God of conquest and survival. Glorifies endurance, strength, and the right of might. He is seen as the first warleader, the one who taught the first tribe to fight. His eye watches all battlefields.

**Religious Practices:** Blood duels and sacrifices before major campaigns; forging weapons in volcanic flame with ritual chanting; spirit-calling at sacred cairns and fire-altars.

**Centers of Worship:** Grushnar (Anvil of Gruumsh), Urthak Var (pyre rites and trancewalking), Molgarak (beast-bonding rituals), Borgnar (Gruumsh's name invoked in contracts with symbolic "burning seals").

## Mercenaries & Criminal Organizations

### The Duskwatch
Often called the "Thieves' Guild," based out of a six-story-deep basement beneath an unassuming tavern in Grushnar. Commands a sophisticated network of burglars, fences, and spies with presence in every nation. Specializes in heists, blackmail, smuggling stolen relics. Led by **The Veiled Whisper**, a faceless figure of mythic reputation whose identity has never been unraveled.

### Mercenary Culture
The Redmarch Coalition boasts a deep mercenary culture. At times entire tribes serve as seasonal mercenaries. Contracts honored with blood-oaths or cursed tokens. Beast-mounted raiders from Molgarak and fire-casters from Urthak Var charge a premium. Grushnar maintains elite shock companies known as **The Bloodsworn**, available for hire only through direct Warchief approval.

### Crime and Smuggling
Borgnar hosts Redmarch's largest black market. Codes and sign language common to prevent overheard deals. Non-lethal enforcement preferred in Borgnar to maintain trade. Grushnar punishes even the smallest theft with arena combat or public maiming.

## Old Blood

The Old Blood manifests in Goblinoids from ruling lineages. When this occurs, the individual is granted immediate status as a raid leader, the equivalent to nobility.

> **Level 7 Power — Fury of the Fallen:** Once per long rest, when reduced to 0 HP but not killed outright, you may take the Attack action before falling unconscious (no movement).
> **Drawback:** After using this feature, you fall unconscious with 1 failed death saving throw.

Marked by red-black tattoos, battle visions, or rage-induced strength. Anyone who develops these marks is usually brought before the Council of Blood or immediately given a warband to lead.

## Grudges & Political Strain

- Most nations view Goblinoids as raiders and existential threats. Goblinoids see raiding as both tradition and necessity.
- Diplomacy often fails due to cultural mismatch and latent arrogance from other species. Elves, dragonborn, and Aasimar are particularly hated.
- Elven border fortresses constantly repel Molgarak beast-riders.
- Dwarven enclaves hoard fragment-processing techniques.
- Human nations vilify goblinoids in religious and royal law.
- Grushnar's dominance is resented by the roaming tribes.
- Borgnar's peacekeeping customs clash with Redmarch blood law.
- Urthak Var's spiritual authority sometimes overrides Warchief rulings.
