# Molgarak

Built into forested cliff faces and split between cave warrens and protected valleys, Molgarak is alive with howls, growls, and laughter. This goblin-dominated city is a place of motion — beasts thunder across stone roads, riders practice synchronized charges, and scouts climb cliff paths like squirrels. The Outrider clans are highly sought-after mercenaries when they aren't the subject of a conflict.

---

## Leadership

**General Urshak** — Goblin dire wolf-rider and warlord of the Outer Clans. Rides a dire wolf named Razzles.

---

## Districts

**Fangpen Quarter** — Beast pits and breeding grounds.
**Whipfang Arena** — Mounted duels and mock battles.
**Black Hollow** — Underground sabotage and stealth barracks.
**Greenskin Hall** — Feasting, mercenary boards, and clan meetings.

---

## School of Note

**The Raiding School of Molgarak** — Mobile corps for goblin youth in mounted warfare, beast rituals, ambush drills, and guerrilla tactics.
