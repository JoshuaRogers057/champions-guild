# Urthak Var

Urthak Var is carved into a smoldering mountain slope where deep underground magma tubes vent steam and light dances along valley cliffs. The city reveres ancestral spirits, fire magic, and martial traditions rooted in legacy. Smoke and incense fill the air, and glowing runes light the stone walkways at dusk. Chief Kane has tapped into the primal magics of the lava tubes and channels them with nearly unmatched prowess across the Coalition.

---

## Leadership

**Chief Kane** — Hobgoblin shaman draped in ash-painted bone charms. Communes with the ancestors before major decisions.

---

## Districts

**Ashfire Rise** — Pyre rites and spiritual battle blessings.
**Cairn District** — Tribal ancestral tombs with eternal flame altars.
**Flame Tunnels** — Magma-heated forge rituals and dangerous rites.
**Spiritmarch Row** — Shrine-lined paths and drumming trance schools.

---

## School of Note

**The Shaman Circle** — Academy for fire elementalists and spirit-touched hobgoblins trained in ancestral invocation and battle-channeling.
