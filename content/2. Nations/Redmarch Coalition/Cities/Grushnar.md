# Grushnar

Grushnar is the thundering heart of the Redmarch Coalition's unity — if such a word can apply to a city constantly on the edge of civil war. Held together by strength alone, the capital is not elegant, but it is terrifyingly functional. Smoke from forge-fires darkens the sky, banners of conquered tribes hang from iron spires, and drums echo across the canyons day and night. Here, every citizen is a soldier first. The weak are crushed, the cunning are watched, and the strong rise.

---

## Leadership

**Warchief Rollin Long-tooth (The Ironhowl)** — Earned his title by breaking an ogre's spine in public combat and seizing control of three warbands through raw fury.

**Council of Blood:**
- **Karg Bonesplitter** — Hobgoblin strategist of the Shattered Pike tribe.
- **Yurra Skullmire** — Bugbear matron, master of shadow warfare, former leader of the Shadowmaw.
- **Thrikkle Safa** — Goblin beast rider commander. Rides a Giant Wolf Spider named Scream into council meetings.
- **Vornok the Black-Fanged** — Orc drillmaster, retired berserker, enforcer of discipline.

## Districts

### The Bloodforge District
**Function:** Weaponsmithing, armor crafting, warbeast gear
**Notable:** *Anvil of Gruumsh* — volcanic shard where relic weapons are quenched in beast blood.
**School:** *Redsteel Hall* — military smithing academy; forging weapons is part of warrior initiation.

### The Maw Market
**Function:** Trade, mercenaries, supplies, black market
**Notable:** *The Pit Barter Ring* — disputes settled by public duel. Enforced by the **Mawhands** (ogre enforcers).

### Hollowfang Warrens
**Function:** Goblin residential, beast cages, experimental training
**Notable:** *Scorchrun Arena* — goblin pit-fights to prove tribe loyalty.
**School:** *Fanglight Burrow* — alchemy, sabotage, and beast-handling tactics.

### Duskwatch Hollow
**Function:** Criminal underworld, information trade, assassination
**Notable:** *The Wood Coin* — smoky tavern for whispered contracts and poisoned wine.
**Leader:** The Veiled Whisper — masked goblin, identity unknown even to Grisha.

### Toothspire Citadel
**Function:** Seat of power, elite military command, diplomacy
**Notable:** *Council Hall of Bones* — war room of the Council of Blood.
**School:** *Howlspire Academy* — elite warrior school for war tactics, melee, and dominance.
