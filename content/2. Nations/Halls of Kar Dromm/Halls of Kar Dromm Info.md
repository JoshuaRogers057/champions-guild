# Halls of Kar Dromm — Government, Military, Economy & Culture
*Dwarves of Eryndor*

---

## Government

The Dwarves are ruled by a King, advised and his power checked by the **Stone Council** — a council of 10 dwarves, one elder from each of the 10 clans. Both the Stone Council and the monarchy rule in terms of foreign affairs. On the domestic side, governance is more honorary than anything where the Monarchy is concerned, with each clan ruling nearly autonomously — including handling local taxes, tariffs, and military matters.

---

## Military

**Core Units:** Heavily armored infantry wielding warhammers and axes. Dwarven heavy armor is arguably the finest in the world.

**Ranged Units:** Musket units enhanced with magitech. Though powerful, their range and accuracy are less than elven longbows. The volleyed fire of a musket line is unparalleled in sheer destructive power compared to most military weaponry.

**Tactics:** Dwarven fortifications are nearly impenetrable. Defensive strategies rely on strongholds and unbreaking infantry lines. Offensively, dwarves utilize solid infantry support with ranged backlines.

---

## Economy

The dwarven economy is centered around mining and craftsmanship. Renowned for their metalwork, enchanted items, and architectural feats.

---

## Fragment Access

Dwarves control three of the world's largest fragment mines, located in Huarthal, Kavzar, and Bardrith. The major fragment mines allow dwarves to power magitech and forge artifacts. Heavy trade with gnomes for magitech designs and with humans and halflings for agriculture.

---

## Culture

Dwarven clans are not peaceful or compromising, even among themselves. Skirmishes are common, but full wars are rare. If a clan becomes too dominant, the others band together to maintain balance. No enemy will find the dwarves divided in a time of external conflict.

Trade disputes are common with other races. Dwarves believe the true owner of an item is the maker — be it clan, family, brand, or person. The dwarves believe that when an item is bought it will be returned upon the death of the purchaser. This has resulted in the dwarves believing that nearly every other race lacks honor and are thieves.

---

## Religion

**Primary Deity:** **Moradin, the All-Father** — god of creation, craftsmanship, and family.

**Influence:** His teachings focus on divine duty through work, and the importance of protecting family, clan, and nation.

## Mercenaries & Criminal Organizations

### The Stalwart Legion

*Base: Thumdar | Leader: Tharvik "Ironhead" Gravestone | Honor: High*

Based in Thumdar, led by Tharvik "Ironhead" Gravestone. They honor contracts above all and never break them.

### The Rotrok Marauders

*Leader: Gorath "The Butcher" Krenz*

A rogue coalition of bandits and mercenaries led by Gorath "The Butcher" Krenz, a ruthless artificer with a passion for chaos.

---

## Old Blood

**Trait: Weight of the Old Blood**

Dwarven noble families and individuals with noble lineage within four generations may manifest the Old Blood.

> **Level 7 Power:** As a bonus action, gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage for 1 round.
> **Cost:** On the following round, gain vulnerability to those same damage types.

**Appearance:** Broader frame, muscular build, and dwarven rune tattoos around the eyes.

---

## Grudges & Political Strain

- Dwarves often clash with other races over ownership and honor.
- Constant raids and wars with goblinoids and pirates.
- Longstanding hatred and rivalry with the Dragonborn.
- Uneasy reliance with gnomes.

## Notable NPCs

### Thumdar: Capital NPCs

**High King Durak Stonemantle** — Revered sovereign chosen once per generation by the ten clan elders. Known for calm wisdom and unshakable resolve.

**High Forgefather Ordrin Flameheart** — Overseer of the Grand Temple of Moradin and the Royal Forge.

**Marshal Brega Stonefist** — Military commander at Stoneward Academy.

**Dean Keldra Runevein** — Head of Runeforge College and magitech defenses.

**Coinwarden Thaldren Hammersum** — Oversees Deepmarket trade and taxation.

**Artificer-Master Brumli Ashbelt** — Leader of the Ember Guild. Inventor with rune-powered prosthetic arms. Has denied gnomish recruitment attempts for years.

**Lord Steward Farnik Graymantle** — Manages the King's Reach and the Stonecloak Guard.
