# Blackwake Anchorage

Blackwake Anchorage is a smuggler port hidden near **Huarthal**, built around old mining tunnels, drainage passages, and concealed routes feeding into the River Silt. It moves cargo under the feet of inspectors, using tunnel carts, sealed river skiffs, false ore wagons, and flooded side channels.

The Anchorage survives because everyone involved understands restraint. No loud crews, no pointless killing, no stolen cargo that brings a clan army down on everyone's head.

---

## Head of Port: Daskorr Mavik Durn

A thick-necked dwarf with a split lip, river-burned skin, and a voice like gravel dragged through an iron pipe. He is not charming, not subtle, and not easily frightened. Daskorr keeps order by making examples rarely, but permanently.
