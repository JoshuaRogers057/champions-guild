# The Glitterdeep Landing

The Glitterdeep Landing is hidden near **Bal Kregger**, tucked into an old mining runoff channel that opens into a concealed underground quay. It specializes in smuggling gemstones, rare metals, forged ownership marks, stolen ledgers, and luxury goods skimmed from noble caravans.

The Landing runs on silence, accounting, and blackmail. Merchants arrive masked, guards are bribed three steps ahead, and every stolen gem passes through cutters who can make its old identity disappear.

---

## Head of Port: Izzara Veyrith

A sharp-dressed dwarf woman with jeweled rings, gold-rimmed spectacles, and a left hand replaced by a delicate crystal prosthetic. She is polite, exacting, and colder than deep stone. Izzara never raises her voice, because by the time she is angry, someone else has already been paid to solve the problem.
