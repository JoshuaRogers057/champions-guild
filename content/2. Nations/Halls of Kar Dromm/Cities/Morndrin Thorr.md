# Morndrin Thorr

Morndrin Thorr is celebration forged in stone. Under Brund's booming voice and bellowing laugh, this city sings, feasts, and thrives. Musicians, bards, and brewers live alongside warriors who prefer song before steel. Here, a dwarf's worth is measured in stories as well as deeds, and no occasion passes without toasts.

*Note: Bardthane [[Brund Flamebrow]] died peacefully in 1021. His funeral drew one of the largest gatherings in Kar Dromm in decades — seven days of ceremony, remembrance, feasting, and song. His seat remains vacant.*

---

## Districts

**Alethrone Hill** — The brewing and performance center. Taverns, amphitheaters, and bardic halls dominate.

**Vaultcross** — Trade nexus for spices, exotic drinks, and bardic artifacts.

**Cindersteps** — Dense, joyful housing with public hearths and open song circles.

**Brassbell** — Central plaza home to the Great Emberbell. Celebrations begin and end here, rain or rockfall.
