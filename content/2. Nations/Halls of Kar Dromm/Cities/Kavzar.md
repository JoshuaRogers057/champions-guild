# Kavzar

Kavzar is the gleaming engine of dwarven precision and magical integration. The Clan of Steel governs here with mechanical discipline and clockwork efficiency. High Forgemaster [[Thrain Steelmantle]] views city planning like crafting a perfect blade — each part has purpose, each flaw is unacceptable. The city reflects the cold, shimmering light of the fragment veins that snake through its walls and streets.

**Fragment Mine:** Kavzar is home to the largest of the three major fragment mines in the dwarven lands.

---

## Districts

**Shardspan Reach** — Glittering residential and administrative quarter overlooking the upper edges of the mine. Homes and halls built from shardstone that hums faintly with magical resonance.

**The Forgeline** — The largest industrial quarter, home to magitech forges, smithies, and engineering workshops. Devices range from rune-fueled hammers to prototype construct limbs.

**Crystalroot Tunnels** — Active mining tunnels spiraling downward into the living crystal matrix. Worked by elite miners known as "Shardborn" who carry enchanted picks and work in silence.

**Vault of the Arcsteel Host** — A sealed underground military zone housing Kavzar's warforged guardians and prototype constructs. Trains steel-clad soldiers, imprints golems, and defends the city from within using shard-powered mechanisms.
