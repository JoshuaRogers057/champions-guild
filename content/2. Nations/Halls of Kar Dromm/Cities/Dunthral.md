# Dunthral

Dunthral thrives on the power of the Pyrestone Channel — a massive deep-earth mine fed by thermal fragment veins. These shards fuel its war machines, constructs, and enchanted suits of power armor. Every forge here is a testing lab.

---

## Districts

**Embervault Core** — Dunthral's literal and symbolic heart. Pyrestone Fragments are fused with technosteel to create experimental weaponry, siege armor, and automatons.

**Ironlace Row** — A volatile zone of innovation where artificers work with fragment-charged materials. Every corner bursts with inventions — some brilliant, some explosive. Test ranges lined with auto-reset runes.

**Sparkreach Citadel** — The training ground for Dunthral's elite artificer-warriors. Fragment-fueled mechs duel in public arenas. Tactical schools teach warcasting and magical sabotage.

**The Pyrestone Channel** — The city's mine and primary fragment source. A lava-vented trench lined with reinforced catwalks and guarded by construct sentinels. Specialized miners known as "ashwalkers" extract and cool the glowing fragments for transport.
