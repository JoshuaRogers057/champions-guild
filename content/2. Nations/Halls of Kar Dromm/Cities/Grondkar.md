# Grondkar

Grondkar is the pulse of the mountain itself. Here, dwarves speak to stone and listen for the groans of deep earth. Under Durna's hammer-like leadership, masons, miners, and seismic seers ensure the kingdom's structural integrity. There is no flash here — just dust, discipline, and unshakable foundations.

---

## Districts

**Quarry Gate** — Massive block extraction and sorting operations. Also hosts mining contests and stoneblessing rituals.

**Hammerdeep** — Home to heavy laborers, builders, and trainee Stonecallers. Unadorned but proud.

**Stonecall Hollow** — Ceremonial pit where earth-songs are sung and seismic readings interpreted in sacred trance.
