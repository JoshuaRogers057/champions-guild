# Drakuz-Dur

Drakuz-Dur is fire and steel incarnate, built into a volcanic caldera and pulsing with raw energy. Flameforge artisans channel molten flows into every aspect of city life. Bardrum is a tempest of a king, forging both weapons and warriors in flame. The city's streets crackle with heat, and its blackstone walls are etched with glowing lava veins. Here, invention often walks the line of danger.

---

## Districts

**Furnace Gate** — The city's main entrance, glowing with warded heat and firesteel arches. Guarded by elite Pyresworn.

**Forgehold Spire** — Spiraled tower where royal forge-altars and the Flameforge throne reside.

**Brimward Alley** — Soot-blackened workshops of inventors, golemwrights, and weapon-testers. Sparks fly day and night.
