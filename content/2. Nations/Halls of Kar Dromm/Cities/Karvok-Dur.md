# Karvok-Dur

Karvok-Dur is a proud bastion of martial might and craftsmanship known as the "Shield of the South." It supplies the kingdom with arms and armor. Thane Hrolgar leads from Steelmarch, where he's often seen training troops or inspecting forge lines. Revered for surviving six sieges and never retreating, his rule is defined by loyalty and hard-earned respect. The Cindergate Crucible lies at the heart of the city, where chosen weapons are tempered in sacred flame.

---

## Districts

**Emberhold** — Industrial forgeworks and smelteries where heat and hammer never stop. Apprentices train in brutal conditions under flame-tempered masters.

**Steelmarch** — Military academy and barracks, where shield-walls, phalanx drills, and battle rites are drilled day and night.

**Crafters' Walk** — A busy avenue for fine smiths, armor detailers, and engravers. Known for masterwork commissions and artisan guild feuds.

**Redstone Hollow** — A red-crystal-lit housing cavern where war heroes and crafters live side by side, surrounded by taverns and shrine-halls.
