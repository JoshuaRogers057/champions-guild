# Bardrith

Perched above an ancient crystal faultline, Bardrith is the dwarves' foremost city for fragment refinement and arcane channeling. Its walls shimmer faintly from fragment residue, and every stone is inscribed with luminous runes to stabilize the raw energy.

**Fragment Mine:** Bardrith is home to one of the three major fragment mines in the dwarven lands.

---

## Districts

**Fragmentgate Commons** — The city's welcoming district and trade center. Features controlled fragment fountains and houses the Bardrith Market Vaults.

**Resonance Hall** — A high-security arcane lab complex where fragments are studied, shaped, and bound into weapons or batteries. Overseen by Runepriests and Elemental Arbiters.

**Echodepth Ward** — Subterranean mines and refinery caverns where raw fragments are excavated and processed. Every mine entrance is rune-locked and guarded.

**Crystal Basilica** — A gleaming temple and research sanctum to Moradin, where religious rites blend with arcane experiments. Rumors speak of fragment-forged golems seen during full moons.
