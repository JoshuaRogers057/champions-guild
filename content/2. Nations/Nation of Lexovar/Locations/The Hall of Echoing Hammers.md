# The Hall of Echoing Hammers

The Hall of Echoing Hammers is a vast monastery-forge carved into the mountains between Lexovar and Kar Dromm. Its halls are part temple, part mine, part workshop, with entire chambers dedicated to forging, construct repair, earth magic, and sacred craftsmanship. The monks honor both [[Gond]] and [[Moradin]], seeing invention and tradition as two hands on the same hammer.

Its defenders are called the **Iron Mantle**, a disciplined militia of smith-monks, earthcasters, and armored construct handlers. They protect the mountain passages and guard a persistent rumor: that somewhere beneath the lower forge-halls is a hidden entrance into the Stone Road.

---

## Forge-Abbot: Forge-Abbot Brannik Gearmantle

A soot-covered rock gnome with thick welding goggles, two clockwork fingers, and a beard braided in the dwarven style out of respect for [[Moradin]]'s faithful. He is cheerful, blunt, and impossible to impress with theory alone. Brannik judges people by what they can make, fix, endure, or admit they got wrong.
