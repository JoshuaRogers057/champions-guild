# Tinker's Hideaway

Tinker's Hideaway is a hidden smuggler port tucked into the lagoon between **Lexovar** and **Kar Dromm**, concealed behind cliffside doors that open only through a mix of illusion, clockwork locks, and tide timing. From the water, it looks like broken stone and sea caves. Inside, it is a cramped lantern-lit harbor packed with illegal prototypes, stolen fragments, and black-market construct parts.

Everyone is welcome, provided they keep the peace. It is not safe because it is lawful. It is safe because violence is answered instantly and without appeal.

---

## Head of Port: Snap

A cross-eyed deep gnome with brass teeth, oil-black gloves, and a habit of finishing other people's sentences incorrectly just to irritate them. He runs Tinker's Hideaway like a workshop with knives hidden under every bench. Snap is funny, paranoid, brilliant, and completely willing to sell someone out if they mistake his jokes for softness.
