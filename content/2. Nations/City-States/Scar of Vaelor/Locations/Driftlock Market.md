# Driftlock Market

Driftlock Market is a smuggler port hidden among unstable sky isles and half-secured sky docks. Cargo moves by glider, rope lift, elemental skiff, and short-range gates, making inspection nearly impossible.

---

## Head of Port: Veyrith Coil

A water genasi broker with silver rings on every finger and a smile that never reaches his eyes. He knows which sky isles will drift where before most pilots do.
