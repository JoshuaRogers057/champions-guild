# The Skyroot Grove

The Skyroot Grove clings to a wild sky isle where trees grow sideways, waterfalls fall into mist, and roots hang into open air like ropes. Its druids tend wind-twisted beasts, storm-birds, cloud serpents, and strange plants that only grow where gravity is unreliable.

---

## Rootcaller: Rootcaller Enathi Galevine

An air genasi druid with pale blue skin, white braids, and a voice that sounds like wind moving through leaves.
