# Scar of Vaelor — Government, Military, Economy & Culture
*Genasi and Elementals of Eryndor*

---

## Government

The city of Vaelor is a loose confederation of people led by the **Circle of Currents** — a council of seven Genasi elders, one representing each elemental aspect: earth, fire, water, and four "wind" seats designated for the north, south, east, and west. Each wind seat is elected by popular acclaim.

Decisions are made communally and enforced by elemental magic and ancient social contracts. No true monarchy or formal nobility exists; leadership is based on merit, magical ability, and one's influence among the fey or elemental courts.

The Circle rarely intervenes in everyday life, mostly mediating disputes between powerful elemental houses or dealing with external threats. The true "law" of Vaelor is survival, freedom, and respect for the Scar's volatile magic.

---

## Military

Vaelor does not field a traditional army. Instead, every citizen is expected to defend their sky-isle if needed.

**Core Defenders** — Genasi warriors and battle-mages capable of manipulating the unstable magic of the Scar to create shields, storms, or earthquakes.

**The Windguard** — A small, elite unit of sky-knights riding elemental beasts like cloud serpents and storm eagles, patrolling the shifting rocks.

**The Shardcallers** — Based out of Vaelor, these are elemental sorcerers trained to "bend" smaller shards of floating stone and terrain, creating ad-hoc defenses or bridges between drifting rocks. They also form temporary bridges between islands for a modest fee.

---

## Economy

Vaelor's economy is a strange one, heavily based on:

**Elemental Crystals** — Rare magical stones only found drifting in the Scar, highly prized across Eryndor for enchantments.

**Skyfishing** — A term used for the harvesting of magical energies and strange flying beasts that drift through the Scar.

**Artificing** — Advanced magical crafting using elemental motes, storm-charged metals, and arcane-infused flora.

**Trade** — Vaelor is a neutral ground for merchants seeking rare magics or illicit goods that cannot be obtained elsewhere.

Travel to and from Vaelor is treacherous. Heavily guarded caravans can make the overland journey to the far southeastern area of Eryndor, but goods brought to and from the Scar itself must be flown — making the journey both difficult and expensive.

---

## Fragment Access

The City-State of Vaelor does not rely on traditional fragments like the rest of the world, nor does it have any mines. The Vaelor instead rely on **Elemental Crystals**, which serve similar purposes but can only be found here.

---

## Culture

Life in Vaelor is unpredictable and vibrant. Survival in a place where gravity shifts, air currents can tear buildings apart, and magic pulses through the rocks fosters a culture that values adaptability above all else. The people of Vaelor prize quick thinking, flexibility, and creative problem-solving.

Songs, tattoos, dances, and elemental displays are important both for storytelling and magical rites. Worship of the **Prime Currents** — elemental forces beyond mortal comprehension — is common, but there is no formal pantheon.

---

## Religion

Small shrines to elemental lords, forgotten gods, or even chaotic spirits are scattered across the floating rocks. Faith is personal and often tied to a family's elemental heritage.

---

## Geography

The Scar of Vaelor is a colossal wound in the world. Some theories say it was once a portal to another dimension that exploded. Others say it was a powerful graviturgy spell that went wrong. No one knows for certain what happened. Today, the Scar of Vaelor is one of the most unique places in Eryndor.

The shattered remains of a mountain range violently exploded into a churning sea of floating rock that now stays suspended over the crater where the mountains once stood. Massive floating, drifting, and rotating rocks — some dozens of miles in circumference, others as small as homes or wagons — float in the sky, defying both logic and gravity.

The Scar is frequently covered in mist, with broken waterfalls falling into nothing, their springs mysteriously never ending.

The city of Vaelor itself sprawls across dozens of floating rocks connected by rope bridges, arcane gates, and elemental pathways. Across the floating mountains, remnants from an age long past remain — unexplored temples, caves, and ruins on both inhabited and uninhabited islands.

---

## Mercenaries & Criminal Organizations

**The Riftblades** — Sky-bound mercenaries who hire out as guards, bounty hunters, and "drift divers" exploring deeper into the Scar.

**The Mistbound** — A shadowy network of smugglers and relic-hunters who use the chaotic geography to avoid law enforcement.

---

## Grudges & Political Strain

Vaelor's independence is a sore point with many larger nations who crave its elemental resources. But invasion and occupation is nearly impossible from a logistics standpoint.

The Drakmir Dominion and certain human duchies have tried — and failed — to establish "trade stations" that have been suspiciously fortified. Each attempt has failed.

Nobody truly knows what caused the Scar of Vaelor. Every culture has a different story: a mage war, a god's death, a shattered plane leaking into the world. The people of Vaelor embrace the mystery and the danger, which makes them loath to surrender their independence.

## Notable NPCs

**Eryssa of the Shifting Wind** — Air Genasi elder, current leader of the Circle of Currents.

**Kaelen Thundershade** — A half-fey, half-fire genasi stormrider, famous for dueling an entire pirate crew in a lightning storm.

**Mira Thornwell** — An enigmatic "songmage" who claims to speak to the Scar itself.
