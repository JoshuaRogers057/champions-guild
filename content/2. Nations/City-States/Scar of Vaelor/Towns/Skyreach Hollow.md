# Skyreach Hollow

**Leader:** Matron [[Seris Windgleam]]
**Function:** Weather harvesting and sky-charting

**Unique Feature:** The Tempest Loom — an arcane observatory that captures and stores storm energy

---

Perched along the ragged edge of a crescent-shaped floating ridge, Skyreach Hollow clings to altitude where air thins and the clouds spiral like living creatures. Shimmering cables of raw lightning are siphoned from passing storms and funneled into crystal-tipped spires. Homes are domed structures reinforced with cloudstone — a porous material that hums during storms. The town glows pale blue during electrical surges, and weather sages walk the cliffs barefoot, reading the sky like scripture.
