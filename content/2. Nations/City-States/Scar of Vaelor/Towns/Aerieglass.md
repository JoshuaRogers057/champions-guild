# Aerieglass

**Leader:** High-Seer Lunira
**Function:** Divination sanctum and star-magic nexus

**Unique Feature:** Transparent glass bridges spanning a hollow spire — during certain eclipses, the reflection shows a different world entirely

---

Aerieglass is a wonder suspended on a hollow spire piercing the sky like a divine spear. The entire town is built in concentric rings of open platforms connected by enchanted glass walkways that shimmer under moonlight and glow during celestial alignments inside the spire. The outside of the spire is battered by winds. Visitors often take a few days to adjust to the constant spinning of the spire. Dreamers and prophets live here, watching constellations dance in the mirror-sky above and below. It is said that during certain eclipses, the reflection shows a different world entirely — and some who stare too long vanish.
