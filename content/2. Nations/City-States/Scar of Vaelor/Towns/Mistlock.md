# Mistlock

**Leader:** Commander [[Ryvek Thornwake]]
**Function:** Elemental Crystal transport and logistics

**Unique Feature:** Secured by enchanted chains and gravity clamps — central docking station for trade into and out of the Scar

---

Mistlock floats at the intersection of several major sky-lanes used by traders, smugglers, and island surveyors. Constructed across several massive stone platforms chained together by thick iron links etched with sigils of stabilization, the town creaks and groans as it gently shifts in altitude. A central docking station processes all trade into or out of the [[Scar of Vaelor]].
