# Driftstone Haven

**Leader:** Artisan [[Korran Flintbrace]]
**Function:** Stone-shaping and levitation rune research

**Unique Feature:** Rotating ring-shaped floating island with its own daily cycle of dawn, dusk, and eclipse

---

Driftstone Haven floats on a perfectly circular landmass that slowly spins around its vertical axis, creating a unique approach to time. It has its own daily cycle of dawn, dusk, and eclipse. The structures here are built into the rock itself, shaped by rune-forging masons who etch levitation glyphs into everything from bridges to bedframes. The central plaza has no roof — instead, it showcases a hanging boulder suspended by pure arcane pressure. Local children practice carving miniature islands that float like birds in a pond.
