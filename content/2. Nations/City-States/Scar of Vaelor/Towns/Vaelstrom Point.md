# Vaelstrom Point

**Leader:** Warden Elthis Vaelshade
**Function:** Sky-beast patrol and aerial defense

**Unique Feature:** Aether Talon roosts for aerial mounts — massive, sleek, feathered reptiles

---

A jagged network of free-floating plateaus and natural stone arches joined by flexible rope bridges. Vaelstrom Point is home to elite beast-riders and cliff sentinels. Aether Talons — massive, sleek, feathered reptiles — nest in engineered perches built into the wind-exposed rock faces. The town has no central road system; instead, bridges are temporary, retractable, and aerial access is preferred. Perpetual gusts shriek through chimes made of giant bone and crystal, and every warrior here is trained from youth to leap before they walk.
