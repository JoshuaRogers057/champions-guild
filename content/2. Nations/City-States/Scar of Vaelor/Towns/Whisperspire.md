# Whisperspire

**Leader:** Whisper-Monk [[Velian Sura]]
**Function:** Scout and spy training

**Unique Feature:** Acoustics where a whisper at the base carries to the summit but a scream fades before it leaves your throat

---

Whisperspire coils upward like a spiral staircase carved into a lone obsidian tower, covered in vines of white blossoms that bloom only in fog. Its acoustics are eerie — a whisper at the base can be heard at the summit, but a scream fades before it leaves your throat. The town serves as a monastery, training agents in infiltration, silence, and stillness. Meditation gardens float on nearby pebbles, and coded messages are passed via hummingbirds trained to navigate the winds.
