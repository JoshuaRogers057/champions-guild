# Dreadwake — Government, Military, Culture & Geography
*City of a Thousand Hulls*

---

## Government

Dreadwake is a floating fortress of chaos — a sprawling city built atop the magically lashed hulls of a thousand ships, cursed wrecks, and relic barges. Set adrift upon the Sea, it rides the tides as a drifting kingdom of pirates, rogues, and sea-damned survivors. Held aloft by unstable arcane forces and the will of its most feared captains, Dreadwake is lawless, alive, and constantly evolving. Its decks shift with every storm. Its bridges creak, rot, and are rebuilt daily. Its laws are written in blood. Each ship that joins or leaves the Dreadwake changes its topography.

### The Council of Twenty
A cutthroat confederacy of the twenty most powerful pirate factions in the world. Each faction rules a portion of the city's hulls, commands a fleet, and holds one vote. In theory, they govern Dreadwake together. In practice, they brawl, bribe, and backstab — unless their city is under threat.

At the head of the Council sits the **Pirate King**, elected by the factions to rule for as long as they can hold the title. Most often only a few years before mutiny, betrayal, or a well-aimed cannon ends their reign.

**Current Pirate King: Rake Bloodhorn** — A towering minotaur warlord with storm-branded red horns and a voice like a crashing wave. Rake wields a glaive made from a sea serpent's spine and wears the crown of anchor chain and bone.

### Defense and Warfare
Dreadwake's greatest defense is unity through chaos — no navy can predict it, and no city defends with more cruelty. A permanent fog surrounds the city, making any assault by unfamiliar forces foolish at best.

**Chainshot Legion** — Mercenary siege engineers and gun crews.
**Mariner Mages** — Spellcasters bound by pact or promise, defending key hulls.
**Ghost Wardens** — Revenants and sea-wraiths that guard the oldest decks.
**The Rotted Fleet** — A half-sunken armada of cursed ships that rise at the necromancers' call.

## Culture

Freedom is Law. Every pirate is free to choose for themselves, but the consequence is never far behind.

### The Pirate's Code
- *"Don't kill the cook."*
- *"Plunder is earned, not begged."*
- *"Mutiny is legal. Losing is fatal."*
- *"The dead don't get votes."*

Officers get 2 shares, sailors get 1, Dreadwake gets 1.

### Festivals
**The Blood Wake** — City-wide race and combat tournament.
**The Gilded Nail** — Captains swear temporary allegiance by driving a nail into the Blackmire Mast. The allegiance is always to a single purpose.
**The Rumlight Vigil** — A night of silence, candles, and sea shanties for those who never came back.

---

## Religion

**Saltspire Row** is a row of buildings with a place of worship for every deity no matter who it may be. Many sailors worship many different things — sea hags, krakens, forgotten gods, and for others, the sea itself.

## Geography

### Key Features

**The Tideheart** — A glowing arcane core beneath the city's center, fused to the keel of the first pirate ship struck down by divine wrath. Protected by oathbound guardians.

**The Blackmire Mast** — A cursed central mast — lighthouse, gallows, and dueling pole all in one.

**Stormroot Arena** — Gladiatorial pit for pirates to resolve disputes by blade, beast, or ballad.

**Gravewake Below** — The flooded undercity — home to monsters, smugglers, and the half-drowned.

**The Dead Drop** — A vertical shaft of swinging ropes, collapsing decks, and freefall. Shortest route across the city — for those who survive.

### Districts

**Main Deck** — Central bazaar. Home to gambling dens, taverns, tattooists, armories, and general anarchy.

**Hulltown** — Residential wreckage stacked five decks high. A maze of hammocks, pulleys, and personal grudges.

**Saltspire Row** — Place of worship.

**The Upper Rigging** — Rope bridges and sail-canopies forming the high-city for elite pirates and spies.

**The Bilge** — Prison, sewer, dumping ground. Also home to the Siltfang smugglers and Coil-touched cults.
