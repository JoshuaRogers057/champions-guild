# The Coiled Stillness

The Coiled Stillness is a swamp monastery hidden among drowned trees, warm mist, and half-sunken stone ruins. Its monks train in patience, poison resistance, silent movement, and sudden violence. They teach that the strongest strike is the one the victim understands only after it has already landed.

---

## Stillmaster: Stillmaster Coil-Eye (Veshka)

A yuan-ti monk with pale green scales, gold eyes, and a voice so calm it sounds rehearsed. She does not threaten. She simply waits for fear to do its work.
