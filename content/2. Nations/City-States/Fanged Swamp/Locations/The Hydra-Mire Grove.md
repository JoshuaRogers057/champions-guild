# The Hydra-Mire Grove

The Hydra-Mire Grove is a brutal druid grove deep in the Fang Swamp, devoted to hydras, swamp predators, venomous plants, and the endless cycle of hunger and regrowth. Its druids tend sacred pools where young hydras are watched, fed, scarred, and interpreted as living omens.

---

## Scale-Seer: Scale-Seer Nathra Vossk

A yuan-ti druid with bone charms, black-green robes, and a staff crowned with hydra teeth. She believes every head cut away teaches the body how to survive.
