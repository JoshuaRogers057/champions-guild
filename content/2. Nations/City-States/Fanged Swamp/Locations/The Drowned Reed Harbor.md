# The Drowned Reed Harbor

The Drowned Reed Harbor is the Fang Swamp's smuggler port, built on rotten platforms, hidden canals, and reed-covered waterways that vanish under fog.

---

## Head of Port: Old Moss Grinn

A hunched bullywug smuggler with milky eyes, gold rings, and a laugh like mud bubbling. He knows which canals are safe, which are watched, and which ones eat boats.
