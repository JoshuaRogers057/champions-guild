# Herds of Elora — Government, Military, Culture & Geography
*A wandering nation of centaurs and satyrs*

---

## Government

The Herds of Elora do not follow a centralized government. Instead, they are bound by a shared tradition of remembrance and respect. Twice a year, during the summer and winter equinoxes, the centaurs and satyrs gather in sacred sites across the land to share what they have seen, heard, and learned. These equinox gatherings serve as both cultural renewal and informal governance.

At the heart of this structure is the **Council of the Wise Ones** — a small group of the oldest and most respected satyrs and centaurs. These individuals serve as living archives, responsible for memorizing, preserving, and interpreting the collective histories, songs, and legends of the Herds. They are not rulers in the political sense, but are looked to for guidance, judgment, and wisdom.

The Wise Ones are protected and escorted by an elite group called the **Hearth Guard**. Members are selected for their loyalty, strength, and ability to navigate the wilds. The Hearth Guard ensures the Wise Ones are kept safe as they travel from one gathering site to another.

There is no permanent capital or central seat of power. Each equinox, the Wise Ones name the location of the next gathering, and the Herds converge there with song, memory, and purpose.

---

## Military

While the Herds of Elora are largely peaceful, they are not without strength. Centaurs often form bands of mercenary cavalry, offering their speed and precision to cities and nations in exchange for coin, supplies, or access to libraries and records. These centaur warriors favor longspears, heavy bows, and swift, coordinated charges.

Satyrs contribute through their roles as support specialists — battlefield healers and magical performers who use bardic enchantments to confuse enemies and empower allies.

The **Hearth Guard**, a mixed force of centaurs and satyrs, serves a unique martial function. Their only duty is to protect the Wise Ones, ensuring that the living memory of Elora is never lost to violence or disaster. They are few in number but highly skilled, trained in both combat and survival.

Combat is never the first solution in Elora. But when the Herds must fight, they do so with clarity, unity, and purpose.

## Culture

The culture of Elora is centered on the act of remembering. To witness and preserve is seen as the highest calling. Every centaur and satyr carries a responsibility to observe the world, gather its stories, and eventually return them to the Wise Ones. This makes every traveler a potential historian, every performer a recorder of truth, and every campfire a chance to preserve a fragment of time.

Centaurs often serve as couriers, moving swiftly across the continent with messages, songs, or important items. They are also the backbone of Elora's martial tradition, known for their endurance and honor. Satyrs embrace a more improvisational lifestyle, often playing music in taverns, gathering rumors, and healing those in need.

The Herds do not live in fixed settlements. Instead, they drift from place to place, setting up temporary camps in forests, ruined castles, or wide valleys. Freedom is sacred. The wind chooses their next path, and the road welcomes it.

Elorans do not dwell on sorrow, though they carry it. They do not hoard wealth, though they may treasure a story or a song above all else. Performance is not only entertainment — it is testimony.

---

## Religion

The Herds of Elora revere **Deneir**, the god of writing, record-keeping, and the preservation of knowledge. To the Elorans, Deneir is not just a divine figure — he is a symbol of purpose. Every act of storytelling, every song passed down, every tale recited under starlight is an offering to him.

Shrines to Deneir take the form of inked stones, engraved trees, or scroll-marked banners carried by scribes and elders. Some satyrs carry necklaces strung with miniature quills or empty scroll tubes as symbols of their devotion. Equinox gatherings always begin with an invocation to Deneir, asking that the stories shared will not be forgotten.

The act of remembrance is itself an act of worship.

---

## Geography

Elora is a nation without walls. It forms only twice a year at one of their sacred meeting places. The people travel freely, leaving only faint traces behind — campfire rings, carved markers, or etched stones that tell who came and what they saw.

Equinox gatherings take place in sacred locations known for their acoustics, openness, or history. Some sites are natural amphitheaters. Others are stone circles or moss-covered ruins believed to be blessed by Deneir himself.

Centaurs prefer the open roads and broad plains, ranging for months at a time across the lowlands. Satyrs enjoy wooded trails, hidden glades, and even time among cityfolk, playing for inns or gathering new tales before returning to the wild.

Some seasonal strongholds exist — ruined castles or long-abandoned keeps that serve as temporary archives and gathering points. These are rarely occupied for more than a few weeks each year.

## Notable NPCs

**Eronas Clearstep** — An elder centaur with a powerful presence and a voice like a mountain breeze. Senior member of the Wise Ones, he has personally committed over two hundred songs and legends to memory. He carries a carved walking staff and speaks only when his words can shape direction.

**Myli Threetune** — A satyr matriarch known for her vibrant laugh and ancient melodies. A composer, historian, and mentor to many traveling bards. She has a magical harp said to echo the voices of past performers.

**Varen Softstride** — A quiet, thoughtful centaur once famous as a courier across dangerous terrain. Now retired due to an old injury, he devotes his time to transcribing stories and training young messengers.

**Elizia Brightleaf** — A satyr healer and scholar of memory magic. She oversees the health and wellness of the Wise Ones and uses music as a healing tool. Considered one of the most insightful voices in the council.

**Commander [[Karnos Flamebrand]]** — A towering, broad-shouldered centaur standing nearly eight feet tall with a dark ash-gray coat and braided mane streaked with copper thread. His upper body is adorned with scaled leather armor patterned with ancient runes of protection and memory. A branded sigil of [[Deneir]] marks his chest. Stern, silent, and impossible to intimidate, Karnos is a living myth among the Herds. He is a mobile juggernaut who charges into battle with overwhelming force, wielding his glaive in sweeping arcs and using his hooves to trample foes that dare close the gap. When the Wise Ones sleep, it is because Karnos is nearby.
