# Sanama — Government, Military, Culture & Geography
*The Prides of Leonin and Tabaxi*

---

## Government

Sanama does not follow a traditional government. It is led by Matrons and Patriarchs of the most powerful prides who guide the movements and agreements of Sanama as needed. Each pride is its own sovereign body, governed from within by tradition and respect. When major decisions must be made, the five Great Prides gather in the **Circle of Flame** to speak.

Each pride is led by either a **Crown** — a male leonin selected for his strength and leadership — or a **Sage** — a female tabaxi revered for her wisdom and insight. These leaders do not vote. They speak. Decisions are made through challenge, argument, and demonstration. Once agreement is reached, it is followed by all.

There are no taxes, no written laws. What binds Sanama together is pride, legacy, and the certainty that honor cannot be broken without consequence.

## Military

Every adult in Sanama is a warrior. Combat is a way of life. Children are raised learning to track, fight, and survive. When a threat appears, the response is swift and without hesitation.

Leonin form the front lines in most conflicts. They fight with claws, spears, ironwood clubs, or scavenged steel. Their ability to adapt makes them the most trusted mercenaries in the region.

Tabaxi take on more specialized roles — trackers, scouts, archers, and quiet killers in the night. Light on their feet and sharp in their thinking, they are the eyes and ears of Sanama's forces.

Sanama prides often take on mercenary work and bounty contracts. These jobs are never assigned — they are accepted freely. Once accepted, they are completed with complete dedication. To be hunted by a Sanama pride is to be caught. Or at the very least, to be fought.

## Culture

Sanama culture is about giving everything in the moment. Whether it is a debate, a duel, a game, or a hunt, the people of Sanama never hold back. They do not cling to past victories. They live in the now. Once the contest is over, they move on. Always forward. Always fierce.

They are hunters and gatherers. They do not plant and they do not till. When the game disappears, so do they. Their homes are temporary, made of leather and bone, shaped into great circular tents that rise and fall with the seasons. When weather allows, they sleep under the stars.

Every pride holds tightly to family. Males, upon coming of age, are expected to leave their birth pride and find a new one, often by proving their worth. Females remain, helping raise the next generation. The young are protected above all else — harm one cub, and you make an enemy of every Sanama.

The city of Sanama is made up primarily of the injured or the elderly who cannot travel. If it is ever threatened, all prides stop their activity and unite for either defense or vengeance.

## Religion

The people of Sanama are not a religious people. Nearly every one resists the gods. They do not deny their existence — they feel the gods are capricious and fickle, thus undeserving of worship. Legend tells that long ago the Sanama peoples were used bitterly by the Aasimar in the name of the gods, and to this day the leonin and aasimar are far from friends.

The Sanama do not build temples or recite doctrine. But they do revere the sun, the land, and the act of challenge itself. Some prides make offerings to the spirits of past warriors. Others whisper to the wind before the hunt.

To overcome hardship is sacred. To face death with honor is divine. That is their faith.

When someone dies, they are either burned in the fire of the Circle or buried beneath a sunstone, their name sung one last time before being passed into memory.

---

## Geography

Sanama exists in the savanna of the Drakmir Dominion. It is not a fixed city — it is a region. A place of sun-bleached earth, roaming herds, and endless sky. Where the prides gather becomes the city. When they leave, the city disappears like a shadow in the grass.

The Drakmir Dominion once tried to control the region but quickly learned the futility of it. Now they allow the Sanama to range freely, recognizing their value as monster hunters and enforcers.

Ancient fire pits, stone rings, and old bones mark the places Sanama has settled before. These are sacred sites. The city might appear near them again. Or it might appear somewhere new.

## Notable NPCs

**Crown [[Surok of Pride Asua]]** — A massive leonin known for his calm presence and deep voice. His mane is thick with braids and beads from past victories. Surok believes in strength guided by patience. He speaks rarely but his words carry weight.

**Sage [[Vel of Pride Gertua]]** — A silver-furred tabaxi with a sharp tongue and quicker claws. [[Vel]] handles negotiations with precision and oversees many of the mercenary contracts taken by Sanama. Her laughter is as cutting as her blade.

**Crown [[Rhogar of Pride Amoro]]** — Younger than the other leaders, Rhogar leads through action. He hunts the biggest monsters, fights the boldest battles, and inspires through sheer presence. His ironwood club is named Skybreaker.

**Sage [[Thassa of Pride Zadha]]** — An elder tabaxi with a painted face and dark robes. She speaks little, listens much, and sees more than most. Her pride is known for precision kills and silent hunts.

**Crown [[Baraz of Pride Nagoda]]** — A weathered lionin with battle scars across his chest and back. He carries a shield said to be made from the bones of a great lion. His gaze can silence a campfire. He does not forgive easily.
