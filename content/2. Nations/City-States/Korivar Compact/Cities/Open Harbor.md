# Open Harbor

**Founding Line:** Stendar Line
**Leader:** Corila Stendar
**Primary Role:** Diplomacy, trade, law, shipwrighting

---

The Stendar line ensures the Compact's political survival. Their city hosts neutral arbitration courts, foreign embassies, and oath-forged trade halls. Stendar diplomats train in negotiation magic, truth-binding contracts, cultural fluency, and witness rites designed to prevent treaty manipulation.

Thanks to Stendar influence, the Korivar Compact is recognized globally as a sovereign nation rather than an outlaw enclave.

Corila Stendar is renowned for her grace, insight, and extraordinary diplomatic skill.
