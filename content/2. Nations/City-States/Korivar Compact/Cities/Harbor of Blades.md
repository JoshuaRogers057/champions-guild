# Harbor of Blades

**Founding Line:** Bakara Line
**Leader:** High Admiral [[Namor Bakara]]
**Primary Role:** Naval warfare and defense

---

Bakara is the military spine of the Compact. Its fortified port houses elite shipyards, watchtowers, and layered coastal defenses. Bakara sailors specialize in archery, boarding actions, mage-assisted combat, and coastal denial strategies.

Their fleets prioritize speed, endurance, and overwhelming ranged dominance.

Bakara philosophy emphasizes vigilance and deterrence. Service requirements here are twelve years — two more than the rest of the Compact. The military and civil sectors are fully integrated; all administrators, guards, and law enforcers are active military personnel. Desertion and mutiny are capital offenses.
