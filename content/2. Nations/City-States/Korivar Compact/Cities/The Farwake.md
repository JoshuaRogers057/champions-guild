# The Farwake

**Founding Line:** Naskra Line
**Leader:** Zeffina Naskra
**Primary Role:** Exploration and intelligence

---

Naskra captains push into uncharted waters, mapping unstable coastlines, magical anomalies, and lands beyond known frontiers. Many never return. Those who do are quietly interrogated, and their discoveries are not always shared with the rest of the Council.

Naskra is essential yet unsettling — rumored to know threats the Compact has not yet faced.

Zeffina Naskra has served on the Council for a century and is believed to carry knowledge no other living Korivari possesses.
