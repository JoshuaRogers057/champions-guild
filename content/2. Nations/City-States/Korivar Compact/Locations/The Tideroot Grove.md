# The Tideroot Grove

The Tideroot Grove grows along Korivar's sheltered inlets, where mangroves, sea grass, coral shelves, and tidal pools form a living barrier around the coast. Its druids watch fish migrations, reef health, nesting sea birds, and the strange magical currents that shift around the island.

---

## Tidekeeper: Tidekeeper Ilyra Naskra

A quiet Korivari druid with dark sea-green eyes, salt-white braids, and hands stained from coral work and healing salves.
