# The Stormglass Vigil

The Stormglass Vigil is a coastal monastery built into one of Korivar's sea cliffs, where monks train in balance, endurance, naval discipline, and weather awareness. Their students practice on rain-slick stone, narrow rigging lines, cliff ladders, and ship decks during violent weather.

---

## Vigil-Master: Vigil-Master Oskara Maeshowe

A lean Korivari woman with sea-gray hair, storm tattoos across her arms, and a voice like a captain giving final orders.
