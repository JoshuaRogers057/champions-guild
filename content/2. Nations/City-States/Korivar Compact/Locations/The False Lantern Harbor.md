# The False Lantern Harbor

The False Lantern Harbor is Korivar's smuggler port, hidden behind misleading beacon lights, reef channels, and tide routes that only local pilots can safely navigate.

---

## Head of Port: Rellan 'Lowlight' Stendar

A charming Korivari smuggler with polished manners, a crooked smile, and a perfect memory for debts. He speaks like a diplomat, lies like a sailor, and never lights the same lantern twice.
