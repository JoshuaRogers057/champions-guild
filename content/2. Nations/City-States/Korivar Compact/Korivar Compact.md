# Korivar Compact — Government, Military, Culture & Geography
*Masters of the Sea, Children of No Nation*

The Korivar Compact is a sovereign island city-state formed three hundred years ago by exiles of mixed elven and human blood. When the great elven and human nations severed all political and cultural ties, those of blended lineage were stripped of inheritance, protection, and citizenship. Rather than scatter, these outcasts followed [[Tessana Korivar]] into self-imposed exile and founded a nation capable of surviving through mastery of the sea.

Today, the Korivar Compact controls some of the most dangerous and economically vital sea lanes in Eryndor. Their sailors, mages, and chartmakers are unmatched, and their neutrality makes them indispensable to global diplomacy and trade. The people of the Compact call themselves Korivari — not half-elves, not mixed bloods, but a unified people defined by oath, voyage, and shared survival.

---

## Tessana Korivar — Founder of the Compact

Tessana Korivar was neither queen nor conqueror. She was a navigator and mediator whose mixed lineage made her unacceptable to both elven and human powers. When exile became law, she chose unity over fragmentation.

She gathered sailors, mages, scholars, traders, and soldiers and led them to an island long considered too dangerous for permanent settlement. There, she bound four powerful family lines into a single confederation — the Korivar Compact.

Tessana rejected hereditary rulership. Instead, she established a system of shared responsibility and mutual restraint. Late in life, she vanished mysteriously; whether she died, transcended, or simply walked away remains unresolved and culturally intentional.

Her doctrine endures:

> *"We will not beg land from nations that deny our blood. Whatever the challenge, we have one mandate: survive. No matter the cost."*

## Government

The Korivar Compact consists of four independent port-cities, each governed by a founding family line. These families form the ruling **Compact Council**, which oversees:

- War and defense
- Foreign treaties
- Trade regulation
- Sea-lane control
- Use of storm and weather magic

Each city governs its own internal affairs, but Compact law supersedes local law. No single family may rule alone — a minimum of three families must agree on any Compact-level decision.

## The Obelisk of Korivar

At the center of the island stands the **Obelisk of Korivar**, a colossal monolith attributed to Tessana herself. Its true origin, age, and construction defy explanation. The stone bears no tool marks, resists magical analysis, and rests upon no foundation — excavations reveal nothing but smooth stone descending into darkness.

When an authorized circle of weather-mages channels through the Obelisk, it summons immense, targeted storms capable of reshaping sea-lanes, crushing hostile fleets, or shielding the Compact behind walls of lethal weather. Activation destabilizes atmospheric pressure, realigns winds, shifts wave patterns miles offshore, and can generate rogue waves with terrifying precision.

The cost is severe: mages suffer exhaustion, accelerated aging, temporary loss of magical ability, and occasionally death. Unauthorized magic near the structure is punishable by exile or execution.

Every major nation monitors the Obelisk closely. When it awakens, the world notices.

## Culture and Identity

Korivari identity is defined by resilience, adaptability, and loyalty to the Compact. **Citizenship is earned through service — not birth.** Every adult must complete ten years of service (twelve in Bakara) to receive full rights.

Non-Korivari may become citizens through the same process, including binding oaths of fealty. Those who complete only part of their service or intentionally avoid it are known as **freebooters** — individuals with limited rights who often gravitate toward unregulated sailing, mercenary work, or piracy. Not all freebooters are criminals, but none are treated as full citizens.

To mark their years of service, many Korivari wear ear loops — one for each completed year — while others mark service with tattoos, scars, or rings.

The Compact's food culture is highly diverse, reflecting its role as a crossroads of trade. The most iconic dishes involve local seafood prepared with intense foreign spices brought by travelers.

The outside world holds mixed views of the Compact. Some nations resent its control of vital sea-lanes but rely on its neutrality and navigation charts. Merchants depend on its safety routes. Pirates fear the Obelisk's storms.

## Criminal Underworld — The Guild of Blades

*Type: Assassins' Guild | Status: Quietly acknowledged, publicly condemned | Headquarters: Unknown*

[[The Guild of Blades]] is an infamous, fully illegal assassins' guild believed to operate somewhere within the Compact. Its existence is a poorly kept secret — everyone knows the Guild exists, but no one knows its location, membership, or structure.

The Guild does not advertise its services, negotiate publicly, or engage in chaotic violence. Instead, it performs rare, precise assassinations that seem to target political threats, criminal rivals, foreign infiltrators, or individuals whose elimination prevents greater conflict.

Their calling card is a single sliver of sharpened sea-glass placed on or near the body. No signature. No message. Just a quiet warning.

Rumors speak of a leader known only as **The Regent**, supported by elite killers called **Blades**, and a network of unaware informants referred to as **Shrouds**. Whether these titles are real or deliberate misinformation is unknown.

The Compact declares the Guild illegal and aggressively denies any and all association. Still, investigations into the Guild often end abruptly, if they start at all. Outside nations regard the Guild with a mix of fear and frustration. No one can prove anything. The Guild's continued survival remains one of the world's most enduring mysteries.
