# Free City of Spens — Government, Military, Culture & Geography
*A refuge where coin speaks louder than blood and everyone has something to prove.*

---

## Government

Spens is ruled by the ancient elven family known as **House Vaerendel**, once of Sylvandar, now exiled and self-made sovereigns of the southeastern peninsula. Many generations ago, they were dismissed from the high elven courts for advocating forbidden ideas — chief among them, the right of all people to shape their own fate. This idea led to the modern movement the elves now consider taboo. They were cast out, but they built something new. What began as a haven for criminals and castoffs is now a full city-state with a population of nearly 60,000.

The Vaerendels rule from the **Highstone Seat**, a marble citadel overlooking the sea, surrounded by open-air markets and sun-swept streets. They do not command armies, but they control the law. Trade, contracts, and movement are tightly monitored through the **Trade Code of Spens** — a simple but strict system based on personal accountability.

There are no taxes on goods, no limitations on what can be bought or sold, but heavy penalties for breaking oaths or interrupting commerce.

Beneath the ruling family, a **Free Council of Merchants, Sailwrights, and Architects** holds monthly sessions to voice concerns and propose infrastructure projects. The Vaerendels are not obligated to listen, but they often do. After all, Spens survives on momentum, not force.

---

## Military

Spens has a minimal standing army called the **Defense Force**, paid well by the city and House Vaerendel. They maintain order and defend the city in times of need.

Additionally, the city employs professional security companies, bounty hunters, and privateers whose loyalty is bought with good coin and public recognition. The largest of these is the **Stonebrand Accord**, a permanent mercenary company under contract to defend Spens' ports and high seas.

The docks are also protected by independent shipmasters who answer to the Trade Code. If a vessel is attacked while under the Spens banner, retaliation is swift and brutal. The message is simple: trade must flow.

In times of true crisis, House Vaerendel can call on old allies from across Eryndor, bound by favors, debts, or past sanctuary. Spens is a city of past sins and owed promises. When it bleeds, those debts come due.

## Culture

Spens is a city of survivors, schemers, artists, fugitives, and fortune-seekers. Its streets echo with dozens of dialects, and every face carries a story. There are no noble houses here beyond the Vaerendels. Reputation is earned by deed, not birth.

Freedom is more than a principle in Spens. It is a lifestyle. People come to escape something — debt, shame, judgment, exile. Here, those things are washed away by salt air and fresh opportunity.

No one asks where you came from. They only care what you're offering.

Art thrives in Spens. Painted murals, open-air theaters, and sculpted plazas fill the city. Trade festivals mark every change of season. Songwriters and street poets become local celebrities.

Citizens respect law, but they fear exile. Being banished from Spens is worse than death to many — it means losing the one place that accepted them, and most have nowhere else they can go.

---

## Religion

Spens allows all religions but enforces none. Shrines and temples exist throughout the city, many of them privately built and maintained. The only law is that no church may interfere with the trade of another. Zealotry is discouraged, and religious disputes are often settled in court.

The two most commonly worshiped deities are **Tymora** for luck and **Waukeen** for profit. Faith in Spens is quiet, practical, and personal.

## Geography

Spens is located on the far southeastern peninsula of Eryndor, where warm sea winds brush the stone-white cliffs and wide beaches. The city is built in tiers, with the Highstone Seat overlooking the lower markets and harbors. The architecture blends styles from across the continent — dwarven stonework, elven arches, gnomish lifts, and halfling gardens.

### Districts

**Highstone District** — Home to House Vaerendel, elite courts, and embassies. Protected and reserved but never fully walled off from the rest of the city.

**The Open Steps** — A massive stone amphitheater and plaza district where public debates, art shows, duels, and festivals are held. The cultural heart of Spens.

**The Gilded Veins** — The financial and mercantile center. Massive trade halls, currency exchanges, and guild houses operate here day and night. Everything has a price, and nearly anything can be found.

**Tidegate Harbor** — The port that made Spens what it is. A wide, curved bay filled with merchant ships, pleasure vessels, and guarded trade warehouses. Harbor masters rule the docks with iron contracts.

**Saltreach** — A lower district filled with taverns, inns, and boarding houses. This is where most new arrivals begin their time in Spens. Loud, crowded, and pulsing with stories.

## Notable NPCs

**Matriarch [[Elyra Vaerendel]]** — The quiet, calculating ruler of Spens. She sees opportunity where others see chaos. Her presence is subtle, and she rarely gives public addresses, but her word shapes the city more than any blade or ballot.

**Vaelin Vaerendel** — Elyra's brother and master of coin. Flamboyant and always dressed for attention, but his mind is razor sharp. The unofficial face of Spens' foreign relations and trusted by the merchant houses.

**Nimire Vaerendel** — Archivist of Spens and interpreter of the Trade Code. She rarely appears in public but is said to know every active contract, every court case, and every political shift in the city. Those who break their word often find her watching.

**Captain [[Thorne Marrek]]** — Commander of the Stonebrand Accord, the city's primary defense force. A former sellsword who now trains and leads mercenaries under Spens' banner. Loyal only to his contract and his pride.

**Priestess [[Malera Sann]]** — High Oracle of [[Waukeen]] and one of the most respected voices in the city's spiritual life. She advises the Vaerendels when asked, and collects coins from merchants not as donations, but as investments in fortune.

**Commander [[Arven Drell]]** — Leader of the Spens Defense Force. A no-nonsense human veteran who rose from the Saltreach slums. Wears chainmail even off duty and believes in fast action over politics.

**Marshal [[Kaera Thorne]]** — Commander of the Stonebrand Accord. A half-orc mercenary captain known for brutal efficiency and iron discipline. Never breaks a contract. Keeps her troops clean, quiet, and deadly. Feared and respected in equal measure.
