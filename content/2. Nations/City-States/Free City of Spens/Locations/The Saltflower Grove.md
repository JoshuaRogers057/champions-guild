# The Saltflower Grove

The Saltflower Grove grows along the warm coast outside **Spens**, where sea grass, flowering vines, wind-bent trees, and cliffside gardens overlook the harbor. Its druids tend refugee gardens, medicinal plants, coastal birds, fishing grounds, and the fragile green spaces that keep the city from becoming all stone and coin.

---

## Greenwarden: Greenwarden Elira Mosswake

A calm human druid with sun-browned skin, salt-gray hair, and a habit of speaking to plants before people. She is kind, but not naive. She has seen too many desperate people mistake survival for permission.
