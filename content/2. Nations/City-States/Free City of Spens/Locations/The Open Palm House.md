# The Open Palm House

The Open Palm House is a public monastery in **Spens**, built for exiles, debtors, refugees, former criminals, failed nobles, and anyone trying to become useful again. Its monks train hand-to-hand combat, discipline, mediation, public service, and restraint under pressure. In Spens, blood has no voice, but conduct does.

---

## Palm-Master: Palm-Master Galer (Garrik Vale)

A scarred half-elf with a shaved head, plain robes, and old soldier's hands. He is patient, blunt, and believes redemption is not a feeling. It is a habit proven daily.
