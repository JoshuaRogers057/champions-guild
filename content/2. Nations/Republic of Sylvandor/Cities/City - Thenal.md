# Thenal

**Population:** 200,000 (70% Elf)

Thenal is the intellectual heart of the Elven Republic — a city of observatories, arcane theory, and disciplined magical innovation built along the steep white cliffs of Sylvandar's southern bay. Unlike the flowing spectacle of Rael or the living grandeur of Sylvandar itself, Thenal projects a quieter kind of power: measured, refined, and intimidating in its certainty.

Silver-tiled towers rise like polished needles above layered terraces and marble promenades overlooking the sea, their upper spires crowned with rotating observatories, planar lenses, and arcane weather vanes that track celestial movement day and night. Cascading aqueducts and controlled waterfalls descend between districts in elegant ribbons of mist, feeding reflective pools and hydro-arcane cooling systems woven directly into the city's architecture. Even beauty here feels deliberate — engineered with scholarly precision rather than artistic indulgence.

Though Thenal possesses one of the Republic's most strategically important harbors, it lacks the crowded energy and mercantile chaos of Rael. Its docks are orderly, quiet, and highly regulated, existing primarily to support Moonspire University and the city's vast appetite for rare knowledge, magical materials, and controlled experimentation. Specialized imports arrive daily beneath strict oversight: preserved planar specimens, unstable fragment samples, forbidden manuscripts sealed in warded containers, and delicate arcane instruments carried beneath silence enchantments. Foreign scholars often describe the harbor as feeling less like a port and more like an extension of a research institution.

Thenal is cosmopolitan by elven standards, though never casual. Scholars, illusionists, artificers, diviners, navigators, and planar researchers from across Eryndor travel here seeking access to Moonspire's unparalleled academic resources. Non-elves are tolerated more readily than in many elven cities, but the culture carries an unmistakable intellectual elitism. In Thenal, ignorance is the ultimate embarrassment. Public conversations drift naturally into debates on magical ethics, celestial mechanics, or the geopolitical consequences of fragment scarcity.

Its residents value logic, restraint, and controlled experimentation above passion or spectacle. Arcane research here is heavily monitored, peer-reviewed, and politically entangled. Discoveries born in Thenal routinely shape national policy within the Vita Forum, giving the city enormous influence despite its subdued demeanor. Its senators are regarded less as politicians and more as philosopher-statesmen whose arguments can redirect the course of the Republic with a single carefully delivered speech.

## Districts

### Moonspire Enclave
The academic heart of Thenal and the sprawling grounds of Moonspire University. Towering lecture halls, floating observatories, ritual amphitheaters, and arcane laboratories dominate the skyline beneath the silent patrol of enchanted sentinels. Entire towers are devoted to singular disciplines: divination, planar theory, illusioncraft, teleportation, fragment stabilization, and magical ethics. At the district's center stands the Moonspire Library, protected by layered stasis wards and reflective defensive enchantments. Students from across Eryndor arrive seeking enlightenment, prestige, or dangerous truths better left buried.

### Crystalwalk Promenade
A breathtaking cliffside district overlooking the southern bay, lined with luminous marble terraces, sculpted gardens, and quiet observatory cafés where scholars debate beneath illusion-crafted constellations. Serves as Thenal's intellectual social center, frequented by professors and wealthy patrons of magical research. Policy is often made here before it comes to any formal chamber.

### The Inkvaults
A labyrinthine district of scriptoriums, magical printing houses, and arcane archives. Houses refinement chambers where enchanted inks and spell-scrolls are crafted with obsessive precision. The written memory of the Republic is preserved here by archivists and magical historians who catalog everything from Senate proceedings to extraplanar anomalies. Entire vaults are sealed behind memory-lock enchantments accessible only through university authority.

### The Silent Quays
Thenal's harbor district, stretching along the lower sea terraces beneath towering cliffside elevators and rune-guided crane systems. Unlike the vibrant ports of Rael, the Silent Quays feel restrained and almost unnervingly efficient. Cargo is inspected by arcane customs officials before being transferred to its destination. Ships arriving here often carry highly specialized imports unavailable elsewhere in the Republic.

### The Astral Heights
A secluded upper district reserved for Thenal's most elite scholars, observatory masters, and senior arcanists. Massive telescope arrays and planar mirrors rotate silently above the skyline, mapping celestial movement and monitoring magical disturbances across Eryndor. The district enforces strict light protocols and limitations.

## Notable Figures

**Senator Therindor Eldarlas** *(Vita Populi — Reformist, Academic Voice)*
A scholarly philosopher and lifelong academic known for long, winding speeches layered with obscure planar quotations and forgotten elven histories. Though frustratingly verbose to some, Therindor is respected as one of the Republic's foremost intellectual voices on arcane openness and magical cooperation.

**Senator Calion Veythar** *(Vita Populi — Centrist Swing)*
A seasoned Moonspire scholar whose calm pragmatism has made him one of the Senate's most influential swing votes. Quiet, analytical, and frustratingly difficult to predict, Calion weighs ideology against practicality with near-surgical precision.

**Headmistress Selai Mistelis**
Archmage of Moonspire University and sister to Senator Nerysa Mistelis. Governs the university with quiet intensity, demanding absolute excellence from faculty and students alike. Many suspect she knows far more about the Republic's hidden magical research than she publicly admits.

**Provost Aethon Valeir**
Master of the Astral Heights observatories and chief overseer of Thenal's planar monitoring operations. Brilliant but increasingly paranoid, Aethon has recently begun warning select officials about "movements beyond the veil" visible only through the city's highest observatories.

**Archivist Seralyth Nym**
Curator of the deepest restricted sections of the Inkvaults. Possesses an unnerving ability to recall nearly any written passage she has ever read, and is rumored to maintain hidden copies of documents erased from official Senate archives.
