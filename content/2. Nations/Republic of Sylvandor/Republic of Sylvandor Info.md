# Sylvandar — Government, Military, Economy & Culture

## Government & Politics

The Republic of Sylvandar is governed through a dual-tiered Senate beneath a ceremonial monarchy, a political structure designed to balance noble tradition with civic representation. Authority is divided between the Crown, the hereditary Eterna Voca, and the elected Vita Populi, all held together by the guiding hand of the First Arbiter. Together, these bodies form the **Vita Forum**.

Eight senators of the Eterna Voca are drawn from the ancient noble houses of Sylvandar, while eight senators of the Vita Populi are elected by the citizens of the Republic's four major cities, with each city sending two representatives to the Forum. Though publicly celebrated as a balanced republic, Sylvandar's political system preserves a deeply aristocratic foundation beneath its representative veneer. Ancient noble bloodlines remain central to elven governance.

House Vaelith serves as the royal line of the Republic, while Houses Elaris, Thirion, Ostrelis, Corvelis, Aureth, Mistelis, Zelis, and Drastlith maintain longstanding authority through the Eterna Voca. Political alliances between these houses have shaped Sylvandar for centuries, and many senators wield influence that extends far beyond the Senate chamber itself.

Though divided by fierce debates over foreign policy, trade, military doctrine, and the future direction of the Republic, nearly every major political faction within Sylvandar shares one underlying belief: that elven civilization is uniquely suited to guide the future of Eryndor. Reformers and traditionalists disagree not on elven superiority, but on how openly that superiority should be exercised.

---

### Crown Authority

**King Aelar Vaelith — Ceremonial Monarch**
Aelar serves as the symbolic heart of Sylvandar and the living embodiment of elven continuity. Serene and soft-spoken, the King rarely intervenes directly in political affairs, appearing instead during ceremonies, festivals, and moments of national significance. His role is almost entirely ceremonial; his only substantive political authority is the appointment of the First Arbiter, which must then be confirmed by the Senate. Despite his limited power, his presence alone carries immense legitimacy among the elven people, and even rival senators are careful not to openly diminish the dignity of the Crown.

**First Arbiter Selura Duskweave — The Voice Between Factions**
Selura Duskweave serves as the presiding authority over the Senate of Sixteen and is widely regarded as the true political head of the Republic. Appointed by the King and confirmed by the Senate, the First Arbiter holds a one-hundred-year term and acts as the balancing force between the Eterna Voca and the Vita Populi. Selura breaks deadlocked votes, moderates Senate proceedings, and quietly shapes the political center of Sylvandar through influence rather than decree.

A sharp-witted enchantress with near-perfect recall, Selura is known throughout the Republic for the floating crystal quill that records her every spoken word. Reserved, calculating, and politically formidable, she has maintained stability within the Republic for nearly a century, preventing factional rivalry from descending into open crisis.

---

### Current Crisis

For the first time in generations, that stability is beginning to fracture.

Selura Duskweave's century-long term is in its last year, and no obvious successor commands enough unity to inherit her office. Reformists, traditionalists, populists, military hardliners, noble houses, and civic factions now maneuver quietly for influence over the succession, each understanding that the next Arbiter may determine the Republic's course for the next hundred years.

The Senate remains balanced on a knife's edge. Deadlocks have become increasingly common, political alliances increasingly fragile, and whispers of unseen influence have begun spreading through the Vita Forum itself. What was once elegant political rivalry now threatens to become something far more dangerous.

## The Eterna Voca — The Eternal Voice

*Eight permanent noble seats. The aristocratic backbone of the Republic.*

The Eterna Voca occupies the eight hereditary noble seats within the Senate of Sixteen and represents the oldest concentration of political authority in Sylvandar. Drawn from the great Houses of the Republic, its senators oversee matters of diplomacy, ritual, military policy, arcane law, and cultural continuity. Though the Vita Populi provides elected representation, the Eterna Voca remains the enduring spine of elven governance, carrying centuries of accumulated influence into every debate.

Membership within the Voca is not merely political — it is dynastic. Families spend generations cultivating alliances, preserving reputation, and maneuvering for influence within the chamber. Rivalries between Houses may simmer for centuries beneath layers of etiquette, courtesy, and carefully measured language.

To outsiders, the Eterna Voca often appears calm and impossibly refined. In truth, it is a battlefield fought with memory, implication, favors, and patience.

### Senators of the Eterna Voca

**Senator Aurelion Thirion — Diplomatic Lawmaster** | *Moderate Reformist*
A master of diplomatic law and arcane ethics, Aurelion is regarded as one of the Republic's foremost authorities on Senate procedure, treaty language, and international conduct. Calm, articulate, and relentlessly composed, he balances tradition with modern statecraft and is favored by foreign emissaries, magistrates, and academic circles alike. His rulings on legal interpretation are studied in courts as distant as Lexovar.

**Senator Lysira Ostrelis — Purist Firebrand** | *Hardline Traditionalist / Isolationist*
One of the fiercest defenders of elven sovereignty and ancestral purity within the Senate. Revered by hardliners and feared across the Vita Forum, she speaks with cutting precision and openly condemns what she sees as the Republic's growing dependence upon foreign powers and "lesser-lived ambitions." Her speeches are famous throughout Sylvandar for reducing opponents to silence without ever raising her voice.

**Senator Therindor Elaris — Philosopher's Voice** | *Measured Reformist*
Spell theorist, philosopher, and patron of the artistic elite. Known for long and often theatrical speeches exploring the moral responsibilities of elven power. He represents the belief that Sylvandar's greatness is strengthened through wisdom, diplomacy, and cultural leadership rather than isolation.

**Senator Vaenor Aureth — General of the Senate** | *Hardline Traditionalist*
A war-hardened military strategist turned statesman. Advocates stronger borders, expanded military readiness, and greater investment in the Republic's standing forces. One of the Senate's strongest defenders of the Woaden Rangers and views protection of the sacred trees as both strategic necessity and holy duty.

**Senator Malrian Corvelis — Spymaster in Robes** | *Political Opportunist / Swing Vote*
Secretive, patient, and notoriously difficult to read. Oversees much of the Republic's intelligence apparatus and often serves as the deciding force in closely divided debates. Came into his position after his father's death and the expulsion of Elven influence from [[Kastalshire]] in 752. Infamous for long stretches of silence followed by sudden interventions capable of shifting the direction of entire Senate sessions.

**Senator Nerysa Mistelis — High Priestess Politician** | *Moderate Traditionalist*
A devout servant of Corellon and the political voice of the Republic's temple orders. Ensures that spiritual identity remains deeply woven into Sylvandar's civic life. She sponsors festivals, sacred rites, temple restorations, and religious education throughout the Republic. Exerts influence through moral authority, institutional loyalty, and centuries of carefully cultivated relationships.

**Senator Thalorien Drastlith — Isolationist Hardliner** | *Traditionalist / Isolationist*
A magical purist and fierce cultural traditionalist. Argues that the Republic's increasing engagement with foreign nations has diluted elven excellence and weakened cultural identity. Advocates for reduced foreign dependence, tighter borders, and a return to inward perfection through art, magic, and disciplined scholarship.

**Senator Cato Zelis — Voice of Measured Reform** | *Moderate Reformist*
A dignified Drow noble and senior statesman of the Eterna Voca. Advocates for greater civic transparency, cautious diplomatic openness, and controlled modernization while maintaining deep reverence for tradition and institutional stability. His greatest political frustration is his daughter, Senator Virellen Zelis of Rael, whose populist theatrics he views as dangerously reckless.

## The Vita Populi — The Voice of the People

*Eight elected seats. Two senators from each of the Republic's major cities.*

The Vita Populi serves as the elected chamber of the Senate of Sixteen, representing the civic and political will of Sylvandar's great cities. Unlike the hereditary Eterna Voca, senators of the Vita Populi rise through public support, reputation, military distinction, academic influence, or popular movements within their home cities.

Regional identity shapes its politics as much as ideology. Rael's cosmopolitan reformers, Val'Quith's martial isolationists, Thenal's intellectual pragmatists, and Sylvandar's populist traditionalists frequently clash within the Vita Forum.

### Senators of Rael

**Senator Caelar Nymaris** | *Reformist — Trade & Diplomacy*
A distinguished Sea Elf diplomat known for refined poise and razor-sharp wit. Draped in flowing sea-green robes and carrying an ornate coral cane. Argues that Sylvandar's future lies in controlled engagement with the wider world through commerce, diplomacy, and strategic partnerships. Openly supports expanded trade with humans, dwarves, and dragonmarked houses. Formerly known as Caelar Ostrelis; he publicly renounced his noble surname when he sought election.

**Senator Virellen Zelis** | *Reformist — Culture & Populism*
Daughter of Senator Cato Zelis of the Eterna Voca. A flamboyant Drow enchantress and celebrated patron of the arts who treats public speaking less like governance and more like performance, dazzling audiences through illusion magic, theatrical rhetoric, and unapologetic charisma. Champions civic transparency, artistic freedom, inter-racial diplomacy, and broader cultural openness.

*Political Tone of Rael:* Rael sends the most openly reformist delegation within the Vita Populi. Caelar represents calculated openness through commerce and diplomacy, while Virellen represents cultural transformation and civic populism. Together they stand in direct opposition to the isolationist blocs of Val'Quith and the hardline traditionalists of the Eterna Voca.

### Senators of Val'Quith

**Senator Victus Elaras** | *Hardline Traditionalist — Martial Authority*
A decorated general and veteran strategist. Advocates stronger military doctrine, tighter borders, and expanded funding for Valiant Hall and the Republic's standing forces. Shadowed by rumors surrounding the assassination of his uncle, Senator Vaenor Elaras, whose increasingly progressive positions had alienated hardline factions. No evidence has ever surfaced, yet the suspicion lingers stubbornly within Senate circles.

**Senator Elyndra Veyth** | *Hardline Traditionalist — Voice of the Ranks*
A battle-scarred Shadar-Kai veteran elevated through popular support rather than noble influence. Presents herself as the voice of Val'Quith's soldiers, scouts, and frontier defenders. Fiercely patriotic and openly distrustful of foreign influence. Where Victus is cold and measured, Elyndra is fiery and direct, often speaking with the cadence of a battlefield command rather than Senate rhetoric.

*Political Tone of Val'Quith:* Val'Quith forms the martial and isolationist stronghold of the Vita Populi. Victus represents disciplined military authority and aristocratic hardline politics, while Elyndra channels populist nationalism and the voice of the ranks.

### Senators of Thenal

**Senator Therindor Eldarlas** | *Reformist — Academic Voice*
A philosopher, planar scholar, and lifelong academic. Advocates broader magical cooperation, academic openness, and increased exchange of knowledge across Eryndor, believing intellectual stagnation to be the greatest threat facing elven civilization. His tendency to lecture rather than persuade often frustrates both allies and opponents.

**Senator Calion Veythar** | *Centrist — Academic Pragmatist*
A senior scholar of Moonspire University. Weighs arguments on practical merit rather than ideological loyalty, frequently siding with different factions depending on the issue before him. This refusal to fully align with either reformists or hardliners has made him both respected and deeply unpopular within Senate politics.

*Political Tone of Thenal:* Thenal remains the intellectual swing city of the Republic, often becoming the deciding voice in contentious Senate matters.

### Senators of Sylvandar (Capital)

**Senator Thalen Duskbranch** | *Hardline Traditionalist — Barkline Populist*
Born among the artisans, laborers, and tradesfolk of Sylvandar's Barkline district. Rose to prominence through fiery populist rhetoric and fierce defense of traditional elven identity. Speaks in blunt, accessible language that resonates deeply with ordinary citizens. Warns constantly that foreign trade, outside influence, and diluted cultural identity threaten the soul of the Republic.

**[Open Seat]** | *Primaries Underway*
The second elected seat of the capital remains unfilled. With First Arbiter Selura Duskweave's term nearing its end and the Senate balanced on a razor's edge, the outcome may determine whether the Republic drifts toward reform, hardline traditionalism, or fragile political compromise.

## Military

Sylvandar maintains a small standing military and relies heavily on citizen readiness. All elven citizens receive training in archery and basic war magic, allowing the Republic to raise capable forces quickly in times of war. The **Sentinel Companies** serve as organized regional defense forces, similar to a national reserve, and provide the backbone of larger wartime mobilization.

Sylvandar boasts the finest archers in Eryndor. Its military favors patience, concealment, and precision over overwhelming force. During the Long War against the Drakmir Dominion, these methods allowed the elves to frustrate far larger armies for decades. Dominion commanders often described the conflict not as fighting an army, but as fighting a forest.

The city watches of Sylvandar are held to high standards, especially in major cities such as Rael and Thenal. Many officers have prior military training, and public order is expected to be maintained with discipline and restraint.

### Elite Military Institutions

**The Republican Guard**
The official protective force of the Vita Forum. Clad in silvered armor marked by the insignia of the Republic, they defend senators against assassination, coercion, and political violence. Membership is one of the highest honors in civic service, reserved for exceptional soldiers, duelists, investigators, and war mages. Though few in number, their presence is treated as an extension of Senate authority.

**The Woaden Rangers**
Stand above even the Republican Guard in prestige and capability. Directly commanded by the King, they are charged with protecting the sacred Woaden Trees, but their duties extend well beyond the groves. When Sylvandar requires its most capable agents — whether at home or beyond its borders — the Rangers are sent. They are revered as guardians of the Republic's living soul and feared as its deadliest unseen hand.

In the years following the Peace of 1000, Sylvandar gradually expanded its naval presence and coastal defenses, particularly through Rael, recognizing that maritime strength offered strategic flexibility the forests alone could no longer guarantee.

## Economy

Sylvandar's economy depends heavily on the skill of its artisans. Elven-made goods are expensive, slow to produce, and valued across Eryndor for their quality. The Republic also profits from its deep knowledge of forest cultivation, especially where other nations pay for elven magic to improve harvests or restore damaged land.

Rare herbs and alchemical plants are carefully harvested for export, but the Republic limits how much can leave its forests. Woaden wood is almost never sold — even the possibility of exporting it can cause fierce debate within the Vita Forum.

For all its wealth, the Republic still imports much of its raw materials. With no native fragment mines, it is dependent on other nations for access to fragments, creating internal tension, especially among Traditionalists. Thenal also imports rare magical components, many of which are refined, studied, or woven into the Republic's highest forms of arcane work.

**The Export of Wisdom**
Sylvandar also exports influence through its ambassadors. These envoys do more than represent the Republic in foreign courts — they remain in place for decades, sometimes long enough to advise several generations of rulers. Their work is patient, subtle, and difficult to measure. The Republic calls this wisdom. Its rivals often call it manipulation.

## Culture & Society

The elves of Sylvandar view themselves not simply as a nation, but as the living continuation of an ancient and refined civilization. This belief shapes nearly every aspect of Sylvandari life. Even elves critical of the Republic rarely question the idea that elven civilization itself stands apart from that of shorter-lived peoples.

Elves of Sylvandar tend to view subrace distinctions as secondary to elven identity itself. Whether High Elf, Wood Elf, Sea Elf, Drow, Eladrin, or Shadar-kai, an elf is first judged by loyalty to the Republic and by their contribution to the continuity of elven civilization.

Conflict resolution within Sylvandar is shaped profoundly by the elven perception of time. Problems are often approached slowly, deliberately, and across decades rather than days. Diplomacy may unfold over years. Political rivalries may span generations. Within the Senate especially, delay itself is often wielded as a political weapon.

Art and beauty occupy a central place within Sylvandari society. To the elves, beauty is not decoration, but evidence of mastery, discipline, and understanding. Music, illusion, sculpture, poetry, tailoring, calligraphy, and magical craftsmanship are treated not as hobbies, but as civic virtues.

Sylvandari society places enormous weight upon reputation, composure, and legacy. Family names carry influence across centuries, public embarrassment can linger for decades, and failure is rarely forgotten entirely.

The Long War against the Drakmir Dominion continues to cast a shadow over the Republic's identity. The destruction of sacred Woaden Trees remains one of the deepest cultural wounds in Sylvandari memory. Diplomatic relations are still withheld from the Dominion. Combined with growing foreign influence, internal political division, declining religious devotion among younger generations, and uncertainty surrounding the succession of First Arbiter Selura Duskweave, there is an undercurrent of anxiety beneath the Republic's polished confidence.

### Racial Grudges & Political Tensions

**Dwarves (Kar Dromm):** Dispute centers on fragment access. The Republic has no native fragment mines, while dwarven control over major fragment sources gives Kar Dromm leverage that many elven nobles deeply resent.

**Halflings (Thalenmark Empire):** Relations strained by trade conflicts and economic dominance. Many elves view halfling ledgers as an undignified form of power.

**Humans:** Relations remain uneasy due to political shifts, rejection of elven advisors, and the speed with which human kingdoms change direction. Elves often mistake human urgency for instability; humans often mistake elven patience for arrogance.

**Drakmir Dominion:** Sylvandar's most emotionally charged rival. The Long War, the burning of Emberwood, the death of Prince-General Caelthir Vaelith, and the destruction of sacred Woaden Trees remain open wounds. Trade may exist in limited form where necessity demands it, but genuine trust is absent.

**Bastions of Solara:** Resented for forcing the Peace of 1000. To Solara, the treaty prevented further bloodshed. To many elves, it preserved the Dominion long enough for shorter-lived enemies to breed, rebuild, and return stronger within a century.

## Religion

Religion in Sylvandar is deeply intertwined with culture, history, artistry, and civic life. To many elves of the Republic, faith is less about rigid doctrine and more about living in harmony with beauty, discipline, knowledge, and the continuity of civilization itself. Temples serve not only as places of worship, but as centers of philosophy, music, magical study, historical preservation, and public ceremony.

**Corellon** stands as the principal deity of the Republic and the spiritual symbol of elven identity. Revered as patron of artistry, magic, beauty, and creation. Open-air temples, moonlit groves, reflective pools, and illusion-lit sanctuaries dedicated to Corellon appear throughout the Republic, particularly within Whispergrove and the older districts of the Bark Sphere.

The sacred Woaden Trees are regarded with near-religious reverence throughout Sylvandar. Though not worshipped as gods themselves, the trees are widely viewed as living symbols of divine favor, continuity, and the enduring soul of the Republic. The temples of Sylvandar still wield significant social and political influence, particularly through long-standing ties to the Eterna Voca.

## Mercenaries & Criminal Organizations

Though Sylvandar presents itself as a refined and orderly republic, wealth, politics, trade, and ancient rivalries have inevitably given rise to mercenary companies and criminal enterprises operating beneath the Republic's polished exterior. Unlike open gang conflicts found elsewhere in Eryndor, criminality within Sylvandar is typically subtle, calculated, and deeply entangled with reputation, influence, and political ambition.

### The Ember Wolves

*Home Nation: Sylvandar Republic | Honor Scale: 7/10*
*Symbol: A burning wolf head beneath a crescent moon*

The Republic's greatest mercenary company, headquartered in Val'Quith. Founded after Vaelith's Fall, when Prince-General Caelthir Vaelith died inside a burning Woaden Tree. The soldiers who survived believed they had failed their prince, their nation, and the sacred heart of Sylvandar. Rather than return home, they formed a company that could keep fighting beyond the limits of Senate command.

The Ember Wolves became a tool for work the Republic could not always do openly. Contracts often include diplomatic escort, anti-monster operations, artifact recovery, intelligence gathering, frontier suppression, and discreet intervention in politically sensitive conflicts. Their composition is heavily elven, though outsiders may join under strict terms.

**Leader:** Maelor Thorneveil — a lean, ash-haired elf with a soldier's rigid posture and one side of his face burned from brow to jaw. He once served under Prince-General Caelthir Vaelith and survived the fire that killed him. To Maelor, the Ember Wolves are not just a mercenary company.

### The Nightbloom Syndicate

The most prominent criminal network of the Branch Sphere, with its strongest presence in Rael. Operates through merchant circles, harbor channels, private salons, and back-alley vice. Deals in illicit goods, blackmail, forgery, protection rackets, and quiet violence when needed. Its upper ranks favor discretion and elegance; its lower ranks are far more visible. A noble may know the Nightbloom as a source of secrets; a dockworker may know them as the people who come knocking when coin is owed.

## Elves — Grace of the Old Blood

Elves with noble lineage within the last three generations may manifest the **Old Blood**, marked by a subtle and often elegant tattoo or symbol around the eyes. These markings are often interpreted as a visible reminder of noble inheritance, ancient grace, and the long memory of elven bloodlines.

> **Drawback.** If you use this ability, your movement speed is 0 until the end of your first turn.
