<div class="eryndor-map"></div>

Drag to pan and scroll (or pinch) to zoom. Capitals are always shown; cities appear as you zoom in, then towns, then villages. Click any marker to open its lore page.

> [!note] Work in progress
> This is the first version of the map. Roads, further settlements and other details are still being added, and marker positions will be refined over time.
