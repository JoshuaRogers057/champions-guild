# Ash-Korrath — Diplomatic Relations

---

## National Identity

Ash-Korrath is a nation that survived by becoming harder than its enemies. Tiefling identity is secrecy, pact-law, infernal lineage, loyalty, and ruthless preservation. Outsiders see paranoia and cruelty; Ash-Korrath sees discipline and survival. Its diplomacy is cautious, transactional, and guarded, revealing only what must be revealed and trusting almost no one.

---

## General Reputation

Most nations view Ash-Korrath as secretive, dangerous, and difficult to read. The tieflings are not broadly loved, but they are no longer easy to dismiss. Their hidden strength, infernal contracts, and refusal to vanish have earned wary respect. Still, few rulers sleep easier after signing a pact with them.

---

## Primary Diplomatic Tensions

Ash-Korrath's old wound is Solara. The ancient Aasimar-Tiefling conflict still defines how both nations are remembered. Beyond that hatred, Ash-Korrath keeps diplomacy narrow: trade if useful, silence if safer, retaliation if betrayed.
