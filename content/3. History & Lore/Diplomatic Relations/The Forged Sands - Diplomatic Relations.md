# The Forged Sands — Diplomatic Relations

---

## National Identity

The Forged Sands defines life by purpose, memory, function, and sacred creation. The Steelborn are not tools, though many nations still treat them that way. Their Creation Forges and core fragments are holy necessities. Their diplomacy is defensive, controlled, and suspicious, especially toward nations that see constructs as property instead of people.

---

## General Reputation

The Steelborn are respected for discipline and feared for their silence. Many outsiders admire their endurance but do not understand their interior life. Some still call them Warforged as if they were built objects rather than a people. The Forged Sands remembers such language. It remembers most things.

---

## Primary Diplomatic Tensions

The Forged Sands needs fragments for survival, not luxury. That single fact drives nearly every foreign relationship. Nations that trade fragments can become partners. Nations that steal cores, treat Steelborn as machines, or meddle with Creation Forges become enemies without appeal.
