# Republic of Sylvandar — Diplomatic Relations

---

## National Identity

Sylvandar sees itself as the keeper of elven beauty, memory, patience, and civilization. Its people are refined, disciplined, magical, and deeply proud. They believe shorter-lived nations often act with dangerous urgency. Their greatest wound is the Long War, and their hatred of the Drakmir Dominion remains one of the clearest truths in Eryndor.

---

## General Reputation

Other nations admire Sylvandar's art, archery, magic, and grace, but often resent its arrogance. The elves are seen as wise by some, manipulative by many, and insufferably patient by nearly everyone. Their beauty is real. So is their condescension. Their wounds are old, but they keep them fresh.

---

## Primary Diplomatic Tensions

Sylvandar's diplomacy is shaped by loss: Woaden trees burned, royal blood spilled, borders scarred, and pride wounded by Solara's enforced peace. Its fragment insecurity worsens matters, forcing the Republic to bargain with nations it privately despises or considers beneath it.
