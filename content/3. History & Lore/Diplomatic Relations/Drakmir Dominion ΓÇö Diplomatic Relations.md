# Drakmir Dominion — Diplomatic Relations

---

## National Identity

The Drakmir Dominion is a nation of service, discipline, merit, and results. Honor is measured by what is accomplished, not what was intended. Dragonborn command, kobolds sustain, and every citizen serves. The Dominion respects strength, order, and usefulness. Its diplomacy is strategic, direct, and threatening because Drakmir believes it deserves to shape Eryndor's future.

---

## General Reputation

The Dominion is respected, feared, and watched. Its armies are disciplined, its mines productive, its engineers capable, and its ambitions impossible to ignore. To its neighbors, Drakmir is not chaotic evil; it is organized pressure. That makes it worse. A reckless enemy can be survived. A patient one must be planned against.

---

## Primary Diplomatic Tensions

Drakmir's central diplomatic tension is that it wants to be recognized as honorable while many remember it as conqueror. The Dominion respects Solara's strength, hates Sylvandar's contempt, studies Lexovar's inventions, and watches every border as a future campaign map.
