# Goliaths - Echo of the Titanborn

The power of the Old Blood is a mysterious force limited to the highest echelon of powerful families and bloodlines. Each manifestation is paired with a significant drawback that cannot be avoided or reduced.

> Any player wishing to use an Old Blood trait must first receive approval through the **#approvals** channel on the Discord.

---

## Eligibility

Goliaths in leadership positions and their direct descendants.

**Physical Marking:** Glowing runes and tattoos etched into the skin.

---

## Level 7 Power

> Once per long rest, as an action, you may enter a Titan's Trance for 1 minute. While in this trance, you gain resistance to one elemental damage type of your choice: fire, lightning, thunder, cold, or poison.

---

## Drawback

> When the trance ends, you immediately gain one level of exhaustion.
