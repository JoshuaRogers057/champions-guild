# Orcs & Goblinoids - Fury of the Old Blood

The power of the Old Blood is a mysterious force limited to the highest echelon of powerful families and bloodlines. Each manifestation is paired with a significant drawback that cannot be avoided or reduced.

> Any player wishing to use an Old Blood trait must first receive approval through the **#approvals** channel on the Discord.

---

## Eligibility

Ruling lineages; immediately elevated to raid leader status upon manifestation.

**Physical Marking:** A mark or tattoo around the eyes.

---

## Level 7 Power

> Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you may take the Attack action before falling unconscious. This feature does not grant movement and does not stack with Relentless Endurance.

---

## Drawback

> After using this feature, you immediately fall unconscious and suffer one failed death saving throw.
