# Elves - Grace of the Old Blood

The power of the Old Blood is a mysterious force limited to the highest echelon of powerful families and bloodlines. Each manifestation is paired with a significant drawback that cannot be avoided or reduced.

> Any player wishing to use an Old Blood trait must first receive approval through the **#approvals** channel on the Discord.

---

## Eligibility

Noble lineage within the last three generations.

**Physical Marking:** A subtle and often elegant tattoo or symbol around the eyes.

---

## Level 7 Power

> Once per long rest, when you roll initiative, you may begin combat as if you had taken the Dodge action.

---

## Drawback

> If you use this ability, your movement speed is 0 until the end of your first turn.
