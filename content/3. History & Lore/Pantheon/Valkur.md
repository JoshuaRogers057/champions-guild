Valkur is the god of sailors, ships, and the courage of those who challenge the sea. He is a boisterous, bold deity who loves the ocean not for its dangers but for the challenge and freedom it represents. He is the divine champion of those who sail not out of fear but out of genuine love of the sea and the horizon beyond it. He is depicted as a huge, laughing warrior mariner with salt-crusted armor and a cutlass in hand, standing at the prow of a ship in a storm.

Valkur represents the courage to sail despite the danger, the skill to navigate difficult waters, and the camaraderie of a ship's crew. He is deeply popular among naval officers, explorers, and anyone who has felt the particular joy of a good wind filling the sails.

## Followers

Valkur's followers are sailors, naval officers, explorers, and those who love the sea in its wild and challenging aspects. His followers are expected to be brave, skilled at seamanship, protective of their crews, and willing to challenge the ocean rather than cower before it. His clergy serve as ship chaplains and harbor priests, blessing vessels before voyages and honoring those lost at sea. They tend to be bold, physically capable, deeply loyal to their crews, and perpetually uncomfortable too far from salt water.

## Divine Relationships

Valkur is the direct divine enemy of **[[Umberlee]]**, the sea goddess, whose storms and cruelty he works to protect sailors against. Where [[Umberlee]] views sailors as prey, Valkur views them as his people to protect. He is allied with **[[Selûne]]**, whose stars guide his sailors through the night, and has a warm relationship with **[[Tempus]]**, as naval combat falls within both their purviews.
