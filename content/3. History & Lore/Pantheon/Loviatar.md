Loviatar is the goddess of pain — not the reluctant suffering of [[Ilmater]]'s martyrdom, but the deliberate, controlled, and savored infliction of agony. She is cold, precise, and eerily beautiful, depicted as a pale woman in white robes who views the causing of pain as an art form. She is disciplined where [[Talona]] is chaotic, methodical where other evil deities are impulsive, and takes a craftsperson's pride in the perfect calibration of suffering.

Loviatar teaches that pain is the truest form of honesty — that in pain, all masks fall and the real person is revealed. She views the capacity to both cause and endure pain as the measure of a person's true nature.

## Followers

Loviatar attracts torturers, inquisitors, those who enjoy cruelty, and paradoxically some ascetics who believe in the spiritual revelation of endured pain. Her clergy are disciplined, methodical, and deeply unsettling — they approach their dark work with the detached professionalism of craftspeople. Her faith has a complicated relationship with **[[Ilmater]]**'s, as both deal in suffering, but where [[Ilmater]]'s followers absorb pain to relieve others, Loviatar's followers inflict it deliberately.

## Divine Relationships

Loviatar has a strange, tense relationship with **[[Ilmater]]** — the two deities of suffering are mirror opposites, and their followers clash constantly. She also has a complicated dynamic with **[[Talona]]**, the goddess of disease, as both operate in the space of physical suffering but through entirely different methods. She serves as a lesser power within the broader sphere of evil deities and has a respectful if cold relationship with **[[Bane]]**, whose disciplined cruelty she understands.
