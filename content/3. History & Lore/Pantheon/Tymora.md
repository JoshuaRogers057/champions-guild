Tymora is the goddess of good luck and the patron deity of adventurers — perhaps the most beloved deity among those who live dangerous lives. She is the embodiment of the belief that fortune favors the bold, and she actively encourages mortals to take risks, embrace adventure, and trust that luck will see them through. She is joyful, warm, and deeply fond of the mortals she watches over.

She was once part of a single deity of luck alongside her twin sister [[Beshaba]], but they were split apart in divine conflict. Where Tymora represents good fortune, [[Beshaba]] embodies misfortune — and the two are in eternal opposition.

## Followers

Tymora is worshipped by adventurers, gamblers, thieves, and anyone who relies on luck in their daily life. Her followers are expected to be bold, take calculated risks, and trust that fortune will reward courage. Her temples are welcoming places that attract travelers and adventurers. Her clergy tend to be cheerful, risk-tolerant, and deeply optimistic — they see every near-disaster as proof that luck is watching over them.

## Divine Relationships

Tymora's eternal enemy is her twin sister **[[Beshaba]]**, whose misfortune directly opposes her good luck. She is allied with **[[Lathander]]** and **[[Selûne]]**, fellow deities of hope and positive energy. She has a warm relationship with **[[Mask]]**, whose followers also rely on luck, though she finds his deceptive methods less honest than her straightforward gambling on fortune.
