# Current Stories

*A reminder of the crises, consequences, powers, and mysteries still shaping Eryndor.*

## Aftermath & Active Crises

*Recent victories changed the world. They did not settle it.*

### The Domes
The original dome over [[Zindariel]] remains, and four new, much smaller domes have appeared, each roughly one mile across: the vault at Othan Manor, the ruins of Castle Bran, the Labyrinth, and the coastal fortress at the Wall. Whatever happened during the Vivisectionist crisis has permanently changed the map of Eryndor.

### The Criminal Shadow War
Criminal organizations across Eryndor are becoming increasingly organized, wealthy, and politically entrenched. The [[Brotherhood of the Rat]], Dreadwake interests, the [[Velvet Ledger]], and other organizations are pieces of a much larger struggle for control of the underworld.

### The Ruin of Talvereth
[[Talvereth]] remains shattered and the [[Gilded Council]] is broken. The [[Bastions of Solara]] are pushing both reconstruction and a restructuring of the council that would divide political representation between halflings and the other peoples of the city.

### The Rebuilding of Spens
[[Spens]] is rebuilding after its temporary capture by a coalition of Dreadwake forces. The city survived, but its political, criminal, and economic landscape has been badly disrupted.

### The Eye of Chaos
The Eye of Chaos remains somewhere in the world and has not been brought to account. Its ultimate destination and intentions remain unknown.

### Northern Kastalshire
The growing druidic and treant crisis in northern [[Kastalshire]] continues to worsen. Groves have gone silent, old relationships with the Crown are breaking down, and something is changing in the northern forests.

### The Better Dawn / Dreaming Dark
The Champions Guild and the Chosen of Lathander achieved a major victory, destroying the immediate invasion route and capturing Aethrael, who remains imprisoned. But elements of the Better Dawn escaped, and the greater Dreaming Dark remains beyond Eryndor.

### Kalamethet
Kalamethet survived. The twisted dragon was last seen within the Labyrinth, and his ultimate fate remains unresolved.

## Nations in Motion

*Governments, cultures, resources, and new technology are shifting the balance of power.*

### Unrest in Kastalshire
The Crown's increasingly centralized rail system and political control are creating serious unrest among the duchies. Old tensions with [[Thundrakar]] and resistance to [[Contramara]]'s growing authority are feeding into the instability.

### Thundrakar's Eastern Foothold
Rurik Grimveil of Thundrakar's Clan Glimveil now holds a castle in far eastern Kastalshire, captured with the aid of the Champions Guild. The stronghold gives Thundrakar a fortified foothold for raids into Kastalshire. The Crown is unlikely to tolerate the incursion for long, and a major Kastalshire response is expected.

### The Airship Race
Airships are rapidly changing military and economic power across Eryndor. [[Drakmir Dominion|Drakmir]], Talvereth, and [[Nation of Lexovar|Lexovar]] already possess one or more, while Kastalshire, [[Republic of Sylvandar|Sylvandar]], and the [[The Forged Sands|Forged Sands]] are very close to fielding their own.

### Kar Dromm in Crisis
Assassinations, vacant clan seats, unresolved succession struggles, and disputes over dwarven rights to ancient masterworks have created growing instability throughout [[Halls of Kar Dromm|Kar Dromm]]. No single crisis has broken the nation, but several are beginning to overlap.

### The First Arbiter
Sylvandar approaches the selection of its next [[First Arbiter]]. Competing visions for how aggressively Sylvandar should exert its influence abroad are beginning to collide.

### The Woaden Wood
Sylvandar's ancient forests are becoming an increasingly valuable strategic resource as other nations race to construct airships. The elves have little interest in surrendering the forests simply because the rest of the world suddenly needs their timber.

### Uncertainty in the Forged Sands
The Steelborn face mounting tension with Lexovar, worsening shortages of the fragments necessary to create new Steelborn, and unsettling mysteries throughout their territory. The Black Silence, old sealed sites, and unresolved questions surrounding Steelborn creation have left the nation increasingly uneasy.

### The Aging Storm of Thundrakar
[[Drogath Stormvein]] will not rule forever, and uncertainty surrounds what comes after him. The secretive [[Polaris]] continues to operate while foreign powers quietly watch the coming succession.

### The Scar of Vaelor
Foreign nations increasingly want permanent access to the [[Scar of Vaelor|Scar]]'s resources and strategic position. The Genasi continue resisting attempts to turn trade agreements into foreign footholds.

### The Future of Sanama
A growing divide is forming between those who want permanent settlements, markets, schools, and infrastructure and those who believe settling permanently would destroy what makes [[Sanama]] what it is.

### The War of the Fanged Swamp
The struggle within the [[Fanged Swamp]] is intensifying as competing powers fight for territory and influence. At the same time, the swamp itself continues to change — waters shift, old routes vanish, and previously buried places reappear.

### Unrest in the Bastions of Solara
Resentment toward Solara's entrenched elite is growing among merchants, veterans, and poorer citizens, while groups such as the [[Ashwings]] and [[Sunbroken]] challenge the state's authority. Airships are also beginning to undermine Solara's historic control of mountain passes and overland trade.

## Unresolved Threats & Mysteries

*Some dangers survived. Others are only beginning to reveal themselves.*

### The Dragons of the Roost
Dragons have begun moving beyond the places where they are normally seen. Why is unclear.

### The Stolen Memories of Elora
A massive collection of genealogies, hidden roads, treaties, testimony, and cultural memory was deliberately stolen from the [[Herds of Elora]]. The archive has never been recovered, and some believe the thieves were trying to erase something, rather than steal it.

### Lexovar's Hidden War
Rival magical institutions continue their quiet academic cold war of influence, espionage, and sabotage. Beneath it, organizations such as the [[Obsidian Chain]] may have penetrated institutions that believe themselves secure.

### Knell Escaped
The sentient bell-construct known as Knell has escaped the Arcanum. Born when mixed magic at the Arcanum awakened a bell into consciousness, the powerful construct now roams Kastalshire and seeks vengeance on the students whose magic brought it to life.

### The Old Blood
Awakenings of the mysterious [[Old Blood]] are becoming more frequent throughout Eryndor. No one yet understands why.

### The Fragile Peace
The great wars are over, but Eryndor is not truly at peace. Old grudges, new weapons, unstable governments, criminal empires, territorial ambitions, and rapidly changing technology are placing increasing pressure on the agreements that have kept another continental war from beginning.
