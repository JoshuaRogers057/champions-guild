---
title: The Legend of Zelri - The Time-Locked Oracle
---

*A legend of Clockspire and the Arcane Council*

---

Last seen three centuries ago, **Zelri Rivenstil** was the Headmaster of Clockspire's magical academy and a master of time-based magics. One morning, she calmly stepped out of bed and said:

*"I need to alter this time stream."*

She walked into her own timestream to halt a feedback loop that she said would have shattered the Arcane Council itself. She reemerged a moment later — aged, silent, and glowing — having lived years in mere moments.

Since then, she has not spoken a word. She disappeared, and has not been seen since.

---

## Signs of Her Presence

Legitimate records of sightings share common details:

- Clocks running backward
- Stars shining underground
- Notes arriving in her handwriting — advising scholars on projects or predicting events with unnerving precision

---

## What Scholars Believe

> *She no longer moves linearly through time. Some say she exists simultaneously in the moment of her death — and its prevention.*
