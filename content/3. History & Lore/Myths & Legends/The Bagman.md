title: The Bagman
---

*A rumor whispered among adventurers*

---

The Bagman was once an adventurer who sought to escape doom by abandoning his party and hiding inside a bag of holding. When he tried to leave, he became lost amid a constantly increasing number of extradimensional storage spaces. Over time, the strange forces of this magical in-between place transformed him into something monstrous.

Now, every night, the Bagman slips out from a random bag of holding. If he cannot find his way home, he drags someone back into the bag with him — leaving behind a trinket from his hidden kingdom of lost junk as the only sign he was ever there.

---

## How to Summon Him

> *Speak too loudly over an open bag of holding.*
> *Or whisper "follow my voice" into a magical storage space three times.*
>
> *The Bagman will come for you.*
