## Where it stands

Sylvandar's ancient forests have become a strategic resource as other nations race to build airships. The elves have little interest in surrendering them simply because the rest of the world suddenly needs timber. The Woaden Rangers closed the region around Duskwillow entirely after a Woaden Tree fell there and the wood began to rot from the roots up, calling it a divine blight. A senator and others were carried off from that village, and no one knows where they went.

## Unanswered questions

- Will Sylvandar ever sell its timber, and to whom?
- What brought down the Woaden Tree at Duskwillow, and will the rot spread?
- Where were Senator Virellen Zelis, Shalk Syrinthil and Lady Faela taken?
- Which nations will press hardest for the forests?

**8 Nov 2025: Whispers of Duskwillow**

A team sent to watch Shalk Syrinthil and his wife, Lady Faela, at a moon-season convocation in the elven village of Duskwillow found a fallen Woaden Tree on the forest path, its roots giving off a necrotic glow. The seeress Vaelith, whom the village trusted, proved to be an arch hag; she and her twin bound Shalk, Faela, Senator Virellen Zelis and others, and vanished by planar means. Vampires then raised the dead from the cliffs, the village fell, and the Moon Pool turned to black glass. Duskwillow now lies quarantined, and the Woaden Rangers have closed the region, calling it a divine blight.
