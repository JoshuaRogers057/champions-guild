## Where it stands

Foreign nations increasingly want permanent access to the Scar's resources and strategic position, and the Genasi keep resisting attempts to turn trade agreements into footholds. In the spring a company escorting two Kalashtar to the Skywake Festival reached the Scar just as an elemental crystal was stolen from Vaelor. The thieves proved to be the Kalashtar themselves, and the company turned them in. The crystal was put back, but it did not repair the damage: the floating islands kept destabilizing, and others began to show the same signs. No one yet understands what is stirring in the Scar's elemental currents.

## Unanswered questions

- What is destabilizing the Scar's elemental currents?
- Why did the Kalashtar want the crystal, and whom did they serve?
- Will the Genasi hold out against foreign footholds?
- Which nations want the Scar most?

**4 May 1026: May the Fourth Be With You**

A company escorted two Kalashtar across the desert to the Scar of Vaelor for the Skywake Festival, clearing giant scorpions from an oasis inn on the way. In the Scar an important elemental crystal was stolen, and from the accounts of the theft the company concluded that its own employers were responsible and turned them in. The elder Kalashtar was punished by the loss of a hand, and the company was given the sunblade used to do it. The crystal was returned, yet the islands kept destabilizing, and no one fully understands what is happening.
