## Where it stands

The Crown's increasingly centralized rail system and political control are provoking serious unrest among the duchies. Old tensions with Thundrakar and resistance to Contramara's growing authority are feeding it. In November, Baron Sel Treffalin, about to be named Royal Minister of Woods, was killed by a magical blast during his acceptance speech in Contramara. Druids of the Verdant Accord had hired several companies to kill him. A company of the Guild is now under suspicion, and an investigation is under way. The bomber has not been named.

**Latest development:** Baron Sel Treffalin was killed in Contramara, and a company of the Guild is under suspicion.

## Unanswered questions

- Which duchy will be first to break openly with Contramara?
- Will the Crown answer resistance with force or concession?
- How far will the old quarrel with Thundrakar feed the unrest?

**2 Nov 1026: the salty maiden**

At the Salty Maiden in Contramara, two druids of the Verdant Accord, Importasta and Natavia, hired a company of five to kill Baron Sel Treffalin. They called on the Baron instead and met Henry Fleshbark, a living tree-like man who hated the Verdant Accord and was controlling the Baron. At the Baron's speech, a blast killed him. Kennoth is suspected of planting the bead that caused it, but nothing is proven, and the company now stands under suspicion.
