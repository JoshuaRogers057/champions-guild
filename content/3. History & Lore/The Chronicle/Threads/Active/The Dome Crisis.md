## Where it stands

The Guild Archive records that the Vivisectionist, Dr. Vathros, was killed in his castle by the assembled Guild forces, and the Guild believes him dead. His death did not end the Dome Crisis. Three domes still stand over Eryndor, at Oathend, at Greyspire and over the wreck of Castle Bran, and the one man known to understand how to unmake them took that knowledge with him. Lord Mask, Lady Mask and Lord Varos remain at large. Creatures bred in the domes are now being studied for use as defences.

**Latest development:** In October the Guild broke into Professor Honeywell's HIVE bunker, destroyed the monster being built there, and left the remaining scientist to be arrested by the Ciliren guards.

## Unanswered questions

- How can the three standing domes be unmade now that Dr. Vathros is gone?
- Where have Lord Mask, Lady Mask and Lord Varos gone, and whom do they serve now?
- What became of Narthex after he left Dr. Vathros's castle?
- What ancient origin do the vault at Oathend, the Obelisk in Korivar, the Wall in Thundrakar and the Last Howl in Ciliren share?

### July 2026

<!-- quest:543 -->
**2 Jul 2026: Artificer to the Rescue**

Six members of the Guild entered the quarantine zone around Elkhara at Captain Varash's request, to find the missing artificers and bring out their research. On the way they found the remains of a company of monster hunters whose journals recorded how the mutated beasts stalked and adapted. The artificers were found alive, but the withdrawal turned desperate when a sentient mutation appeared that seemed to command the rest. The lead artificer, Zippy, has since produced a potion said to grant immunity to the mutations, and Narthex volunteered to lead a return.

*Guild members present:* [Scarlett Hawkins](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/355/backstory) · [Moorf Oakenbraid](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/426/backstory) · [Jaden](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/38/backstory) · [Orriata](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/398/backstory) · [Vax'ilya](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/393/backstory) · [Garrius Thorn](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/388/backstory)

**19 Jul 2026: The Lost Cult**

Baroness Selvara Thorne and Kellan Vare of the Veilkeepers of Oathend hired five members of the Guild to investigate an abandoned estate. They learned that Sire Treffalin, long thought a victim, had in fact founded the circle that destroyed the estate; he sacrificed his own family in pursuit of power over death and was sealed in a tomb below by his followers. The company recovered the forbidden tome known as The Rule of the Ninth Grave, but on the road out were ambushed by two figures calling themselves Lord Mask and Lady Mask, who escaped. The book was delivered safely to the Veilkeepers.

**24 Jul 2026: The Corupted**

Led by Narthex, five members of the Guild received a vaccine from Zippy and went into Elkhara, where they landed amid an assault on the Drakmir garrison's barricade and held it. They travelled upriver, rescued a group of looters trapped in a village chapel, and negotiated passage past intelligent mutations. At the artificers' laboratory they killed Shavar, one of the original mutated brothers. Narthex had by then revealed that he had not taken the vaccine and was walking the zone exposed to its influence.

**24 Jul 2026: The Nest**

The same company went on into the deepest part of Elkhara to destroy the Nest. On the way they met the vampire Bictra, an agent of the vivisectionist Dr. Vathros, who gave Narthex a means of reaching the doctor directly; Narthex by then admitted he had caught the mutation. The company killed Fenalith at the Nest, and with his death the source of the mutations was destroyed. Narthex took his leave and used Bictra's magic to seek out Dr. Vathros, and the mist that carried him away also took the Titan's Spine.

### August 2026

<!-- quest:602 -->
**10 Aug 2026: Veil Torn: Mini Epic! (Acolyte)**

The Guild Archive records the fall of Oathend. Narthex, once a member of the Guild and now corrupted, arrived at the Veilkeepers' manor with an item he said needed storing; what he released was a horde of undead, and a green mist rose that has not lifted. Five members of the Guild held the yard for an hour and a half, and nine villagers died regardless. They found Narthex in the chapel with a knife at Lady Selvara's throat, and Nox teleported her clear. A vampire in the manor told them that Oathend was the first of many and that the mist would consume Eryndor. The Guild then broke open the vault and freed a second Guild party sealed inside, and destroyed the machine below, which halted the dome's growth but did not end it. Oathend was lost.

<!-- quest:603 -->
**10 Aug 2026: Veil Torn: Mini Epic! (apprentice)**

A second Guild party descended into the vault beneath the Veilkeepers' manor to halt a new dome spreading across Kastalshire. The vault's construction resembles the Obelisk in Korivar, the Wall in Thundrakar and the Last Howl in Ciliren, suggesting a shared and poorly understood origin. In the vault they met the vampire Bictra again; Alaric and Aubrey were killed, and Aubrey disfigured Bictra in her last moments before Bictra was spirited away. The party then disabled the machinery driven by the Vivisectionist's lieutenants, Marshal Drazan Volsk and Iscander Rhuel, who escaped, and fought their way out. The dome stopped growing, the Veilkeepers had fallen, and Airie slipped away through an unknown portal.

**15 Aug 2026: Securing a legacy**

Five members of the Guild were sent to bring in Sir Kale, who had escaped the Dome and was being hunted by undead. He carried maps naming the three main targets of the coming assault: the Wall, the Labyrinth and the ruins of Castle Bran. Airie died getting Kale and the maps out, a hero in the Guild's service, and only a battered Guild badge came back. On the return a ghost released from a Bag of Holding nearly killed the party before it was destroyed.

**16 Aug 2026: Empire of Bones: Epic-05 (Acolyte)**

The Guild Archive records the Empire of Bones, the Guild's assault on the Vivisectionist's strongholds. This team approached Greyspire Fortress by sea, was dragged into the lower reaches by the Undertow, and fought upward, killing Marshal Drazan Volsk but never reaching the prisoners held below. The rituals were completed and the Greyspire Dome took hold, leaving the Wall and Thundrakar badly exposed. Avalin Florrelington, called Vali in the record, turned on the necromancer Malvaren Duskweaver as the dome closed, and the colour began to drain from her; the rest escaped believing her dead.

**16 Aug 2026: Empire of Bones: Epic-05 (Journeyman/Apprentice)**

Valdrin, Zila, Prime and Tristan were sent to the Last Tower on the border of the Drakmir Dominion and the Redmarch Coalition, to stop another dome rising through the Obsidian Obelisk beneath it. They found the tower's residents slaughtered, and below it an undead court and a mummified archaeologist weeping over an ancient mural. In the labyrinth they killed Bictra, avenging Aubrey and recovering her Guild badge. Lord and Lady Mask ambushed them and were recalled by the Vivisectionist, and in a portal realm Valdrin defeated Kalamathet again. They then joined the final assault on the Vivisectionist and earned the name the Unyielding.

**16 Aug 2026: Empire of Bones: Epic-05 (Journeyman)**

Davian Stone, Luca Rasvid, Digdug and Shedinn entered the mist around Dr. Vathros's castle with no way to call for aid. They destroyed one of the portals feeding his armies, freed prisoners, and found his laboratories. At the top Dr. Vathros offered to dismantle the domes and vanish for a hundred years if they would let him live, and the Guild refused twice. The fight lasted nearly two hours, with Shedinn killed and brought back by Jericho, Davian brought down nine times, and Narthex abandoning his master mid-battle. Reinforcements arrived through the portals, and Dr. Vathros was killed with a spear through the skull. Three domes remain, and the one who knew how to unmake them is gone.

*Guild members present:* [Luca Rasvid](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/285/backstory) · [Digdug](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/84/backstory) · [Shedinn](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/8/backstory) · [Davian Stone](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/181/backstory)

**16 Aug 2026: Empire of Bones: Epic-05 (Acolyte/Apprentice)**

This team tracked Lord Varos to a crashed section of Castle Bran, where he was raising a new dome. The necro-artificer Warrick Marrowspring had rebuilt one of the castle's towers to serve as its catalyst, held aloft by his magical chains. The Guild killed Marrowspring, but an elven woman with sealed lips took all of his research, and she and Varos escaped through a portal with Jericho and Vax'ilya in pursuit. The dome expanded, and Kyber'lex and Cage withdrew after assessing the labourers there so as not to be trapped inside.

*Guild members present:* [Vax'ilya](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/393/backstory) · [Kyber'lex the kobold](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/125/backstory) · [Jericho](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/29/backstory) · [Cage](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/332/backstory)

### October 2026

**10 Oct 2026: HIVE Bunker: Beta Testing**

Professor Honeywell asked the Guild to test a new defensive bunker, and the members found its trials relied on unethical practices and on improving dangerous specimens taken from the domes. They destroyed the defences and the monster being built there. The disturbance drew the Ciliren guards, who arrested the scientist left on site.
