## Where it stands

The Smiles Gang, a band of criminals dressed as clowns, has troubled Ciliren for more than a year, with ransom traps in the mountains, victims lured from the streets, and fencing for sea elves, all under the eye of a servant of Norebo. Guild companies have broken their schemes more than once, yet the gang's leader in the floating isles escaped through a portal, in ruins marked with the sign of Cyric that offered a trial of rebirth. The strange girl Nesser, kept in the gang's bunker, is unexplained. The Guild holds records of the guards the gang bribed and means to send members back to deal with them.

**Latest development:** A second team in the floating isles defeated the fallen angel guarding the temple's trial of rebirth, though the gang's leader there remains at large.

## Unanswered questions

- What is the Smiles Gang after in the floating isles?
- Where did the gang's leader go through the portal?
- What is Nesser, and why does the gang keep her?
- Is the servant of Norebo behind the gang also the one who leads it?

### August 2025

<!-- quest:142 -->
**2 Aug 2025: Down Bad**

Nox sent three members to find a missing husband, Roderick, whose wife Elise and daughter Trist said he had vanished the night before. A gang calling itself Smile, dressed as clowns, attacked the company, which learned that it served a servant of Norebo, god of gambles. Meeting the god's tests, they resisted the urge to wager more than they could bear to lose, and found that Roderick had gone willingly, risking everything for riches and permanently changed by one of his gambles. His wife could not accept his recklessness and left with the company for the Guild, to work there.

<!-- quest:147 -->
**12 Aug 2025: Taken**

A guard asked the Guild to look for a missing party of travelers in the mountains near Ciliren, and the members sent found themselves trapped under layers of earth and rock. The whole affair proved to be a trap laid by the Smile gang, and the company worked its way out and caught up with the gang before any ransom was paid for their 'recovery'. One member of the gang was taken and handed to the Ciliren guard, and the rest remained active.

### December 2025

<!-- quest:292 -->
**23 Dec 2025: The Naughty List**

Nox asked the Guild to look into homeless people vanishing from Ciliren. The company found that the Smiles Gang was back, luring victims with laced food and drink into a cave outside the city. There they met Putrice, a follower of the Frost Maiden, wiped out the group, and killed the Polaris member who was using the captives as sacrifices to summon an unknown beast. Some captives could not be saved in time, but the ritual was not completed.

### July 2026

<!-- quest:584 -->
**30 Jul 2026: Give Me the Money**

Newer Guild members were sent to look into the Smiles Gang, whose crimes in Ciliren were growing. Disguised as interested recruits and a noble in distress, they slipped into the gang's underground bunker and learned that sea elves were using the gang to fence stolen goods. They also met a young girl named Nesser, whose unnatural appearance and madness nearly broke the minds of Kellick and Sylvie. Working together, they took records of the stolen goods and of the guards the gang had bribed, so that other members could return to finish the job.

*Guild members present:* [Aspx](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/383/backstory) · [Sylvie](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/452/backstory) · [Kellick](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/348/backstory) · [Burnwick Firebeard](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/373/backstory)

### September 2026

<!-- quest:659 -->
**26 Sep 2026: Reach for the Skies**

The Guild answered a distress call from researchers of the Arcane Ward who were studying floating land masses over the Lexovar Woods. They found the Smiles Gang already there, curious about ruins marked with the sign of Cyric, god of lies. The company saved the two surviving researchers and their findings, but the gang's leader in that region escaped through a portal deeper into the floating isles. The matter was left to a later group to learn what the gang is doing there.

*Guild members present:* [Aspx](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/383/backstory) · [Riven Valaereth](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/422/backstory) · [Rowan Drake](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/501/backstory) · [Samson](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/439/backstory) · [Flash MCgalleon](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/386/backstory)

### October 2026

<!-- quest:665 -->
**3 Oct 2026: Reach for the Skies (Pt 2)**

A second team went up to the floating isles after the Smiles Gang and found more of its members corrupted into bestial forms, while others had fallen to the tests within the temple. The ruins proved to be a trial for those who sought rebirth, though age and the celestial set to run it had made the challenge impossible to meet. The company defeated the fallen angel left to watch over it as punishment, succeeding where lesser heroes ran or perished.
