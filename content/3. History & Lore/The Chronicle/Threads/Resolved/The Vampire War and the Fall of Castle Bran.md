## Where it stands

The Vampire War closes with the fall of Castle Bran. Through the autumn of 1025 members of the Guild met vampires at masquerades, in casinos and manors, and on the open sea, and two of their errands were set by Septimus Vex, who presented himself as a retired adventurer in need of relics and proved to be one of the undead. When the flying fortress, which the Guild may have unwittingly set loose over the Scar of Vaelor, was boarded in a three-team assault from the Guild's airship and brought down, the war lost its centre. Vex fell on the bridge but did not stay fallen, and only a second visit to the wreck ended him. What remains is the Dominion's cordon around the wreck, the dome that has risen over it, and the question of the vampires who escaped before it.

**Latest development:** In December 1025 a Guild party sent to inspect the crash site destroyed Septimus Vex for good among the ruins of Castle Bran, killed a shadow dragon named Darc, and let a band of looters go.

## Unanswered questions

- What became of Rovan ‘Redtide’ Marrick, who invited the Guild to dinner?
- Besides Dr. Vathros, where are the vampires freed at the masquerade, and the ten followers Lady Crowthorne sent out, now?
- Who is the master vampire who escaped the manor on the hill?
- Is Alfie, turned vampire and escaped as mist, still at large?

<!-- quest:183 -->
**6 Sep 1025: The Gambler's Veil**

A party infiltrated an underground casino at the docks, run by Jonric Thainholt, and won enough in the main room to reach the high-stakes table. When Shalk provoked him, Jonric attacked; with his death, the vampire familiars that served him turned on the patrons, and the party rescued many of them and took one culprit alive. **4 Oct 1025: An Awkward Gathering**

Shalk's marriage to Lady Faela Syrinthil stunned the noble courts of Rael, and it began with an ambush on the Driftshade Road, where assassins tried to keep him out of the city and left behind an order signed only with the initial C. When no priest would bless the union, Senator Virellen Zelis bound it under the civil law of Rael. That night Faela led the party to Syrinthil Manor, rumoured to have been seized by creditors, where the Ember Wolves' contract was being used by Velessara Dhalrune, a vampire agent of the Enshadowed, as cover for an undead terror meant to cripple the city through fear. The party killed her, but a barrier beneath the manor failed and the courtyard filled with undead; the Guild and the surviving Ember Wolves fought until dawn, and Rael still stood.

**16 Nov 1025: EPIC-03 (Apprentice)**

The Guild's assault on the flying fortress, Castle Bran, was made in three teams, and this one did not land at the castle but was launched into it from catapults on the Guild's airship. It crashed through the middle deck into a vampire celebration, cut it to pieces, and destroyed the Weather Engine below, which was kept running by a vampire overseer and defended by air elementals and ice mephits. On the command bridge the team met Septimus Vex, who dropped all pretence, and struck him down, though, being a vampire, he was not yet finished; the fortress began to sink, and the team escaped to the airship with all hands alive. Prisoners taken earlier in the season were recovered in the assault, among them Cragga Forgefrown and the noble Davian Stone, who afterwards joined the Guild.
