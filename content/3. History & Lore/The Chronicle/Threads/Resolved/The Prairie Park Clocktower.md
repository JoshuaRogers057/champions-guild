## Where it stands

The Guild Archive records that the clocktower ticks on and Prairie Park stands because of what was done at Dravonnis. Aysa carried the hourglass home and used it on the fracture, and the laboratory below was closed. Shedinn fell fighting the undead of the fort, and Fletcher held the time dragon at bay so that Aysa could escape. The Guild keeps both names in its records among those who gave their lives for others.

**Latest development:** Aysa returned alone from Dravonnis with the dragon's breath and used it to repair the time fracture beneath the clocktower.

## Unanswered questions

- Was Zephia ever cured, or did the illness take her?

### October 2025

<!-- quest:221 -->
**8 Oct 2025: The Hidden Heart**

The Guild Archive records that Aysa, Shedinn and Fletcher traced the ticking beneath the Prairie Park clocktower to a secret laboratory. They passed its traps and puzzles and read the chronomancy research of its former owner, Warton, and his wife Zephia. The records showed that Zephia had been dying of a terminal illness. An illusory Warton asked the party to bring him the breath of a time dragon to restore her.

### December 2025

<!-- quest:274 -->
**3 Dec 2025: The Dragon's Hourglass**

Aysa, Shedinn and Fletcher travelled to the ruined fort of Dravonnis and found a kobold research team already inside and the spirits of its old garrison caught in a time loop. They allied with Commander Ovarax, who helped them rescue most of the missing kobolds, but Shedinn fell in a battle with the undead in the fort's graveyard. Aysa captured the time dragon's breath in the hourglass, and Fletcher died holding the dragon back so that she could flee. She returned alone to the clocktower and used the hourglass to repair the time fracture.

*Guild members present:* [Shedinn](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/8/backstory) · [Aysa](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/16/backstory) · [Fletcher](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/44/backstory)

*Featured dispatch:* “Fletcher decided to make a last valiant sacrifice, and held off the dragon on his own as I ran.” (Aysa)
