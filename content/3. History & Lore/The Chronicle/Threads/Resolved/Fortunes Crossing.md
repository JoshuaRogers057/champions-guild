## Where it stands

The affair ended beneath Goldbrook Manor. The company went in as destitute guests at the Founders' Day feast and found a temple to Asmodeus and the gold meant for a dark ritual. They slaughtered the cultists. Jeffro then arrived and, enraged by the doom brought on his children, struck Thad down, and Zafan killed him in return with his burning maul. Istral and the Velvet Ledger secured the aftermath, and the party took what gold it could carry. The victory did not last. Days after Thad was buried, the company watched Miren taken at the tavern by infernal magic and dragged into the Nine Hells.

**Latest development:** Days after the fight at Goldbrook Manor, Miren was seized by infernal magic at the tavern and dragged into the Nine Hells as a Lemure.

## Unanswered questions

- What became of the Whisper Stone?
- Can Miren be brought back from the Nine Hells?
- What will the surviving Goldbrooks do now that Jeffro is dead?

### July 1025

<!-- quest:131 -->
**23 Jul 1025: Fortunes Crossing (Part 1)**

A brawl at Fortunes Crossing left one patron dead while the Watch kept out of it. Soon after, Dorian Pellshade offered a company of members city property in return for finding his missing friend, Jasper Belrose. Their search took them through the trinket shop Whims & Wonders, where they collected strange curios, among them a purple diamond, and on to a tavern in Saltgate and a Velvet Ledger hideout. They killed four kenku there and freed Sylas Vintarre, who named the Bronze Talons as Jasper's captors, just as Captain Elias Greythorn and the City Watch arrived.

### August 1025

<!-- quest:144 -->
**6 Aug 1025: Fortunes Crossing: Session 3**

The company reached the tavern on Silvershade Lane with the deed in hand and found it worn and haunted, its piano playing by itself and a ghost throwing Zed across the room. Sylas suggested contacts, among them the Goldbrook family, and Zafan grew evasive at the name. By morning the deed was signed over to Zed, Kairis and Thad, Zafan declining a share, and Zed's research gave the ghost's name as Tarrin. Zafan persuaded the spirit that they shared one dream, to make the tavern great, and Sylas then brought word of a repair bargain with the Stone & Timberwrights Guild.

<!-- quest:148 -->
**13 Aug 1025: Fortunes Crossing Session 4**

The company met Mira "Nails" Grindlehook, who offered to repair the tavern at no charge if they recovered a missing shipment of lumber. They traced it into the woods and found the lumber crew slain by an ettin. The Verdant Accord, a druidic faction, had drawn the creature to the site after the guild ignored warnings and began cutting a sacred grove. The party killed the ettin, returned the timber and secured the repairs.

### September 1025

<!-- quest:178 -->
**3 Sep 1025: Fortunes Crossing: Sesson 5**

With the repairs finished, the company hired a manager and prepared Tarrin's Tankard to open. A hospitality permit came easily, but a liquor license did not, because rumors of watered ale and shady dealing traced back to Thessa, who kept a rival tavern. The party slipped into her tavern and found evidence of her own off-the-books trade. They took it to Meryn, a former investigator, in the hope that he could clear the way for the license.

**17 Sep 1025: Session 6: The Grand Opening**

With the last permits secured, the company set about opening Tarrin's Tankard, though Kairis's promise of a launch within two days put them all on a sprint. Niv hurried to arrange food and drink contracts while the others built interest in the opening. The night began well until an explosion shook the street outside. The company searched the area, found a lead, and planned to visit the House of the Inspired Hands in the morning.

**20 Oct 1025: Fortunes Crossing: Session 9**

The party tracked down and destroyed Nix, the construct behind the explosion, and found in its chassis a map of Talvereth marked with a single X over a cemetery. There Zed and Thad broke into crypts at random, which brought a Knight of Kelemvor, who felled Thad with one stroke of his greatsword and had Zed taken by the city watch. The rest pressed on without Zed and found slain cultists of Asmodeus. One survivor, Vaelle, said she knew where the Stone had been taken and wanted revenge on the traitors who had left her to die.

**29 Oct 1025: Fortunes Crossing: Session 10**

Following leads past the Great Exchange, the party found Asmodean cultists and demons holding the Whisper Stone in an old windmill. A fiend took the Stone, broke out through a window, and passed it to a waiting carriage, which Kairis followed to the Goldbrook compound. Meanwhile Zed told the magistrate he had been working against the Velvet Ledger, named his companions, and tried to bribe a guard. Zafan and Thad were arrested at the Tankard, leaving only Kairis, under a false name, at liberty.

### December 1025

<!-- quest:277 -->
**8 Dec 1025: Fortunes Crossing: Session 15 (Finale?)**

Disguised as destitute guests at the Founders' Day feast, the company entered Goldbrook Manor and found a secret temple to Asmodeus beneath it, with the gold meant for a dark ritual. They slaughtered the cultists, and Jeffro then arrived and struck Thad down in his rage over the doom brought on his children. When Jeffro tried to burn Zafan in a column of fire, Zafan refused to fall and killed him with his burning maul. Istral and the Velvet Ledger secured the aftermath and let the party take what gold it could carry, but days after Thad's burial Miren was seized by infernal magic at the tavern and dragged into the Nine Hells as a Lemure.
