## Where it stands

The Guild Archive records that the railroad east began as a commission at Rails End and ended as a finished line to the Free City of Spens. Along the way the Guild sealed caves, fought tree spirits, bought four days' protection from the Brotherhood of the Rat and stopped robberies on the trains. When the last rails were laid, Spens had its railroad. The Guild's part in that work was done, though the city's other troubles were not.

**Latest development:** A Guild company escorted an archaeologist to Spens and stopped a train robbery, which let the railroad workers complete the track all the way into the city.

## Unanswered questions

- Was the stranger who called himself Alric of Halmark the Minister of Transportation, or someone else?
- What became of the vampires holding the ruined watchtower near the Drakmir Dominion border?

### July 2025

<!-- quest:111 -->
**4 Jul 2025: Progress Rolls On**

The Guild Archive records that the party met Captain Mary of the Queen's Pleasure and sailed with her to Contramara. There Lord Alric, Minister of Transportation of Kastalshire, set them their work for the railroad at Rails End, a mobile town. They met the railboss, and then rescued surveyors from local bandits.

<!-- quest:117 -->
**11 Jul 2025: Progress Rolls On. Part 2 Splinters on the Line**

The party solved the line's troubles one at a time, sealing a cave for the cut crew and fighting tree spirits for the lumberjack crew. In the struggle a lumberjack named Hadley was turned into a tree; a necromancy crystal was fed to him, and he fell into a strange state. They then met the Brotherhood of the Rat and a woman named Linda, to whom they gave shares in exchange for four days' protection, and set out for Contramara expecting a train to be robbed. Murcon Thunderhelm and Wizbit stayed behind to know Linda better.

*Guild members present:* [Ren Stray](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/4/backstory) · [Bogrum](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/15/backstory) · [Wizbit](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/5/backstory) · [Murcon Thunderhelm](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/3/backstory) · [Narthex](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/35/backstory) · [Aubrey](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/9/backstory)

**18 Jul 2025: Progress rolls on part 3**

At Contramara the party took part in a tournament, where Ren Stray did well at the joust and met a mysterious stranger. The stranger gave his name as Alric of Halmark and told Ren to call on him at the palace in need. The party also stopped a train robbery, which left their standing with the Brotherhood of the Rat in question.

### September 2025

<!-- quest:205 -->
**23 Sep 2025: Free City of Spens**

The Guild Archive records that the party travelled to the Free City of Spens, where the elven ambassador Lamar El' Lavor told them trade had slowed sharply, with bandits on the roads and pirates at sea. They dealt with bandits attacking the rails, and found evidence of a threat to the city coordinated by vampires, who appear to hold a ruined watchtower near the border of the Drakmir Dominion.

*Guild members present:* [Mazgar Thorrak](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/63/backstory) · [Maurice Seelie](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/11/backstory) · [Cragga Forgefrown ](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/61/backstory) · [Sombersun](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/104/backstory) · [Murcon Thunderhelm](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/3/backstory)

### October 2025

<!-- quest:217 -->
**5 Oct 2025: Eastbound and down**

Bo, Oren Nero, Rousseau, Kyber'lex the kobold and Bili Ruben were hired to escort an archaeologist to Spens, and succeeded admirably. They prevented a train robbery, which allowed the railroad workers to complete the tracks all the way to the city. They also helped the Sanama nomads hunt a clutch of ankhegs, and cleared a warehouse that had been the hideout of thugs shaking down the neighbourhood.

*Guild members present:* [Bo](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/113/backstory) · [Oren Nero](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/117/backstory) · [Rousseau](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/123/backstory) · [Kyber'lex the kobold](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/125/backstory) · [Bili Ruben](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/65/backstory)
