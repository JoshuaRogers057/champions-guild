## Where it stands

Management of the Kryzath outpost is settled. General Kiz had offered permission for a hall in the city in return for one more mission, and the Guild received it. The first manager, a Cracked Scale appointee, sold Anathema to a Dominion officer for questioning and was dismissed for it. Anathema, whose lineage appears to be both divine and draconic, took her place with the Dominion's approval.

**Latest development:** In late December 1025 a Guild party reconciled General Kiz with a young cadet, his illegitimate son Drakath, and called off the duel of honor between them.

## Unanswered questions

- What is Anathema's divine and draconic heritage, and what part will it play in the Dominion?
- How will the Cracked Scale answer the dismissal of Softstep?

### November 1025

<!-- quest:269 -->
**29 Nov 1025: Establishing The Outpost**

Shedinn, Digdug, Eireth, Ki Ka'Rah and Besswynne Mòr arrived in Kryzath and hired Sirikra ‘Softstep’ Vellune, who is tied to the Cracked Scale crime syndicate, to run day-to-day operations at the guild hall, bringing in Anathema to help. They let a band of deserters escape with money, and their leader, Gaz, promised Shedinn a favor in return. They had the papers stamped and began repairs on the hall. At the Scolded Scale Inn they fell foul of cocky cadets from the Draconic Legion Academy and their leader Kleon, and Besswynne struck him, so a duel of honor was set for noon the next day.

**30 Nov 1025: Drakmir Sage part 2**

On their second day in Kryzath the party made a strong impression on the locals. At the Academy's training grounds they defeated Kleon's squad of cadets, to the surprise of several instructors, and Ki Ka'Rah cleared a lumber camp of a fiery infestation by burning down the corrupted structure. Overnight Eireth robbed the home of Marvo the Marvelous, who had stolen her true name, reclaimed it, and handed the evidence of his deceit to the Ashbringers. The next morning Anathema, having taken blunt advice from Digdug about earning respect, struck Softstep in the face with his shovel.

<!-- quest:272 -->
**30 Nov 1025: Drakmir Saga part 3**

The party secured the Dominion's obsidian quarry from shadow-twisted creatures that had crept in after the fall of Castle Bran, settled a dispute among the elite glassworkers, and won the notice of the Ashbringer Mercenaries and the friendship of the instructor Pelvar Ironcrest. In their absence Softstep, the provisional manager, sold Anathema to a Dominion officer named Kleon, who took her to a storm-shrouded castle to be interrogated. After praying to Lathander, the party was carried to the castle, freed Anathema, defeated Kleon's forces and found clues to her divine and draconic heritage. Back in Kryzath, Softstep was dismissed for the betrayal, Anathema was made outpost manager, and with the Dominion's approval the Guild's new stronghold was established.

### December 1025

<!-- quest:282 -->
**13 Dec 1025: Simply Theft (Skye Personalized)**

Eireth was threatened by Selerion Vaelthar, a man from her past, and brought Ki Ka'Rah, Alexander Rivers and Maul M.D. to rob a casino. The attempt failed, so they killed him instead. Alexander spent two days in jail for belligerence toward the guards and for his part in a killing in broad daylight, and was acquitted when his story was verified. The party also did great damage to the Kryzath outpost with spells and was charged for the repairs.

*Guild members present:* [Maul M.D.](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/17/backstory) · [Eireth](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/145/backstory) · [Ki Ka'Rah](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/178/backstory) · [Alexander Rivers](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/135/backstory)

<!-- quest:296 -->
**28 Dec 1025: Sins of the Father**

Lily of the Wood, Donder Stormbringer, Hunter and Zhek'har Valoran travelled to the Drakmir Dominion, where General Kiz, a friend to the Guild, had refused a duel of honor under mysterious circumstances. They found that the challenger, a young cadet named Drakath, was Kiz's illegitimate son, and chose to avoid violence and see the two reconciled. With the duel cancelled, the party took a random job posting to hunt an owlbear and secured the road from it.

*Guild members present:* [Donder Stormbringer](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/195/backstory) · [Zhek'har Valoran](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/186/backstory) · [Hunter](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/187/backstory) · [Lily of the Wood](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/110/backstory)
