## Where it stands

The matter of Naerwyn closes with the old temple cleared and one member of the Guild lost. The party had gone in on a contracted recovery job, reportedly racing other factions for the relics of the site, and the Velvet Ledger appears to have got below first. Vorga died holding back a powerful undead entity so that the others could reach the surface. Of what she faced, and of what the Ledger set loose, little is known. Kestrel, changed by what he found there, was restored only when Davian later carried the book taken from the ruins to Dr. Vathros.

**Latest development:** The expedition ended in January 1026 with Vorga dead and Davian Stone maimed; Kestrel, transformed there, was restored months later.

## Unanswered questions

- What became of Kestrel, said to have been transformed into a larval state?

**7 Jan 1026: The Silence of Naerwyn**

Five members of the Guild ventured to the untamed edges of Thalenmark in search of the secrets of the Naerwyn ruins. As they approached the great pyramid, the stone floor crumbled beneath them and dropped the whole party into the dark halls below. They were trapped, with no clear way back to the surface.

**18 Jan 1026: The Silence of Naerwyn (Part 3)**

The expedition ended in severe loss. The party had entered Naerwyn on a contracted recovery job, reportedly competing against other factions for relics, and later evidence suggests the Velvet Ledger reached the lower chambers first and broke open a sealed structure whose contents remain unclear. Vorga was killed in the retreat, confronting a powerful undead entity to delay its advance, and her body was not recovered. Davian Stone escaped but lost an arm, from either a trap or a hostile creature, and Kestrel is said to have been transformed into a larval state.

*Guild members present:* [Kestrel Veydrak ](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/139/backstory) · [Tannis Pendragon](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/173/backstory) · [Davian Stone](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/181/backstory) · [Vorga Emberthane](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/119/backstory) · [Zhek'har Valoran](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/186/backstory)
