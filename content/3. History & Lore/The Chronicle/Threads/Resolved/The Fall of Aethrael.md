## Where it stands

The Chosen of Lathander were gathered over a few months, named one by one, and tested against the Dreaming Dark's agents in the Woad, at the Veiled Chorus and in the Astral Sea. Their last act was to close two connected rifts at the same moment, above and below, and to take Aethrael alive; he remains imprisoned. Skraggapost was emptied of danger before the bridge fell, and not one of its people was lost. Some elements of the Better Dawn escaped, and the greater Dreaming Dark still lies beyond Eryndor, but the door Aethrael built is gone. The old darkness Lathander warned of was not destroyed, only denied its way in.

**Latest development:** In August the assembled Chosen of Lathander closed the rifts at Skraggapost, saved its whole population and captured Aethrael.

## Unanswered questions

- What judgment awaits Aethrael?

### December 2025

<!-- quest:280 -->
**22 Dec 2025: Chosen of Lathander Saga**

Mazgar, Kairis, Aubrey and Ki Ka'Rah set out to restore Shedinn to life, and Lathander set them nine items to gather as a test. They rescued two children from owlbears, stopped a train crash, and passed the monks' Trial of Potential, after which Mazgar received the stone of leadership. They also freed the kobold artificers' kin from the Cracked Scale, who now know what the company did. At an old battlefield they saw the memories of fallen heroes, and Kairis received a pipe of memories.

**26 Dec 2025: Lathander Saga 3**

With Shedinn back, the Iron Marches district gathered to welcome him. Mazgar as Herald of Lathander, Shedinn as Shield and Ki Ka'Rah as Wand set out with Aubrey and uncovered a plot to assault the dreams of Eryndor, drawing the Quari out of Dal Quor and spreading a narcotic glass across the land. They freed Dr. Salussan in exchange for information, and learned that five Chosen exist across Eryndor, in Kastalshire, Kar Dromm, Lexovar and the Drakmir Dominion. The enemy was stalled, but the threat was far from over.

### June 2026

<!-- quest:505 -->
**5 Jun 2026: Dead Weight (Lathander Calls)**

Mazgar, Shedinn, Eldarin and Everrett, newly named Prayer and Wrath of Lathander, went into the Woad with Airie to find Veyren Solace, a Lucent-Bound servant of the Dreaming Dark. In an old vampire mansion Veyren had raised the dead with the Carrion Throne Shard and set them to building a Dream Anchor. The company fought through the dead, defeated Veyren and destroyed the anchor before it took hold. The shard was delivered to Kellan Vare.

### July 2026

<!-- quest:565 -->
**14 Jul 2026: The Dawn’s Summons**

Elyrion, a Solar of Lathander and the Chosen's main contact, sent six members of the Guild to find the missing Planetar Aethrael. They found him at the Veiled Chorus, where he had become Inspired and was using the monastery's Philosophy Day to preach enforced harmony: a world without hunger or fear, bought by giving up the freedom to choose otherwise. Eldarin led the party through the arguments, broke Aethrael's influence and saved everyone in the monastery. Aethrael escaped before he could be defeated.

### August 2026

<!-- quest:588 -->
**1 Aug 2026: Tracking Trouble (Eldarin)**

Eldarin led Shedinn, Everrett, Rhanak and Tristan in the hunt for Aethrael. They saved the Monastery of Potential, where Eldarin was named Lightwarden of Sol'Aenor, and Abbot Varesh Talan joined them, seeking vengeance on the Lucent-Bound. An explosion threw them into the Astral Sea, where the artificer Farifax helped them escape, and the party learned that Aethrael meant to open a great rift at Skraggapost to admit the Dreaming Dark. Two connected rifts, one above and one below, would have to be closed at the same moment.

<!-- quest:590 -->
**3 Aug 2026: Chosen Assemble**

The Chosen of Lathander gathered at Skraggapost, with Aubrey standing in for Ki Ka'Rah, and divided between the land below and the Astral Sea. They destroyed every anchor in Aethrael's network in step with one another and collapsed his bridge before the Dreaming Dark could settle in Eryndor. The whole population of Skraggapost was teleported to the Guild hall in Lexovar, and Aethrael was captured alive. Word spread through the Redmarch Coalition, where the Chosen are now spoken of with honour.

*Guild members present:* [Mazgar Thorrak](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/63/backstory) · [Digdug](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/84/backstory) · [Eldarin Solari](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/310/backstory) · [Rhanak Steelwind](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/363/backstory) · [Shedinn](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/8/backstory) · [Tristan Hammerborn](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/182/backstory) · [Everrett Stonehelm](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/12/backstory) · [Ki Ka'Rah](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/178/backstory) · [Aubrey](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/9/backstory)
