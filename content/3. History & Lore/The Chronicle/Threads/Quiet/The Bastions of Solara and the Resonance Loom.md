## Where it stands

The Guild Archive records that Archbishop Boel's conspiracy to steal the materials of Starforged arms ended with his trial and execution before the Tribunal of the White Sword. His rescuers, Lord Varos Neightreign and the elven woman Faelassa, escaped, and Lieutenant Rahmiel has sworn that Varos will answer for his treachery. Elsewhere in the Bastions the Night Lady's school in Auris Prime has been broken but not ended, since some of its pupils refused to leave. The Ashwing Cinder-Marshal Sariel Veyne has all but finished the Resonance Loom, a device meant to suppress the will of whole crowds, and about forty children vanished on the night he completed it.

**Latest development:** The Guild investigated the Ashwing flight of Auris Prime, found Sariel Veyne completing the Resonance Loom beneath the Hall of the First Oath, and learned that some forty children had disappeared that night.

## Unanswered questions

- Where are Lord Varos Neightreign and Faelassa, and has the pursuit Rahmiel promised set out?
- Where did the forty missing children go, and is the Resonance Loom finished?
- Is the Night Lady the same person as the Nightmother who refitted Marrowspring's blueprints for the Ashwing flight?
- What was the verdict on Maevre Lume, Varrus Cind and Orvek Thane?

### May 2026

<!-- quest:498 -->
**24 May 2026: A Mole Among Us**

The Guild Archive records that Lieutenant Rahmiel of the Bastions of Solara asked the Guild to find who had stolen the materials set aside for Starforged weapons and armor. Because Rahmiel suspected officials of high rank, Mazgar Thorrak, Airie Lonewolf, Everrett Stonehelm, Brerien the wanderer and Borba worked somewhat outside the law, searching the derelict quarters of the Starforges. The trail led to a conspiracy of espionage and corruption reaching the very top of the Bastions. Archbishop Boel was summoned before the Grand Jury of Solara with Maevre Lume, Varrus Cind and Orvek Thane, but Boel fled the Bastions and is wanted by the state, while the other three were to stand trial ten days later.

### August 2026

<!-- quest:611 -->
**13 Aug 2026: A Judgment Most High**

The Guild was summoned again by Lieutenant Rahmiel of the Dawnguard Legionnaire. Archbishop Boel, found responsible for the disappearances and the unlawful making of Starforged arms, had been tried before the Tribunal of the White Sword, found guilty by the Triumvirate of Radiance and sentenced to death by the Procession of Lights. Torgar Solemn Brother, Bison, Jericho, Jaden, Borba and Eldarin Solari were sent to keep the execution secure, and found that Lord Varos Neightreign of the Luminous Counsel, long in the service of the necrotic arts and afflicted with vampirism, was working with an elven woman named Faelassa to save the archbishop. The Guild saw Boel to his death, but Varos and Faelassa fled; the party then helped restore some normality to the Gleamgate Commons in Auris Prime while Rahmiel readied a team to pursue them.

**22 Aug 2026: The Road Between Lights**

The Guild Archive records that Tievor Tole, Dr. Goldfoot, Lucian Black, Ambrose Callowmere and Skritch escorted five priests and a precious cargo by wagon from the Gleamgate Commons of Auris Prime to the Starforges. On the road they met mindless, shambling humanoids and giant centipedes warped by necrotic magic, which showed more interest in the wagon than in its escort. A monster burst from the ground on the second night, and on the third a hulking creature called a Marrowspine joined the fight in a ravine, leaving the shambling creatures swifter and more purposeful. The wagon reached the Starforges battered but whole, and Rahmiel at once began to write further contracts for the Guild.

<!-- quest:628 -->
**27 Aug 2026: A Voice Beneath the Choir**

At Rahmiel's request Kaya Sunwalker, Aurariel Amberdell, Julian Valerius, Tievor Tole and Zinen Wildeye looked into the disappearance of children in Auris Prime, each of whom had drawn a shadowy woman, an eye with a broken halo and a school, and had dreamed of the same woman. They were told to be discreet, and to bring the children home safely above all. The company followed a girl, Seris Valane, out of the city to a mansion like a boarding school, where they were drawn into an unsteady pocket of reality, defeated its guardian the Broken Eye, and then fought and killed the aasimar Preceptor Vaelis. The children were freed, though not all wished to leave: the Night Lady, whom the elder students call the Mistress, has woven a loyal following and is teaching them how to overthrow a government.

<!-- quest:630 -->
**28 Aug 2026: Clear the Marsh**

Sister Meriel Thane, who had stepped down as a candidate for the vacant seat of Archbishop to serve under Rahmiel, asked three prospective members, Amir, Thorfin Stonebreaker and Jenny Black, to help the garrison of Mirewatch Outpost against necrotic creatures attacking the north-eastern outposts of the Gleaming Spire. After a skirmish, Captain Caelis Rhyne sent them into the marsh to learn where the creatures came from. They found a young marrow crawler, one of Warrick Marrowspring's leftover creations, and learned that the Black-Organ Heart, another of his creations, was calling to the rest and growing stronger. Guided by a prayer of Thorfin's, they smashed the crystalline Heart, though Jenny and Amir were nearly drowned by it first.

### September 2026

<!-- quest:649 -->
**13 Sep 2026: Ashes of the First Oath**

The Guild Archive records that Therion Grace, Jenny Black, Lug StoneBlood, Sylas Nightshade and Aspx went to Auris Prime on Rahmiel's word, passed on by Sister Meriel because Rahmiel believed Ashwing watchers were reporting his every move. They were to investigate the local Ashwing flight, seize the lesser members behind the theft of calibration tools and oath-sealing wax, and send for Rahmiel by sunstone rather than face the flight's leader. Their search, through the home of the missing oathwright Maelis Dorn and on to Furnace Nine in the Hollow Steps, led them past a failed Marrowspring experiment that the Nightmother had refitted for her own ends, and on to the Vault of the Sacred Vows beneath the Hall of the First Oath. There the Cinder-Marshal Sariel Veyne, Rahmiel's childhood friend and oathbrother, had already copied two of the three oath-seals needed for the Resonance Loom, a device of mass mind-control, and the party watched him begin the last Loom Anchor before withdrawing; that night some forty children disappeared.
