## Where it stands

The Dusk Queen's soul was broken into pieces, and Guild companies have taken several of them from her: a glowing staff, a corrupted unicorn, and the five shards of a broken mirror. The last of these now lie with the Oakspire Enclave, a druidic order sworn to destroy her. The Queen has not been laid to rest, and she is hunting both the shards and the Enclave. Synthia, the girl the Guild took in, belongs to the Enclave, and its members want her back; no one knows whether she has gone.

**Latest development:** The shards of the Dusk Queen's soul were delivered to the Oakspire Enclave just ahead of the Queen's pursuit, and the Enclave demanded Synthia's return.

## Unanswered questions

- Has Synthia gone back to the Oakspire Enclave, or is she still hidden at the Guild?
- What was the creature that escaped the demiplane in the Lexovar Woods?
- Who sent the kidnappers, and who cursed the commoners of the Slums District?
- How many pieces of the Dusk Queen's soul are still unaccounted for?

### March 1026

<!-- quest:391 -->
**21 Mar 1026: A Maze of Wonder**

Mr. Cuttles asked the Guild to clear a maze he had found, and it proved to be a dungeon tied to the Dusk Queen. Past traps, puzzles and guardians, the company met an elven woman and a halfling man who had wandered in seeking something precious, unaware that hundreds of years had passed within the tomb. They helped the woman find her lost lover's locket, then released the Dusk Queen and brought her down, leaving only her glowing staff. Back at the Guild they learned that the staff was a phylactery holding a fraction of her soul, and that more pieces might wait elsewhere.

### April 1026

<!-- quest:419 -->
**8 Apr 1026: Unicorn Sighting**

Sent to confirm a unicorn sighting in the woods south of Ciliren, the company walked into a trap laid by another piece of the Dusk Queen. They fought cursed humans turned into animals, spoke with beasts that had forgotten the common tongue, and befriended a girl turned half spider. After defeating the corrupted unicorn they broke the piece housing the Queen's power, which restored the woods and the cursed souls, and the girl, Synthia, and a wood elf named Noir came back with them to the Guild. Synthia would not name her family, fearing her fun would end, but she showed strong gifts as a priestess in training of Mielikki.

### June 1026

<!-- quest:535 -->
**24 Jun 1026: Twisted Shadows**

Polaris, a known enemy of the Guild, called for help in looking into the recent actions of one of its own members in Thundrakar. The company found that a rogue member was empowering creatures of the Dusk Queen to steal the shadows and souls of the people there, strengthening a link to her domain. They also met Frost Icescales, wife of the current leader of the local goliath clan, who had saved many wandering goliaths by her ingenuity. The scheme was stopped and many of the stored souls released, though a few were taken by the Polaris member Bulari, and the company learned that the shadows kept some of their former minds and befriended one, which they named Greg.

### July 1026

<!-- quest:553 -->
**5 Jul 1026: A Show of Power**

Mr. Cuttles sent six members to take down a new demiplane in the Lexovar Woods, where the Dusk Queen had used pieces of her soul, set as the shards of a broken mirror, to guard it. The company fought through the plane's warped creatures and collected all five shards, which destroyed the plane. Among the ruins lay the remains of an ancient dragon, and the company fed it the soul of a fae nymph in a bargain to win the shard it held. They were told that only one creature escaped before the domain was gone for good.

*Guild members present:* [Davian Stone](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/181/backstory) · [Luca Rasvid](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/285/backstory) · [Vaelynn Whisperwind](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/79/backstory) · [Eldarin Solari](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/310/backstory) · ["Zed" Zedicus d'Thuranni](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/55/backstory) · [Ki Ka'Rah](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/178/backstory)

### August 1026

<!-- quest:631 -->
**29 Aug 1026: Far From Home**

Synthia asked the Guild to return a baby owlbear named Paws to its mother. The company crossed the woods, fought forest gnomes and other woodland creatures, and faced the enraged mother, who was undergoing a strange transformation. They also met members of Synthia's enclave, who called her Synthesia and meant to take her home, and the Guild kept her hidden, since she preferred her freedom for the present. Paws was reunited with its mother and the company returned.

### September 1026

<!-- quest:647 -->
**12 Sep 1026: Ill Intentions**

Captain Vince Weathers hired the Guild to guard a fair in the Slums District, which came under attack by cursed commoners who caused havoc as a distraction while others sought to kidnap Synthia. The company put down the attackers and tracked them through the Lexovar Woods, where the realm of the fae was reaching into the mortal world. They overtook the would-be kidnappers and rescued Synthia before she could be spirited away.

<!-- quest:654 -->
**19 Sep 1026: Dangerous Goods**

The Guild was charged with carrying the shards of the Dusk Queen's soul to the Oakspire Enclave, a druidic order sworn to destroy her and all she stands for. On learning that Synthia, the Guild's stowaway, belonged to the Enclave, its members demanded her return as well. A mishap with a teleportation circle let the Dusk Queen strike at the Guild for a time, and the shards then had to be carried through shadow monsters, rival parties after the package, and an elven settlement under attack. The company delivered the shards before the Dusk Queen herself could catch them.

*Guild members present:* [Torgar Solemn Brother](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/372/backstory) · [Red Moon](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/268/backstory) · [Lucian Black](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/479/backstory) · [Sma-Gol](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/288/backstory) · [Kestrel Veydrak ](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/139/backstory)
