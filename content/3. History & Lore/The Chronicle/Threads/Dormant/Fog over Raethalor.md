## Where it stands

The Guild Archive records that Raethalor lies under Sire Valkek's permanent garrison, and that Countess Vyra Nashen has been turned out of her manor. The hag Melthora stands behind much else: Captain Redfall swore to return her cursed music box, and she now holds bargains with several members of the Guild, who owe her favours. Tallo, the boy mid-way through a monstrous transformation, was carried to her in the hope that her price for his healing could be paid, but the archive does not record how that ended. The orphanage keeper, Mistress Ellid, is likewise unaccounted for. Elsewhere a vampire lord has fallen, and the vampires he held walk free.

**Latest development:** At Stonehall, Bogrum buried his father, killed his brother and learned disturbing things about his pact with Melthora.

## Unanswered questions

- What became of Tallo after he was taken to Melthora?
- What will Melthora ask of those who owe her favours?
- Did Captain Redfall return the music box to Melthora, and what did he want in return?
- What became of Mistress Ellid, the orphanage keeper?

**29 Jul 2025: The Secret of Cavanth's Cove**

Commissioned by the sailor Captain Redfall, the party entered the long-abandoned grotto of the pirate Captain Cavanth in search of a jewel-encrusted child's music box said to have belonged to the hag Melthora. They fought twisted creatures there, and learned that the many-headed beast in the depths was Saxson, Cavanth's own daughter, transformed by the hag's curse. They took the music box from a hidden vault and gave it to Redfall, who swore to return it to Melthora, though his motives remain his own.

*Guild members present:* [Fletcher](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/44/backstory) · [Nephele ](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/19/backstory) · [Aysa](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/16/backstory) · [Warjo](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/6/backstory) · [Zacari Seras](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/21/backstory)

### August 2025

<!-- quest:140 -->
**1 Aug 2025: Fog and Whispers**

The Guild Archive records that Mistress Ellid, keeper of the orphanage, had been kidnapped and the boy Tallo was missing. The party learned that illegitimate nobles had vanished across Kastalshire, and that Sire Valkek had brought a permanent garrison to Raethalor and evicted Countess Vyra from her manor. They rescued Tallo and found him in the midst of a transformation; after debating whether to kill him, they chose to take him to the hag Melthora in the hope of paying her price for his healing. The countess told them of a smugglers' cove where a ship could be had.

*Guild members present:* [Aubrey](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/9/backstory) · [Bogrum](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/15/backstory) · [Narthex](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/35/backstory) · [Wizbit](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/5/backstory) · [Ren Stray](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/4/backstory) · [Murcon Thunderhelm](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/3/backstory)

<!-- quest:150 -->
**15 Aug 2025: Fog and war 1**

The party met the hag Melthora and made bargains. A member recorded only as Gram became a fifty-year-old halfling who will now age backwards, owing two favours; Narthex took a crystal spellbook of endless pages, owing one; and Wizbit was given a ship, the Myrra, and the promise of his genie's return, with Melthora as his patron. Bogrum had one eye turned into a Hag's Eye tied to Melthora, and learned that his father had been killed by his brother and that he must go to Thundrakar to put things right. Murcon and Ren took nothing, but Ren was told that Mira had been kidnapped.

*Guild members present:* [Aubrey](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/9/backstory) · [Murcon Thunderhelm](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/3/backstory) · [Ren Stray](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/4/backstory) · [Wizbit](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/5/backstory) · [Narthex](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/35/backstory) · [Bogrum](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/15/backstory)

**29 Aug 2025: Giant Slayer**

Vaelynn Whisperwind, Wizbit and Everrett Stonehelm joined Bogrum in his village, Stonehall, for the funeral of his father, Fanir. Banished from his clan, Bogrum made his case to inherit his father's possessions, and it was agreed that a competitive giant hunt would decide it. Wizbit took the Myrra for his own and holds documents that prove his legal ownership of the vessel. Bogrum killed his brother, and learned some personal and upsetting things about his pact with Melthora.

*Guild members present:* [Bogrum](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/15/backstory) · [Wizbit](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/5/backstory) · [Everrett Stonehelm](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/12/backstory) · [Vaelynn Whisperwind](https://championsguildrpg.com/characters/79/backstory)
