## Aftermath & Active Crises

*Recent victories changed the world. They did not settle it.*

### [[The Dome Crisis]]
*Active · World · Mysteries & Arcana*

The Dome Crisis began at a masquerade in August 2025. When members of the Guild killed the vampire lord Tavard there, the vampires he held in thrall went free, and one of them, the necromancer Dr. Vathros, went his own way. He went to the Dome over Zindariel, which had stood long before him and whose making nobody understands, and made it his headquarters, since necromancy is stronger inside it than anywhere else in Eryndor. There he studied it until he learned how to make more, and many of the monsters that have since spread across the continent came out of his laboratories. In the summer of 2026, known by then as the Vivisectionist, he went to war and raised new domes, each about a mile across, over the vault beneath the Veilkeepers' manor at Oathend, the wreck of Castle Bran and Greyspire Fortress. When the Guild reached him in his castle in August he was a lich as well as a vampire, the first time anyone had seen him in that form. They destroyed him, but the only one known to understand how to unmake the domes took that knowledge with him. Lord Mask, Lady Mask and Lord Varos remain at large. In October, members of the Guild who tested a defensive bunker for Professor Honeywell found it built on specimens taken from the domes, and destroyed it. The map of Eryndor has been permanently changed.

### [[Northern Kastalshire]]
*Ongoing · Nation · Mysteries & Arcana*

The druidic and treant crisis in northern Kastalshire has continued to worsen. Groves have gone silent, the old relationship between the forest folk and the Crown is breaking down, and something is changing in the northern forests. Members of the Guild have answered calls from druids in other forests, where a corrupting figure called the Druid of Wrath is spoken of, but nothing known ties those calls to the north. There is no confirmed account of what has happened in the northern groves.

### [[The Fall of Talvereth]]
*Ongoing · Nation · Crises & Wars*

Talvereth came through the riots of the Shadow War badly scarred, and the Gilded Council is broken. Guild companies retook the courts, the docks and the districts at the cost of Koal EmberHome and Warjo, and though the Velvet Ledger has been struck in its warehouses and tunnels it still works; a shipment of Glass seized at the docks pointed toward two members of the Council. The Bastions of Solara keep an army outside the city and ambassadors within it, and press both for reconstruction and for a restructuring of the Council that would divide representation between the halflings and the other peoples of the city. Their armies have also strained the trade routes with fees and seizures. Perrin Grint now stands as heir to the Quiet Ledger, an intelligence network his father built to oppose the Velvet Ledger. Negotiations over the city's future are still under way, and whatever they settle will reach beyond Talvereth to trade across Eryndor.

### [[The Rebuilding of Spens]]
*Ongoing · City · Crises & Wars*

Spens is rebuilding after a coalition of pirate crews from the Dreadwake took it in the spring. Guild members who had just helped to obtain a map to the Eye of Kaos escaped the city as it fell. It was later given back, bought with one of the keys the Guild's company carried, in a bargain struck with the Driftbone Armada under the authority of the hag Veysha. The city survived, but its political, criminal and economic landscape has been badly disrupted. In the summer the Guild found a secret fighting pit whose owner proved to be a devil, and a resort where a man was held in an enchanted sleep by one. No one knows who now holds the city's districts.

### [[The Corruption of Kalamathet]]
*Quiet · Nation · Crises & Wars*

The corrupted dragon Kalamathet has been killed twice: once in his nest in the desert, where the cure for Heliothrax was completed and Glassroot Grove saved, and again in a portal realm during the Guild's assault on the Vivisectionist. He had last been placed in the Labyrinth, and his ultimate fate was held to be unresolved. The Crimson Scale taken from his body vanished from Molly Quilhart's pack and is in unknown hands. Given his history, members of the Guild decline to treat his death as certain.

### [[The Eye of Kaos]]
*Dormant · Nation · Mysteries & Arcana*

The Eye of Kaos, a vast ship hidden beyond reach, has not been brought to account, and its destination and intentions remain unknown. Scarlett Hawkins's company gathered keys from island to island and held three of them when talks with the Driftbone Armada, which held two, bought the freedom of Spens at the price of one. Eliot Marsh then stole a key and escaped, and he has since taken blood from Guild members twice: a drop from Axum, and a vial from each member of a later company. Nothing is known of any pursuit.

### [[The Rat and the Scale]]
*Dormant · Faction · Intrigue & Crime*

Criminal organisations across Eryndor are growing more organised, wealthy and politically entrenched, and the Brotherhood of the Rat, Dreadwake interests, the Velvet Ledger and others appear to be pieces of one larger struggle for the underworld. In Rakkath the Guild broke the Cracked Scale's hold, though a few outposts remain: Vorthak Cinderscale still controls the area around a warehouse, and Klein, a Rat pirate, has taken Rakkath itself. The assassin Leothin has never been caught, and Serrixa has escaped the Guild more than once and is loose again. Aveline has split from the Rats and gathered the pirate admirals to seize the free city of Spens, with the Guild of Blades clearing its defenders first. Spens did fall to a pirate armada in April, and was later bought free in a bargain struck in the Fanged Swamp.

## Nations in Motion

*Governments, cultures, resources, and new technology are shifting the balance of power.*

### [[The Airship Race]]
*Ongoing · World · Crises & Wars*

Airships are rapidly changing military and economic power across Eryndor. The Drakmir Dominion, Talvereth and Lexovar already own one or more, while Kastalshire, Sylvandar and the Forged Sands are close to fielding their own. The Guild has sailed in them itself, and has been hired to feed the trade: one company gathered silk for airship sails in canyons on the border of the Drakmir Dominion and the Forged Sands, and another recovered a compass built to work aboard an airship from thieves who meant to sell it to the Redmarch Coalition. In March a first airship, nearly finished in a Thalenmark warehouse, was taken in a raid that killed every worker on the site, and no one claimed it.

### [[Kar Dromm in Crisis]]
*Ongoing · Nation · Crises & Wars*

Assassinations, vacant clan seats, unresolved succession struggles and disputes over the dwarves' right to ancient masterworks have created growing instability throughout Kar Dromm. No single crisis has broken the nation, but several are beginning to overlap. Guild envoys to Thumdar found the road ambushed and Stonegate Hold missing its trade ledger and pass-seal, stolen by Rok Rot smugglers who had help from inside. Earlier, in the autumn of 2025, dwarven mercenaries of the Rok Rot company told the Guild that a pale prophet was rallying clan after clan and stirring trouble. No one is known to have found the prophet.

### [[Polaris in Thundrakar]]
*Active · Nation · Intrigue & Crime*

Polaris is an arcane order the Guild has met in dungeons, on the tundra, at sea and across the gate to the Abyss, always at work on fragments, souls or fiends. Over a year the Guild has thwarted it again and again and earned the respect of several goliath clans, and in October it caught up with Polaris's remaining forces in Thundrakar's eastern mountains and kept them from the Stygian devil Stagor's animated remains. Drogath Stormvein will not rule Thundrakar forever, and uncertainty surrounds what comes after him. Polaris continues to operate while foreign powers quietly watch the coming succession, and its purpose remains unknown.

### [[The First Arbiter]]
*Ongoing · Nation · Intrigue & Crime*

Sylvandar is approaching the selection of its next First Arbiter, whose term is nearing its end. The post is appointed by the King and confirmed by the Senate, and the Senate is evenly balanced. Competing visions for how aggressively Sylvandar should exert its influence abroad have begun to collide. No Guild quest has yet been drawn into the contest.

### [[The Future of Sanama]]
*Ongoing · Nation · Intrigue & Crime*

A growing divide is forming in Sanama between those who want permanent settlements, markets, schools and infrastructure and those who believe that settling for good would destroy what makes Sanama what it is. The nomads have kept their leadership through the Guild's own reckoning: late in 2025 a Guild company rescued the Sanama Elders from a vampire raiding force and brought them home. A Guild party has also hunted ankhegs beside the nomads, and the Guild has made friends among the Pride. There is no sign that the divide has been settled.

### [[The War of the Fanged Swamp]]
*Ongoing · Nation · Crises & Wars*

The struggle inside the Fanged Swamp is intensifying as competing powers fight for territory and influence. The swamp itself keeps changing: waters shift, old routes vanish and buried places reappear. In May a Guild company crossed it and found a beacon in a half-sunken temple, learning there how war, betrayal and older powers shaped the swamp's suffering. They were questioned by Veysha of the Mirevein Coven, an ancient hag whose coven has long influenced the swamp, and through her met the Driftbone Armada. The talks held, but which power will come out ahead is not known.

### [[The Woaden Wood]]
*Ongoing · Nation · Crises & Wars*

Sylvandar's ancient forests have become a strategic resource as other nations race to build airships. The elves have little interest in surrendering them simply because the rest of the world suddenly needs timber. The Woaden Rangers closed the region around Duskwillow entirely after a Woaden Tree fell there and the wood began to rot from the roots up, calling it a divine blight. A senator and others were carried off from that village, and no one knows where they went.

### [[Uncertainty in the Forged Sands]]
*Ongoing · Nation · Mysteries & Arcana*

The Steelborn face mounting tension with Lexovar and worsening shortages of the fragments they need to make new Steelborn. Unsettling mysteries run through their territory, among them the Black Silence, old sealed sites and unanswered questions about how a Steelborn is made. In the summer a Guild company escorted a Steelborn home to the Forged Sands, beating off attacks by the Obsidian Chain, the Golden Chain and the Cracked Scale on the road and the train. The Steelborn named the Guild Friends of Steel and promised that future missions would come. A Steelborn had earlier been found among prisoners in a hidden vault beneath Cogveil College.

### [[Unrest in Kastalshire]]
*Ongoing · Nation · Crises & Wars*

The Crown's increasingly centralized rail system and political control are provoking serious unrest among the duchies. Old tensions with Thundrakar and resistance to Contramara's growing authority are feeding it. No Guild quest has changed the picture since the summer.

### [[The Scar of Vaelor]]
*Ongoing · City · Mysteries & Arcana*

Foreign nations increasingly want permanent access to the Scar's resources and strategic position, and the Genasi keep resisting attempts to turn trade agreements into footholds. In the spring a company escorting two Kalashtar to the Skywake Festival reached the Scar just as an elemental crystal was stolen from Vaelor. The thieves proved to be the Kalashtar themselves, and the company turned them in. The crystal was put back, but it did not repair the damage: the floating islands kept destabilizing, and others began to show the same signs. No one yet understands what is stirring in the Scar's elemental currents.

### [[The Bastions of Solara and the Resonance Loom]]
*Quiet · Nation · Crises & Wars*

Resentment toward Solara's entrenched elite is growing among merchants, veterans and poorer citizens, and groups such as the Ashwings and the Sunbroken challenge the state's authority. Archbishop Boel's conspiracy to steal the materials of Starforged arms ended with his execution, but Lord Varos Neightreign and the elven woman Faelassa escaped, and Lieutenant Rahmiel has sworn that Varos will answer for it. The Night Lady's school in Auris Prime has been broken but not ended, since some of its pupils refused to leave. The Ashwing Cinder-Marshal Sariel Veyne, Rahmiel's oathbrother, had copied two of the three oath-seals for the Resonance Loom, a device meant to suppress the will of whole crowds, and about forty children vanished the night he began its last anchor. Airships are also beginning to undermine Solara's historic control of the mountain passes and overland trade.

### [[Clan Glimveil's Foothold]]
*Dormant · Nation · Crises & Wars*

Rurik Grimveil's Clan Glimveil, a small goliath clan of the far northern tundra that was once always one bad winter from extinction, now holds Castle Kelgor, said to lie in far eastern Kastalshire. Lord Elric Halister, its former ruler, was killed by members of the Guild who entered as traders in March, and the castle's plans and a hidden tunnel route were given to Rurik. The stronghold gives Thundrakar's goliaths a fortified foothold for raids into Kastalshire. The Crown is unlikely to tolerate the incursion for long, and a major Kastalshire response is expected, though none is yet known. The Guild's standing with Rurik, built over three missions, remains good.

### [[The Stone Accord]]
*Dormant · Nation · Crises & Wars*

The Guild went to Thumdar as envoys to the dwarves, and the journey cost Ranmir IronFist his life in a forge held by hostile forces. At his funeral Rhanak promised to see the forge restored, and a Guild party has since driven the salamanders from the lower works and cleared the temple beneath them. The dwarves of Thumdar think well of the Guild, and the Council has set it a task. It is not known whether the task was carried out, nor what lay behind the sealed boxes the company opened.

## Unresolved Threats & Mysteries

*Some dangers survived. Others are only beginning to reveal themselves.*

### [[The Dragons of the Roost]]
*Ongoing · World · Mysteries & Arcana*

Dragons have begun moving beyond the places where they are normally seen, and no one can say why. The Roost, a secluded settlement high in the northern mountain forests, keeps its own memory of such visitors: in 986 a pair of wyverns driven south from Thundrakar fell upon it. No Guild quest explains the movements.

### [[The Fragile Peace]]
*Ongoing · World · Crises & Wars*

The great wars are over, but Eryndor is not truly at peace. Old grudges, new weapons, unstable governments, criminal empires, territorial ambitions and rapidly changing technology press on the agreements that have kept another continental war from beginning. Members of the Guild have seen how little it takes. In one village a hired band dressed as Helm's soldiers helped a vampire cabal turn the faithful against tiefling refugees, and in the Thalenmark Empire an order of the Basilica of the Radiant Crown meant to cover a killing of tieflings by calling them bandits.

### [[The Old Blood]]
*Ongoing · World · Mysteries & Arcana*

Awakenings of the mysterious Old Blood are becoming more frequent throughout Eryndor. No one yet understands why. Those who awaken are said to carry a mark and a cost that cannot be avoided or lessened. No Guild quest bears on the cause.

### [[Lexovar's Hidden War]]
*Ongoing · Nation · Intrigue & Crime*

Lexovar's rival magical institutions keep up a quiet academic cold war of influence, espionage and sabotage. Members of the Guild have seen its edges: mercenaries working for Gearshield Academy went after a researcher and his warforged companion, and papers in a hidden room tied the academy to secret warforged research. The mercenary Redford has escaped the Guild twice. Cogveil College was found to hide stolen research, false records and duplicate constructs. Beneath all of it, organizations such as the Obsidian Chain may have penetrated institutions that believe themselves secure; the Chain has been found rooted in the Starforges' workforce and turning up at a wizard's ruined tower.

### [[The Stolen Memories of Elora]]
*Ongoing · Nation · Intrigue & Crime*

A vast collection of genealogies, hidden roads, treaties, testimony and cultural memory was deliberately stolen from the Herds of Elora. The collection has never been recovered. Some believe the thieves meant to erase something rather than to steal it. The Herds keep a sworn order, the Hearth Guard, to protect their Wise Ones and gathered histories, and it is not known to have found the thieves.

### [[Knell Escaped]]
*Ongoing · City · Mysteries & Arcana*

The sentient bell-construct called Knell has escaped the Arcanum. It was born when mixed magic at the Arcanum woke a bell to awareness, and in May wild magic surged through the academy from the bell tower. A Guild company stopped the immediate threat and saved the school, but Knell fled before it could be destroyed or contained. Its whereabouts are unknown, and it is said to seek vengeance on the students whose magic brought it to life.

## The Guild's Own Stories

*Debts, rescues, rivals and old wounds: the stories the Guild's members carry.*

### [[The Smiles Gang]]
*Active · City · Mysteries & Arcana*

The Smiles Gang, a band of criminals dressed as clowns, has troubled Ciliren for more than a year, with ransom traps in the mountains, victims lured from the streets, and fencing for sea elves, all under the eye of a servant of Norebo. Guild companies have broken their schemes more than once, yet the gang's leader in the floating isles escaped through a portal, in ruins marked with the sign of Cyric that offered a trial of rebirth. The strange girl Nesser, kept in the gang's bunker, is unexplained. The Guild holds records of the guards the gang bribed and means to send members back to deal with them.

### [[The Willmer Books]]
*Active · City · Mysteries & Arcana*

Allan Willmer, a librarian of Lakebarrow, used magic books to trap the Guild's members and to afflict others, and the Guild first met him through an offer of books for its library. He is dead. The hag Madam Frump, with whom he had made a bargain, killed him when he nearly gave away her secrets. What she wanted from him is unknown, and the cursed books he wrote may not all be accounted for.
