## Active

### [[The Dome Crisis]]
*World · Mysteries & Arcana*

The Guild Archive records that the Vivisectionist, Dr. Vathros, was killed in his castle by the assembled Guild forces, and the Guild believes him dead. His death did not end the Dome Crisis. Three domes still stand over Eryndor, at Oathend, at Greyspire and over the wreck of Castle Bran, and the one man known to understand how to unmake them took that knowledge with him. Lord Mask, Lady Mask and Lord Varos remain at large. Creatures bred in the domes are now being studied for use as defences.

### [[Polaris in Thundrakar]]
*Nation · Intrigue & Crime*

The Guild Archive records that Polaris is an arcane order the Guild has met in dungeons, on the tundra, at sea and across the gate to the Abyss, always at work on fragments, souls or fiends. Over a year the Guild has thwarted it again and again, and has earned the respect of several goliath clans in doing so. In October the Guild caught up with its remaining forces in Thundrakar's eastern mountains and kept them from the Stygian devil Stagor's animated remains. Polaris is still active, and its purpose remains unknown.

### [[The Smiles Gang]]
*City · Mysteries & Arcana*

The Guild Archive records that the Smiles Gang, a band of criminals dressed as clowns, has troubled Ciliren for more than a year, with ransom traps in the mountains, victims lured from the streets, and fencing for sea elves, all under the eye of a servant of Norebo. Guild companies have broken their schemes more than once, yet the gang's leader in the floating isles escaped through a portal, in ruins marked with the sign of Cyric that offered a trial of rebirth. The strange girl Nesser, kept in the gang's bunker, is unexplained. The Guild holds records of the guards the gang bribed and means to send members back to deal with them.

### [[The Willmer Books]]
*City · Mysteries & Arcana*

The Guild Archive records that Allan Willmer, a librarian of Lakebarrow, used magic books to trap the Guild's members and to afflict others, and that the Guild first met him through an offer of books for its library. He is dead. The hag Madam Frump, with whom he had made a bargain, killed him when he nearly gave away her secrets. What she wanted from him is unknown, and the cursed books he wrote may not all be accounted for.

## Quiet

- [[The Bastions of Solara and the Resonance Loom]] (Nation): The Guild investigated the Ashwing flight of Auris Prime, found Sariel Veyne completing the Resonance Loom beneath the Hall of the First Oath, and learned that some forty children had disappeared that night.
- [[The Corruption of Kalamathet]] (Nation): In August, Valdrin and his companions defeated Kalamathet again in a portal realm, though they judged his death unlikely to be permanent.
- [[The Fall of Talvereth]] (Nation): Perrin Grint learned that his late father, the councilor Marlowe Grint, had secretly run the Quiet Ledger, and for now he has sealed its archive and left the choice open.
- [[Old Marnie and the Stolen Children]] (City): At her cottage the company defeated the Arch Hag Marnie, but her daughters escaped and several orphanage children, Sasha among them, remain missing.
- [[The Wolves of Lakebarrow]] (City): In the woods the company defeated the Big Bad Wolf's lieutenant and freed Red and Conan, but the Wolf itself remained at large.
- [[The Dusk Queen's Shattered Soul]] (Faction): The shards of the Dusk Queen's soul were delivered to the Oakspire Enclave just ahead of the Queen's pursuit, and the Enclave demanded Synthia's return.

## Lingering threads

*Nothing has moved here in months. Last known state:*

- [[Clan Glimveil's Foothold]] (Nation): The Guild Archive records that Clan Glimveil, a small goliath clan of the far northern tundra, has gained a castle.
- [[The Eye of Kaos]] (Nation): The Guild Archive records that the Eye of Kaos, a vast and mysterious ship, remains out of reach.
- [[The Stone Accord]] (Nation): The Guild Archive records that the Guild went to Thumdar as envoys to the dwarves, and that the journey cost Ranmir IronFist his life in a forge held by hostile forces.
- [[Lord Olsino's Plague]] (City): The Guild Archive records that a deadly disease that sickened settlers and some Guild members traces back to Lord Olsino, who paid a den of vampires to perfect it while the false cleric Lita carried out experiments on settlers.
- [[The Thing Beneath Talvereth]] (City): The Guild Archive records that a spreading sickness of mutation and corrupted crystal has been traced, piece by piece, to the dark beneath Talvereth.
- [[Fog over Raethalor]] (Faction): The Guild Archive records that Raethalor lies under Sire Valkek's permanent garrison, and that Countess Vyra Nashen has been turned out of her manor.
- [[The Bloodpits of Borgnar]] (Faction): The Guild Archive records that a Guild company died in the Redmarch blood pits in 2025 and that the record of it ends with a vow: blood will be met with blood.
- [[The Rat and the Scale]] (Faction): The Guild Archive records that the Cracked Scale's hold on Rakkath has been broken, though a few of its outposts remain, and the Brotherhood of the Rat feels secure there for now.
- [[Kestrel and Silas Vane]] (Personal): The Guild Archive records that Silas Vane, found alive after his supposed murder, slipped away and has taken the name Marcellin Roth, and is believed to have gone to Talvereth.
