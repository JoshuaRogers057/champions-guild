# Tieflings - Flame of the Old Blood

The power of the Old Blood is a mysterious force limited to the highest echelon of powerful families and bloodlines. Each manifestation is paired with a significant drawback that cannot be avoided or reduced.

> Any player wishing to use an Old Blood trait must first receive approval through the **#approvals** channel on the Discord.

---

## Eligibility

Lineage tracing back to the infernal bloodlines of the ruling council of Infernal Lords of Ash-Korrath within three generations.

**Physical Marking:** A mark or tattoo around the eyes with a faint infernal glow.

---

## Level 7 Power

> Once per long rest, when you take damage from an enemy within 15 feet, you may use your reaction to force all creatures within 15 feet to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier) or become frightened for 1 minute, repeating the saving throw at the end of each of their turns.

---

## Drawback

> After using this feature, you gain one level of exhaustion.
