# Gnomes - Cunning of the Old Blood

The power of the Old Blood is a mysterious force limited to the highest echelon of powerful families and bloodlines. Each manifestation is paired with a significant drawback that cannot be avoided or reduced.

> Any player wishing to use an Old Blood trait must first receive approval through the **#approvals** channel on the Discord.

---

## Eligibility

Noble lineage within the highest echelon of powerful gnomish families.

**Physical Marking:** A mysterious mark or tattoo around the eyes, believed to grant superior magical insight.

---

## Level 7 Power

> Once per long rest, you can cast *Identify* or *Dispel Magic* as an action.

---

## Drawback

> After casting the spell, you take psychic damage equal to 3 × your proficiency bonus. This damage cannot be reduced or prevented.
