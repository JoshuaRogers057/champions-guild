# Halflings - Luck of the Old Blood

The power of the Old Blood is a mysterious force limited to the highest echelon of powerful families and bloodlines. Each manifestation is paired with a significant drawback that cannot be avoided or reduced.

> Any player wishing to use an Old Blood trait must first receive approval through the **#approvals** channel on the Discord.

---

## Eligibility

A marked ancestor within three generations.

**Physical Marking:** Coin-like circles around the eyes and temples.

---

## Level 7 Power

> Once per long rest, you can force an enemy to reroll an attack roll made against you.

---

## Drawback

> Your next attack roll, saving throw, or ability check is made with disadvantage.
