# Aasimar - Radiance of the Old Blood

The power of the Old Blood is a mysterious force limited to the highest echelon of powerful families and bloodlines. Each manifestation is paired with a significant drawback that cannot be avoided or reduced.

> Any player wishing to use an Old Blood trait must first receive approval through the **#approvals** channel on the Discord.

---

## Eligibility

High houses, lordly bloodlines, or military officers within three generations.

**Physical Marking:** A glowing symbol around the eyes.

---

## Level 7 Power

> Once per long rest, when you take damage from an enemy within 30 feet, you may use your reaction to emit a burst of radiant light. The attacker must make a Dexterity saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier) or take 3d8 radiant damage and be blinded until the start of their next turn.

---

## Drawback

> After using this ability, you are blinded until the start of your next turn.
