# Dragonborn - Old Blood of the Wyrm

The power of the Old Blood is a mysterious force limited to the highest echelon of powerful families and bloodlines. Each manifestation is paired with a significant drawback that cannot be avoided or reduced.

> Any player wishing to use an Old Blood trait must first receive approval through the **#approvals** channel on the Discord.

---

## Eligibility

Powerful dragonborn families; these bloodlines frequently produce Scale Bearers, generals, and military commanders.

**Physical Marking:** A mark or tattoo around the eyes.

---

## Level 7 Power

> Once per long rest, you may choose to deal maximum damage with your breath weapon instead of rolling.

---

## Drawback

> After using this ability, you take half of the damage you dealt. This damage ignores resistance and immunity and cannot be reduced or negated.
