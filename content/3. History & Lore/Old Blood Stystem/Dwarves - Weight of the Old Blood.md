# Dwarves - Weight of the Old Blood

The power of the Old Blood is a mysterious force limited to the highest echelon of powerful families and bloodlines. Each manifestation is paired with a significant drawback that cannot be avoided or reduced.

> Any player wishing to use an Old Blood trait must first receive approval through the **#approvals** channel on the Discord.

---

## Eligibility

Noble families or noble lineage within four generations.

**Physical Marking:** Ritual-like symbols around the eyes.

---

## Level 7 Power

> As a Reaction, you gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage until the end of your turn.

---

## Drawback

> On the following round, you gain vulnerability to those same damage types.
