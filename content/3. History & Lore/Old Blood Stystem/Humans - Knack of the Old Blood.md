# Humans - Knack of the Old Blood

The power of the Old Blood is a mysterious force limited to the highest echelon of powerful families and bloodlines. Each manifestation is paired with a significant drawback that cannot be avoided or reduced.

> Any player wishing to use an Old Blood trait must first receive approval through the **#approvals** channel on the Discord.

---

## Eligibility

Noble ancestry within five generations; immediately elevated to minor nobility upon manifestation.

**Physical Marking:** A mysterious mark or tattoo around the eyes.

---

## Level 7 Power

> Once per long rest, when you fail a saving throw, you may reroll it. You must use the new roll.

---

## Drawback

> After using this feature, you immediately gain one level of exhaustion.
