Azuth is the god of wizards and the mastery of arcane spellcraft — a deity concerned not with magic as a vast cosmic force, as Mystra is, but with the disciplined study and precise application of it. He is the patron of those who approach magic as a craft to be perfected through long hours of study, careful practice, and rigorous methodology. He is depicted as a thin, elderly wizard in robes, calm and precise in every movement.

Azuth serves almost as a lieutenant to Mystra, handling the mortal-facing aspects of magical governance while she tends to the Weave itself. He is not a flashy deity — he values patience, precision, and the quiet satisfaction of mastery over the thrill of raw power.

## Followers

Azuth is worshipped by wizards, spellcasters who favor disciplined study over innate ability, and scholars of magical theory. His followers are expected to study diligently, record their magical findings, teach those who are willing to learn, and never waste magical power through carelessness. His clergy tend to serve as instructors at arcane academies, magical librarians, and advisors to ruling powers on matters of arcane law. They tend to be methodical, patient, and somewhat pedantic — deeply proud of their mastery and occasionally dismissive of those who rely on innate magical ability rather than studied craft.

## Divine Relationships

Azuth serves **Mystra** closely and their relationship is one of deep mutual respect — she provides the Weave and the cosmic scope of magic while he tends to its mortal practitioners. He once imprisoned **Savras** within a staff, a controversial act that still colors their relationship. He is allied with **Oghma**, as magical knowledge and general knowledge are deeply intertwined. He opposes **Cyric** and **Shar**, whose deception and Shadow Weave corrupt the magical order he works to maintain.
